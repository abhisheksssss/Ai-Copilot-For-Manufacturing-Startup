## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Street vendors identified in survey and in possession of Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)
- Vendors identified in survey but not issued Certificate of Vending / Identity Card (Provisional Certificate generated via IT platform by ULBs)
- Street Vendors left out of ULB-led identification survey or started vending after survey completion (with Letter of Recommendation from ULB / TVC)
- Vendors from surrounding development/peri-urban/rural areas vending in ULB geographical limits (with Letter of Recommendation from ULB / TVC)
