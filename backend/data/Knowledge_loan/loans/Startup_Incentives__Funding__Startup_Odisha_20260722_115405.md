## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- The date of its incorporation / registration is not prior to 7 years
- The entity’s annual turnover does not exceed Rs.25 Crore, for any preceding financial year
- If not registered in Odisha, the entity employs at least 50% of its total qualified workforce in Odisha
- The entity is not an extension of the existing family business; or formed by splitting up or reconstruction of a business already in existence

---

## ⭐ Features
- Monthly allowance of Rs.20,000
- Product Development and Marketing/ Publicity assistance up to Rs. 15 lakh

---

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Startup Registration**: The registration by a startup entity for recognition can be done online by simply filling a Startup Registration Form

---

## 📊 Important Facts
- A startup applicant can reapply for startup recognition/ financial assistance one more time after a gap of 3 months from the date of communication of rejection

---

## 💵 Pricing & Fees
- Rs.20,000 (Rs.22,000/-, if the startup Founder (s)/Co-Founder (s) are Women or transgender or from Social Categories such as SC/ST/SEBC/PH)
- Rs. 5 Lakh to Rs. 1.00 Crore
- Rs. 50 thousand to Rs. 10 Lakh per month

---

## 📄 Documents Required
- Incorporation certificate of the entity
- Self-certification for Startup definition
- Self-Certification for Proof of innovation

---

## 🏛️ Government Schemes
- **Startup Odisha**: The startup entity will have to submit its application online in the Startup Registration Form
