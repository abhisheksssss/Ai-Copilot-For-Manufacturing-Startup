## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business vintage: Minimum 3 years in the current profession.
- Business income: Minimum business income of ₹30 lakhs.
- Age: Minimum 21 years at the time of loan application. Maximum 70 years at the end of loan tenure.
- Minimum income: ₹2.5 lakhs (individuals) or ₹3 lakhs cash profit (non-individuals) for the last 2 years.
- Residence/office stability: Owned property or 12 months rented stability (residential), 24 months office stability.
