## 📋 Summary
The Government of India through the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance has launched an Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (‘ECLGS’) 5.0 in May 2026. The scheme provides credit guarantee cover to lending institutions for credit facilities extended to eligible borrowers to tide over any short-term liquidity mismatches in view of the West Asia crisis.
