## ✅ Eligibility Criteria

## ⭐ Features
- Flexible Limit Options: Avail Term Loan, Cash Credit, or Overdraft facility up to ₹1 crore.
- First-Time Venture Support: Aimed at supporting greenfield (first-time) enterprises.
- Credit Guarantee Backing: Limits may be secured with collateral or covered under CGFSIL (guarantee issuance on hold by Ministry of MSME).

---

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Term Loan**: Long term loan for business expansion
- **Superbiz – Flexi Credit for Business**: Working capital basis combined cash flows and property of applicant and co-applicant
- **Insta OD**: Instant unsecured overdraft for Current Accounts

---

## 📊 Important Facts
- Designed specifically for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs starting new businesses.
- Non-individual entities must have 51% ownership by SC/ST or women.
- Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme (CGFSIL) guarantee issuance on hold by Ministry of MSME.
