## 📋 Summary
To facilitate access to affordable credit to informal micro-entrepreneurs/ micro-enterprises (IMEs) for their livelihood enterprise promotion. The scheme provides door step assistance with the help of Partner Institutions, who assist SIDBI in the credit processes including collection of application information, digital documentation, collections, etc. A faster credit delivery is ensured with the help of seamless digital integration between OPI and SIDBI and with the help of Prayaas App/ portal.

## 📊 Important Facts
- More than 2,94,000 beneficiaries have been assisted under the scheme (as of December 2023).
- The scheme is operational in 30 States/UTs.
- More than 85% of the beneficiaries assisted under the scheme are women entrepreneurs.
- More than 70% of the assisted beneficiaries belong to the socially backward sections of society.
