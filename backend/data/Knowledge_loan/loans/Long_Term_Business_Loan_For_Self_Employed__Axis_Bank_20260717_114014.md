## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business Vintage: Minimum 5 years (2 years for doctors)
- Business Turnover: Minimum Rs. 10 lakhs in the latest year (3 years for doctors)
- Age: Minimum 24 years at the time of loan application, Maximum 65 years at the end of loan tenure
- Minimum Income: ITR income Rs. 3 lakhs for 2 years each (2.50 lakhs for doctors)
- Residence / Office Stability: Either office or residential property is to be owned
