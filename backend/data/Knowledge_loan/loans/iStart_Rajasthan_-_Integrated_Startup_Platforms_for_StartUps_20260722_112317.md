## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Enterprise Age: Period of existence and operations should not be exceeding 10 years from the date of incorporation or as defined by DPIIT from time-to-time.
- Annual Turnover: Entity should have an annual turnover not exceeding Rs. 100 crore for any of the financial years since its Incorporation or as defined by DPIIT from time-to-time.
- Original Entity: Should not have been formed by splitting up or reconstructing an already existing business.
- Innovative & Scalable: Entity should work towards development or improvement of a product, process or service and/or have scalable business model with high potential for creation of wealth & employment.
- Registration: Entity should be registered in Rajasthan or has branch/sales office in Rajasthan.
