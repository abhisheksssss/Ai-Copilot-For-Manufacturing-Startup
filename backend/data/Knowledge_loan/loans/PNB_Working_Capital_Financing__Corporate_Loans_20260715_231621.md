## 📊 Important Facts
- Beneficiaries of BGs should get the BG confirmed from respective controlling offices and get the guarantees issued through SFMS (Structured Financial Messaging System) only.
- Bank Guarantees are transmitted through IFN 760/ 760 COV & amendments thereof are transmitted through IFN 767 COV of SFMS, being the only valid modes of transmission.
- IBA has advised that a separate advice of the BG be sent by the issuing bank to the advising bank through SFMS only after which the paper BG would become operative.
- Verification of the genuineness of BGs can be done by visiting the Bank’s website (https://pnbnet.org.in/OBGV/).
