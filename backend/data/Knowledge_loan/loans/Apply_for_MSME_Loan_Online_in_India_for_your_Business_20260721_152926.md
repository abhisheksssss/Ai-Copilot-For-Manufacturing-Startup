## 💵 Pricing & Fees
- Processing Fee: Upto 2% of the facility amount
- Proposal Login Fee (Non refundable): Rs. 5000/-
- Renewal / Review Fee for Existing cases: Upto 2% of the facility amount
- Commitment Charges in case quarterly average utilisation in CCOD account is < 60%: 0.5% p.a. on the difference between the actual utilisation and average utilisation of 60% for the quarter
- Take Over/ Pre-closure Charges (full pre-closure): Working capital -5% of the Sanctioned amount, Term Loan - 5% of principal outstanding
- Term Loan part pre-closure: Upto 5% on the principal amount prepaid
- Overline charges in OD/CC: Upto 24% p.a. of the limit overdrawn above drawing power from the date of Overline till the date of regularisation of limits
- Penal Charges for limit expired Temporary Overdrafts (TOD): Upto 24% p.a. on the utilised limit till the date of regularisation of limit
- Penal Charges for Term Loan: Upto 36% p.a. on overdue instalment due but not paid till overdue amount is regularised
- Incremental Interest on sanctioned Temporary Overdrafts (TOD) & Adhoc limit: Upto 2% p.a.
