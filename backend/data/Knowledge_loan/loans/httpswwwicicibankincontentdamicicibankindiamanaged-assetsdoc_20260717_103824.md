## 📋 Summary
The Government of India has introduced ECLGS 5.0 to provide additional credit facilities to eligible borrowers to address short-term liquidity gaps due to the West Asia crisis.

|-------|-------|
| **Main Topic** | Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) 5.0 |
| **Target Audience** | Eligible borrowers including MSMEs, non-MSMEs, and airlines with fund-based working capital limits from ICICI Bank as of March 31, 2026. |

## 💰 Loan Details

| Parameter | Value (as on website) |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Loan Name** | ECLGS 5.0 |
| **Loan Amount** | MSMEs and Non-MSMEs (except airlines): Up to 20% of peak fund-based working capital or ₹100 crore per borrower, whichever is lower. Airlines: ₹1,500 crore per borrower. |
| **Repayment Tenure** | MSMEs and Non-MSMEs (except airlines): 5 years including 1-year moratorium. Airlines: 7 years including 2-year moratorium. |
| **Interest Rate** | MSMEs: EBLR + 0.75%. Non-MSMEs: MCLR + 0.75% (capped at 9% p.a.). |
| **Processing Fee** | Nil |
| **Collateral / Security** | Charge on existing securities (primary and collateral) and assets created from the loan. |
| **Financing %** | MSMEs: 100% of default amount. Non-MSMEs and airlines: 90% of default amount. |
| **Moratorium Period** | MSMEs and Non-MSMEs: 1 year. Airlines: 2 years. |

---

## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business enterprises with fund-based working capital limits from ICICI Bank as of March 31, 2026.
- Scheduled airlines with outstanding credit facilities from ICICI Bank as of March 31, 2026.
- Credit facilities categorized as 'standard' (excluding SMA-2) as of March 31, 2026.
- Borrower's account not tagged as NPA with ICICI Bank or any lender at sanction/disbursement.
- Borrowers who availed CGSE are ineligible up to the CGSE limit already availed.

---

## ⭐ Features
- 100% credit guarantee for MSMEs on default amounts.
- 90% credit guarantee for non-MSMEs and airlines on default amounts.
- Nil guarantee and processing fees.
- Nil prepayment charges.
- Moratorium period for repayment.

---

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **ECLGS 5.0 Credit Facility**: Provides working capital loans to eligible businesses and airlines with government-backed credit guarantees.

---

## 📊 Important Facts
- Operating guidelines issued by NCGTC on May 08, 2026.
- Loan applications must be submitted through the JanSamarth portal.

---

## 💵 Pricing & Fees
- Interest rates: EBLR + 0.75% for MSMEs; MCLR + 0.75% (capped at 9% p.a.) for non-MSMEs.
- No processing or guarantee fees.
