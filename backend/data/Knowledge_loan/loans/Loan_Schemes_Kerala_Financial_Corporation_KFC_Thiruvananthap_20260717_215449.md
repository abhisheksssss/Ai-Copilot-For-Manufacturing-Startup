## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Term Loan For Industrial Activities**: For new and existing enterprises with moratorium up to 2 years
- **Working Capital Loans**: Upper limit up to Rs.5 crore with moratorium up to 12 months
- **Financing Construction Activities And Housing Projects**: For builders and developers with repayment period 6 years
- **Short Term Loan**: Upper limit up to Rs.5 crore with moratorium up to 6 months
- **Modernisation, Upgradation, Expansion and Diversification**: Loan up to 90% of expansion cost with moratorium up to 24 months
- **Contractor Loan**: Sanctioned as Line of Credit for five years with bill discounting
