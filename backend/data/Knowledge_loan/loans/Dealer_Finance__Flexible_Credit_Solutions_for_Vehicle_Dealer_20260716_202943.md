## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Inventory Funding**: Finance vehicle inventory using a revolving facility with 'pay as sold' repayment.
- **Cash Credit**: Working capital for non-vehicular purposes (spare parts, receivables).
- **Bank Guarantee**: Apply for bank guarantees mandated by vehicle manufacturers at competitive rates.
- **Letter of Credit**: Secure Letter of Credit services for global trade operations with compliance support.
- **Term Loan**: Financing for showrooms, workshops, and machinery with flexible repayment terms.
