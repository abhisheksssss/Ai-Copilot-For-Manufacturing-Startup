## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Fund Of Funds**: To strengthen Kerala’s startup investment ecosystem by enabling capital flow into professionally managed venture capital funds
- **Creative Economy Fund**: VC Fund focused on investing Creative Economy Startups
- **Ignite - Investor Education Program**: Enabling HNIs and Ultra HNIs to become confident angel investors through structured education, tools, and curated access.
- **Seeding Kerala**: Seeding Kerala is an annual investor meet bringing together angels, HNIs, and institutional investors.
- **VC 101: Bootcamp**: VC 101: Bootcamp helps founders understand how venture capital works, bridging investor insights and real fundraising experiences to navigate VC funding with clarity and confidence.
- **Pitch Day**: This initiative supports founders at the very beginning of the fundraising journey and serves as a stepping stone to larger investor interactions by helping them articulate traction, business model, and growth strategy more effectively.
- **Investor Cafe**: Connects investment-ready startups with active investors through curated one-on-one meetings that drive meaningful fundraising conversations.
- **Resources**: Equipping startups with practical tools, templates, and insights to successfully navigate fundraising.
