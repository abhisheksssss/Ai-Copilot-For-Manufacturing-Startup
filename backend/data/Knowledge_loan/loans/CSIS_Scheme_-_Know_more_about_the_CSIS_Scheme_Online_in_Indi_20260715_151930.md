## 📋 Summary
The Central Scheme for Interest Subsidy (CSIS) provides interest subsidy on education loans for students from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) with annual family income up to Rs. 4.50 lacs. The government covers interest during the moratorium period (course duration + 1 year) for recognized technical/professional courses in India.

## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Annual family income up to Rs. 4.50 lacs (certified by Income Certifying Authority)
- Pursuing recognized technical/professional course in India
- Eligible for subsidy only once (undergraduate or postgraduate degree/diploma)
- No subsidy if course discontinued midstream or expelled (except medical grounds)

---

## ⭐ Features
- Government bears interest during moratorium period (course duration + 1 year)
- Applicable for integrated courses (graduate + postgraduate)
- Scheme implemented through Canara Bank (Nodal Bank)
- Applicable for disbursements on or after 1st April 2009
