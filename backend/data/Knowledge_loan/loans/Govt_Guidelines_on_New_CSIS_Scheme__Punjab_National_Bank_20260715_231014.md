## 📋 Summary
The Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme (CSIS) aims to ensure students from low-income families (annual income up to Rs.4.5 lakh) can pursue higher education without financial barriers. Launched in 2009 and modified in 2022, it provides full interest subsidy during the moratorium period (course duration plus one year) for education loans under the Indian Banks’ Association (IBA) Model Education Loan Scheme for technical/professional courses in India.

## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Education Loans taken under IBA Model Education Loan Scheme
- Students having gross parental/family income up to Rs.4.5 lakh per annum
- Students enrolled in professional/technical courses from NAAC accredited Institutions or NBA accredited programmes or Institutions of National Importance or Central Funded Technical Institutions (CFTIs)

---

## ⭐ Features
- Full interest subsidy during the moratorium period (course duration plus one year)
- No collateral security or third party guarantee required for loans up to Rs.7.50 lakh
- Linked to the Indian Banks’ Association (IBA) Model Education Loan Scheme
- Interest subsidy admissible once for undergraduate, postgraduate, or integrated courses

## 📊 Important Facts
- Interest subsidy applies to a maximum loan amount of Rs.10.00 lakh (previously Rs.7.50 lakh for loans sanctioned between 01.04.2018 to 31.03.2022)
- Scheme modified with Union Cabinet approval on 19.01.2022
- Subsidy admissible only once for a single course
