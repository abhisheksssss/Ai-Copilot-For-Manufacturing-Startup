## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business Vintage: Minimum 3 years in current profession
- Business Income: As per ITR, it should not be loss making in last 1 year (1 year ITR & Computation of Income required)
- Age: Minimum 21 years at the time of loan application, Maximum 70 years at the end of loan tenure
- Residence / Office Stability: Borrower should own either the office or residential property; Residence stability should be of at least 12 months; Office stability should be of at least 24 months
