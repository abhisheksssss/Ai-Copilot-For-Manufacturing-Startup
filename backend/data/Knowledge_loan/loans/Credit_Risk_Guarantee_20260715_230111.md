## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Individuals with low income under EWS/LIG/MIG category
- Applicant(s) with annual income up to Rs. 9.00 lakhs
- Applicant(s) should be having no home or one Home, self-declaration to be obtained regarding same
- Minimum Age shall be 21 years and Maximum Age shall be 60 years
- Carpet Area for a house up to 60 sq mtr
