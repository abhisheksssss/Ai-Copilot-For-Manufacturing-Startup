## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business must be a specialty/super specialty hospital, nursing home, diagnostic center, pathology lab, specialty clinic, dialysis center, endoscopy center, IVF center, medical/paramedical educational institute, or large medical equipment dealer
- Residency/office occupancy for at least 3-5 years
- Minimum 3 years of experience in managing a healthcare business
- Demonstrated repayment history for any loan over the past 12 months
- Bank account transaction details for the last 12 months from a scheduled commercial bank
