## 📋 Summary
CGTMSE is a Government of India scheme launched in 2000 in collaboration with SIDBI, offering collateral-free loans to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs). It provides guarantees on loans availed from Member Lending Institutions (MLIs), with credit guarantee coverage up to ₹5 crore (revised from ₹2 crore in April 2023).

## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Both new and existing Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) engaged in manufacturing, trading, or service activities (excluding agriculture and self-help groups) are eligible.
- The borrowing business must demonstrate profitability, viability, and maintain a good financial track record, as assessed by the lending institution.
- Applicants must not have defaulted with any bank or financial institution.

## 💵 Pricing & Fees
- Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF) for small-ticket loans up to ₹10 lakh is intentionally kept low.
- Maximum AGF of 1.35% of the loan amount for larger loans between ₹2-5 crore.
