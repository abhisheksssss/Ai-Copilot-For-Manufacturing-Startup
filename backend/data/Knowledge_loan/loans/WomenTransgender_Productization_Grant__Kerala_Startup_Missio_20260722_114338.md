## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Startup must be in the Product Development stage
- Women/transgender founder(s) must hold minimum 51% shareholding
- Women/ Transgender Founder must be capable of effectively pitching her product and clearly presenting its key features and essential elements before the Expert Committee.
- Must hold a valid KSUM Unique ID
- Must be incorporated as Pvt Ltd / LLP / OPC with active MCA status
- Startups that have already received Scale-up Grant are not eligible
