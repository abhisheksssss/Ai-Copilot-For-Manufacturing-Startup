## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements specified under the scheme guidelines.
- Startup must be in the Product Development stage
- Must be incorporated as Pvt Ltd / LLP with active MCA status
- Must own an innovative technology product
- If Idea Grant was previously availed, only the balance eligible amount can be claimed.
- KSUM Unique ID is mandatory at the time of application filing
