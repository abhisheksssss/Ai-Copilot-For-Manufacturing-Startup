## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- All business entities engaged in manufacturing/services classified under MSME as per MSMED Act
- Valid GST Registration where applicable
- Minimum 30 active units in a cluster within 200-250 km geographical area
- Proper backward/forward integration/linkages in the cluster

---

## ⭐ Features
- Cluster-based schemes for pooled borrowers in common business activities
- Need-based finance assessed per business requirements
