## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Credit Guarantee Scheme**: The scheme aims to provide credit guarantees for the credit facilities extended to MSEs by the Member Lending Institutions (MLIs), without the requirement of collateral security or third-party guarantees.

---

## 📊 Important Facts
- CGTMSE has established itself as an important institution endeavoring to increase the flow of credit to MSEs segment without the hassle of collateral security or third-party guarantees.
- With effect from 01.04.2025, this scheme facilitates credit guarantees for credit support of upto Rs. 10 crore to MSEs, extended by the MLIs.

## 🏛️ Government Schemes
- **Credit Guarantee Scheme**: The Ministry of MSME implements the Credit Guarantee Scheme (CGS) for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) through the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE), which was jointly set up by the Ministry of MSME, Government of India, and SIDBI in the year 2000.
