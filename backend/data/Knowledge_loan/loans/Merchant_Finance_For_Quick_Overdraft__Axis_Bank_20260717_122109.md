## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Business vintage: Minimum 3 years in current profession
- Turnover: Minimum business income of ₹30 lakhs
- Age: Minimum 21 years of age at the time of loan application. Maximum 65 years at the end of loan tenure
- Residence / Office Stability: Residence stability should be of at least 12 months. The applicant should be operating from same city for the last 3 years. Either office or residence to be owned
- Minimum Income: In case of individuals, minimum income to be of ₹2.5 lakhs as per ITR for last 2 years. In case of non-individuals, minimum cash profit should be ₹3 lakhs for last 2 years
