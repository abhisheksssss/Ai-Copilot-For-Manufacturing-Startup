## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Scheduled Banks are eligible for financial support by SIDBI under various refinance schemes.

---

## ⭐ Features
- Refinance Assistance from SIDBI to eligible Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), subject to meeting the prescribed norms.
- Mission: To create a national network of strong, viable and sustainable Microfinance Institutions (MFIs) for providing microfinance services to the economically disadvantaged people, especially women.

---

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Refinance to Banks**: Financial support to Scheduled Banks under various refinance schemes.
- **Assistance to NBFCs**: Refinance Assistance to Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) registered with RBI as 'ICC – Investment and Credit companies'.
- **Micro Lending Development**: Capacity Building Grant, Transformation Loan, and Portfolio Risk Fund for Microfinance Institutions (MFIs).
