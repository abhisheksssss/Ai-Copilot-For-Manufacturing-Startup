## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Startup must be in the early revenue stage
- Must own an innovative technology-based product
- If Idea/ Productization Grant was previously availed, only the remaining eligible amount can be claimed
- Must hold a valid KSUM Unique ID
- Must be incorporated as Pvt Ltd / LLP / OPC with active MCA status
