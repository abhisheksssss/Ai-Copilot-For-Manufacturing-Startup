## ⭐ Features
- Facilitates credit to MSMEs by having an integrated mix of various public and private sector banks.
- Provides an option for MSMEs to apply for a new bank or switch over of an account to a different bank as given under tie up arrangements.
- Helps the MSMEs in completing documentation as required by the bank and after submitting it to the bank NSIC officer does the follow up with the bank.
- To facilitate larger number of MSMEs who are desirous of availing credit.
- To provide handholding support to MSMEs without any cost to MSMEs.

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Bank Credit Facilitation Scheme**: Facilitates credit support to MSMEs (fund based or non-fund based limits) from various Nationalized and Private Sector Banks.
