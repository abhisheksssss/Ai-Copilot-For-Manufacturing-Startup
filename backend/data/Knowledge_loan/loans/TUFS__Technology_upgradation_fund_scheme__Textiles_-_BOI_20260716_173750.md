## 📋 Summary
The Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS) provides credit linked Capital Investment Subsidy (CIS) to promote ease of doing business, generate employment, and promote exports through 'Make in India' with 'Zero effect and Zero defect' in manufacturing.

## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Every individual entity will be eligible for one time capital subsidy only on the eligible investment as per the rates and the overall subsidy cap.

## ⭐ Features
- Promote ease of doing business
- Generate employment and promote exports
- Implement 'Make in India' with 'Zero effect and Zero defect'
- Provide one-time capital subsidy for technology upgradation
- Indirectly promote investment in textile machinery manufacturing

## 🛍️ Products & Services
- **Stand Up India**: Bank loans between 10 Lakh and 1 Crore to SC or ST or Women Borrower
- **NULM**: National Urban Livelihoods Mission
- **Star Weaver Mudra Scheme**: The Handloom Scheme aims at providing adequate and timely assistance from the Bank to the weavers to meet their credit requirement i.e. for investment needs as well as for working capital in a flexible and cost effective manner. The Scheme will be implemented both in rural and urban areas.
- **PM Svanidhi**: For all the Street Vendors engaged in vending in urban area
- **CLUSTER BASED LENDING**: 
- **ECLGS5.0 Scheme**: Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) 5.0 Designed to help eligible MSME & Non-MSME borrowers manage short-term liquidity requirements with flexible repayment and government-backed assurance.
- **PM VISHWAKARMA**: Collateral free ‘Enterprise Development Loans’ to artisans and craftspeople upto Rs.3 lakh in two tranches, at a concessional rate of interest fixed at 5%, with Government of India subvention to the extent of 8%.
- **PMMY/Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana**: For setting up of new/upgrading existing Micro business enterprises in the manufacturing, processing, trading and service sector and carrying out activities allied to agriculture, financing to weavers and artisans (income generating activity).

## 📊 Important Facts
- ATUFS will be implemented from 13.01.2016 to 31.03.2022
- Projects for technology upgradation covered by a prescribed limit of term loan sanctioned by the lending agencies will only be eligible for grant of benefit under ATUFS
