## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Any individual above 18 years of age
- No income ceiling for assistance
- VIII standard pass education required for projects above ₹10.00 Lakh (manufacturing) and ₹5.00 Lakh (business/service sector)
- Assistance available only for new projects sanctioned under PMEGP
- Existing units or units that have availed Government subsidy under other schemes are not eligible
