## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements specified under the scheme guidelines.
- Startup must have generated ₹10 lakhs revenue from the product in the last 6 months, OR raised not less than ₹30 lakhs through external equity investment
- Must own an innovative technology-based product
- Startups that availed old Scale-up Grant (₹12 lakhs) are eligible only for ₹3 lakhs top-up
- Must hold a valid KSUM Unique ID
- Must be incorporated as Pvt Ltd / LLP / OPC with active MCA status
- If Idea/ Productization/ Market Acceleration Grant was previously availed, only the remaining eligible amount can be claimed

---

## ⭐ Features
- The scheme offers financial assistance of up to ₹15 lakhs to enable startups to expand into new markets, strengthen branding and marketing efforts, enhance product features, and accelerate customer acquisition and revenue growth.
