## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- Applicant age should be 18- 75 years
- If the applicant is above 60 years, then Legal Heir Declaration is required
- Minimum land holding requirement of 0.5 acre of irrigated land for KPC Crop Loan & 1 acre of irrigated land for KPC Poultry & KPC Pisciculture
- An individual must run his business for a minimum of 2 years with profits to be eligible for KPC Poultry & 3-5 years with profits to be eligible for KPC Pisciculture
