## 📊 Important Facts
- Analyse the exact amount of loan cover you require for your business to avoid over-borrowing.
- Check your credit score and take steps to improve it, as this loan is provided without any collateral.
- Review the interest rate and tenure to determine your repayment capacity.
- Consider that the interest rate charged may be higher than rates for loans against collateral.
- A lender will always check your debt-to-income ratio to ascertain your repayment ability for an unsecured loan. The lower the ratio, the better.
- Check the repayment, prepayment, and foreclosure terms and charges.
