## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- All new units as well as existing units which go for industrial expansion located anywhere in North Eastern Region, which commence commercial production within 10 years period from the date of notification of NEIIPP, 2007

---

## ⭐ Features
- Capital Investment Subsidy @ 30% of the value of plant and machinery
- Interest Subsidy @ 3% on working capital loan
- 100% reimbursement of insurance premium

## 📊 Important Facts
- Rs.3635.45 crore has been released to the states of NER till 31st July, 2023
- Rs.109.97 crore had been released to the states of NER during the Financial Year 2022-23
- Rs.200.00 crore in BE under NEIIPP, 2007 for the current FY 2023-24

## 💵 Pricing & Fees
- Rs.1.50 Crore limit of automatic approval of subsidy
- Rs.1.50 Crore to Rs.30 Crore with the approval of Empowered Committee
- Above Rs.30 Crore with the approval of the Union Cabinet

## 🏛️ Government Schemes
- **Central Capital Investment Subsidy Scheme**: Provides for Capital Investment Subsidy @ 30% of the value of plant and machinery

- **Central Comprehensive Insurance Subsidy Scheme**: Provides for 100% reimbursement of insurance premium
