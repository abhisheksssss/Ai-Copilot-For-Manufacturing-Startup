## ✅ Eligibility Criteria
- The Unit must mandatorily have Udyam & GST Registration
- The Unit must be profit making for the last three years
- The Unit‘s Sales must be over Rs 150.00 Lakhs in last 3 years for Wholesalers
- The Unit‘s Sales must be over Rs 100.00 Lakhs in last 3 years for Retailers
- The Unit is purchasing goods from atleast two registered manufacturers who should be registered on GST
