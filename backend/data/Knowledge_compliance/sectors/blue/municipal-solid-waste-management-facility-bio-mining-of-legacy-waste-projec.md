# Overview
**Municipal Solid Waste Management Facility - Bio-mining of legacy waste projects** is classified under **`Annexure-II-B` (Essential Environmental Services (Domestic Waste - Blue Category))**, Serial No. **`1.3`**, overseen by the **`UPC-II`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Blue Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **93.0**.



# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `93.0` (Category Range: `Essential Environmental Services (PI +2 Yrs CTO Validity)`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 90/100` | Severity: `High`):** Sub-scores `W1=35` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=30` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=25` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 60/100` | Severity: `High`):** Sub-scores `A1=35` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=25` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `H1=0` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=0` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **CGWA / State Ground Water Authority NOC (if extracting groundwater, with electronic flowmeter)**
4. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
5. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Full-Fledged ETP with Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) / Secondary & Tertiary Treatment & OCEMS | Mandatory High-Efficiency Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD - ESP/Bag Filter/Wet Scrubber) with stack monitoring & OCEMS
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** As per Pollution Index / Common Facility norms (+2 years CTO validity incentive)
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Blue*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
