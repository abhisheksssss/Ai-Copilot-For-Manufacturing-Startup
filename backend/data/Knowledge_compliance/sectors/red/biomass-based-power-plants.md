# Overview
**Biomass-based power plants** is classified under **`Annexure-I` (Industrial Sectors)**, Serial No. **`114.3`**, overseen by the **`IPC-II`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Red Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **88.1**.



# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `88.1` (Category Range: `80-100`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 45/100` | Severity: `Medium`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=15` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=30` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 75/100` | Severity: `High`):** Sub-scores `A1=25` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=25` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=25` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 60/100` | Severity: `High`):** Sub-scores `H1=10` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=50` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **Hazardous Waste Authorization (Rule 6) under Hazardous and Other Wastes (M&TBM) Rules, 2016**
4. **CGWA / State Ground Water Authority NOC (if extracting groundwater, with electronic flowmeter)**
5. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
6. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) / Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) to meet Environment (Protection) Rules standards | Mandatory High-Efficiency Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD - ESP/Bag Filter/Wet Scrubber) with stack monitoring & OCEMS | Dedicated Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Storage & Authorized TSDF/CBWTF/Recycler Disposal
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 6 months (Once every 3 months for 17-category & Common Facilities)
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Red*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
