# Overview
**Industries Engaged In Recycling/Reprocessing/ Recovery/Reuse Of Hazardous Waste (Items As Per Schedule Iv Of H&Ow( M & Tbm) Rules, 2016.) - Industries engaged in recycling / reprocessing/ recovery/reuse of Hazardous Waste, excluding lead, paint, and ink sludge** is classified under **`Annexure-I` (Industrial Sectors)**, Serial No. **`151.5`**, overseen by the **`WM-II`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Orange Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **75**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** This includes items namely - Brass Dross, Copper Dross, Copper Oxide Mill Scale, Copper everts, Cake & Residues, Waste Copper and copper alloys in dispersible form, Slags from copper processing for further processing or refining, Insulated Copper Wire, Scrap/copper with PVC sheathing including ISRI-code material namely “Druid” Jelly filled Copper cables, Zinc Dross-Hot dip Galvanizers SLAB,, Zinc Dross- Bottom Dross, Zinc ash/Skimming arising from galvanizing and die casting operations, Zinc ash/Skimming/other zinc bearing wastes arising from smelting and refining,, Zinc ash and residues including zinc alloy residues in dispersible form.

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `75` (Category Range: `55-79.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 45/100` | Severity: `Medium`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=30` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=15` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 60/100` | Severity: `High`):** Sub-scores `A1=35` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=0` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=25` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 30/100` | Severity: `Low`):** Sub-scores `H1=20` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=10` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **Hazardous Waste Authorization (Rule 6) under Hazardous and Other Wastes (M&TBM) Rules, 2016**
4. **CGWA / State Ground Water Authority NOC (if extracting groundwater, with electronic flowmeter)**
5. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
6. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) / Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) to meet Environment (Protection) Rules standards | Mandatory High-Efficiency Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD - ESP/Bag Filter/Wet Scrubber) with stack monitoring & OCEMS | Dedicated Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Storage & Authorized TSDF/CBWTF/Recycler Disposal
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 1 year
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Orange*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
