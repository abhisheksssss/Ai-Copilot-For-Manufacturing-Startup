# Overview
**Ship breaking industries** is classified under **`Annexure-I` (Industrial Sectors)**, Serial No. **`155`**, overseen by the **`WM-III`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Orange Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **57.5**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** Ship breaking releases a large number of pollutants, including toxic waste, used/waste oil, poly- chlorinated biphenyls, and heavy metals.

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `57.5` (Category Range: `55-79.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=0` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=0` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 30/100` | Severity: `Low`):** Sub-scores `A1=0` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=30` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 50/100` | Severity: `Medium`):** Sub-scores `H1=30` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=20` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **Hazardous Waste Authorization (Rule 6) under Hazardous and Other Wastes (M&TBM) Rules, 2016**
4. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
5. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD) / Fugitive Dust & Fume Extraction System | Dedicated Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Storage & Authorized TSDF/CBWTF/Recycler Disposal
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 1 year
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Orange*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
