# Overview
**Hydrogen production through electrolysis (using renewable energy) on BOO/BOOT/BOT, mode etc., located in the premises of end user industry and directly using de-mineralized water & other utilities (cooling tower, ETP, etc.) sourced from end user industry** is classified under **`Annexure-I` (Industrial Sectors)**, Serial No. **`68.7`**, overseen by the **`IPC-I`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **White Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **10.0**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** i. DM water as feed water for electrolyser and cooling/chilling water requirement to be met by the end user industry. ii. Wastewater and other waste generated during O&M shall also be managed by the end user industry.

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `10.0` (Category Range: `0-24.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=0` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=0` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `A1=0` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=0` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 10/100` | Severity: `Low`):** Sub-scores `H1=0` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=10` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Written Intimation / Self-Declaration Undertaking to State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) / PCC (Exempt from CTE & CTO)**
2. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH, if applicable)**
3. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services, if applicable)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** None (Practically Non-Polluting; no trade effluent or process emission treatment required)
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Exempt from routine Consent inspection (Intimation to SPCB/PCC only)
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `White*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
