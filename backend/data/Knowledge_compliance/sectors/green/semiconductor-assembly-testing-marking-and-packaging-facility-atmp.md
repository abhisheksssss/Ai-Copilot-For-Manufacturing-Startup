# Overview
**Semiconductor Assembly, Testing, Marking and Packaging Facility (ATMP)** is classified under **`Annexure-I` (Industrial Sectors)**, Serial No. **`163.4`**, overseen by the **`WM-III`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Green Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **43.1**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** (HF), Mixed Nitric HF (HF + HNO3), Phosphoric acid, Sul phuric acid (H2SO4), Hydrogen Peroxide, Isopropyl alcohol (IPA) / Methanol (Methanol Only), Stripper EKC-265 /ACT N396 (ACT N396 Only), BHF – 63 U, Choline etchant, etc. ii. The air pollutants which are being emitted during the manufacturing process are SiH4, PH3, B2H 6, HF, HBr, DCS, NF3, SF6, BCl3, Cl2, HCL, NH3, C2F6, CHF3, CF4, C4F8, C2F6 etc. iii. Process waste, used oil etc. are generated as hazardous waste.)

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `43.1` (Category Range: `25-54.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=0` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=0` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 25/100` | Severity: `Low`):** Sub-scores `A1=0` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=25` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 35/100` | Severity: `Medium`):** Sub-scores `H1=25` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=10` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **Hazardous Waste Authorization (Rule 6) under Hazardous and Other Wastes (M&TBM) Rules, 2016**
4. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
5. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD) / Fugitive Dust & Fume Extraction System | Dedicated Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Storage & Authorized TSDF/CBWTF/Recycler Disposal
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 2 years
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Green*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
