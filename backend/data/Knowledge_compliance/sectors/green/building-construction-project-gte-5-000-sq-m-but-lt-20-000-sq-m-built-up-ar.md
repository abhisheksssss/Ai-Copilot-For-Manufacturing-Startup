# Overview
**Building construction project ≥ 5,000 sq. m., but < 20,000 sq. m. built-up area (without connectivity to terminal STP)** is classified under **`Annexure-III` (Service / Infrastructure Development Sectors)**, Serial No. **`10.2`**, overseen by the **`UPC-I`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Green Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **40.0**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** ( i. During the construction phase, the sector is mainly air polluting. However, in post construction phase it is mainly water pol luting due to generation of sewage. Consent to Establish/Operate to be taken as per EC conditions, as applicable. ii. Building construction project ≥ 5,000 sq. m., but < 20,000 sq. m. built -up area (with connectivity to terminal STP) may not require separate classification. iii. For projects < 5000 the wastewater shall be managed according to on -site sanitation methods as mentioned in the Manual on Sewerage and Sewage Treatment System (2013), published by the Central Public Health and Environmental Engineering Organisation (CPHEEO ), and as amended from time to time.)

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `40.0` (Category Range: `25-54.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 40/100` | Severity: `Medium`):** Sub-scores `W1=20` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=0` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=20` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `A1=0` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=0` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `H1=0` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=0` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **CGWA / State Ground Water Authority NOC (if extracting groundwater, with electronic flowmeter)**
4. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
5. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Mandatory Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) / Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) to meet Environment (Protection) Rules standards
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 2 years
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Green*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
