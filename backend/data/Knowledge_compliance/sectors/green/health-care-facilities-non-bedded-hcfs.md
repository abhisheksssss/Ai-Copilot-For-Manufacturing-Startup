# Overview
**Health Care Facilities - Non-bedded HCFs** is classified under **`Annexure-III` (Service / Infrastructure Development Sectors)**, Serial No. **`3.8`**, overseen by the **`WM-I`** division of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Under the CPCB *Classification of Sectors into Red, Orange, Green, White and Blue Categories (January 2025)* framework, this sector is categorized as **Green Category** with a Cumulative Pollution Index (PI) score of **25.0**.

**Sector Specific Notes & Conditions:** (HCFS) (AS DEFINED UNDER BIO-MEDICAL WASTE MANAGEMENT RULES, 2016) (Sectors generates bio-medical waste. As per methodology scores assigned to H.)

# Pollution Profile & Severity Score
- **Cumulative Pollution Index (PI):** `25.0` (Category Range: `25-54.9`)
- **Water Pollution (`PIw = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `W1=0` (Oxygen Demand), `W2=0` (Toxic/Other Pollutants), `W3=0` (Wastewater/Sewage Volume).
- **Air Pollution (`PIa = 0/100` | Severity: `None`):** Sub-scores `A1=0` (Point Source Process Emissions), `A2=0` (Fugitive Emissions / Odour), `A3=0` (Fuel Type & Quantum).
- **Waste Generation (`PIh = 25/100` | Severity: `Low`):** Sub-scores `H1=0` (Disposal Method / Potency), `H2=0` (Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Quantum).

# Statutory Approvals & Clearances Required
1. **Consent to Establish (CTE) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) under Water Act, 1974 & Air Act, 1981**
3. **Bio-Medical Waste Authorization under Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016**
4. **Factory License (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health - DISH)**
5. **Fire Safety NOC (State Fire and Rescue Services)**

# Mandatory Compliance & Mitigation Guidelines
- **Treatment & Control Systems:** Dedicated Hazardous / Bio-Medical Waste Storage & Authorized TSDF/CBWTF/Recycler Disposal
- **Surveillance & Inspection Frequency:** Once every 2 years
- **Progressive Environmental Management (Star Rating Incentive):** Units demonstrating 100% wastewater treatment & reuse, 100% dependency on cleaner gaseous fuels (PNG/CNG/LPG/CBG/Electricity), and verifiable pollution reduction over 1 year of compliant operation are eligible for `Green*` star upgradation with extended CTO validity and reduced inspection frequency under Chapter 4 of CPCB 2025 Guidelines.
