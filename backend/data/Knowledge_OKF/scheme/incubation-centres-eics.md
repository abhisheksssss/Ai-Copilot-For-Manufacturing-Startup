# Overview
Incubation Centres (EICs) aim to support established incubators in scaling their startup support capabilities through AIM/related incubation support.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include existing incubators and institutions that are currently supporting startups.

# Benefits
The scheme provides up to ₹10 crore grant, with a requirement for matching private contributions. This funding is aimed at enhancing the capacity of incubators and indirectly supporting startups.

# Funding
Funding is provided in the form of grants and capacity support to incubators, facilitating indirect startup support through enhanced capabilities of the incubation centers.
