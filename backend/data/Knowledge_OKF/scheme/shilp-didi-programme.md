# Overview
The Shilp Didi Programme is a government initiative launched in 2024 to economically empower women artisans, known as "Shilp Didis," by providing them with training, digital skills, and market access through e-commerce platforms and physical exhibitions.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants are women artisans from 72 districts across 23 states, covering 30 diverse handicrafts such as textiles, pottery, metal crafts, embroidery, etc.

# Benefits
Participants will benefit from:
- E-training modules on entrepreneurship, regulatory compliance, social media, and e-commerce.
- Marketing opportunities through Dilli Haat, craft fairs, and curated events.
- Integration into e-commerce platforms for nationwide and global visibility.
- Inclusion in the National Handicrafts Development Programme (NHDP) clusters.

# Funding
The programme is implemented by the Ministry of Textiles, through the Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), with a focus on making women artisans financially independent, improving their design and business skills, and helping them leverage modern marketing and entrepreneurship tools.
