# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (PMJAY) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched in 2018, subsuming the Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) and Senior Citizen Health Insurance Scheme (SCHIS). It aims to provide health insurance coverage to the bottom 40% of India's population based on the latest Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) data.

# Eligibility
The scheme targets beneficiaries identified by the latest SECC data, specifically covering the bottom 40% of the Indian population. Senior citizens aged 70 and above are also eligible under recent expansions.

# Benefits
- Offers a sum insured of Rs.5 lakh per family for secondary care and tertiary care hospitalisation.
- Covers the cost of hospitalization, pre-hospitalization, medication, and post-hospitalization during the treatment of tertiary and secondary care procedures.
- Provides an Ayushman Card worth Rs 5 lakh that can be used to avail free treatment at more than 27,000 empanelled hospitals.
- Requires every hospital to provide Pradhan Mantri Arogya Mitras (PMAMs) to assist beneficiaries.

# Funding
The scheme is centrally sponsored and implemented in collaboration with state governments through the National Health Authority (NHA) and State Health Agency (SHA).
