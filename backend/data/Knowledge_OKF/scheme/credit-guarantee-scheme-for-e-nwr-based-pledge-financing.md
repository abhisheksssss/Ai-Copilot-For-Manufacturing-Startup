# Overview
Credit Guarantee Scheme for E-NWR Based Pledge Financing is a scheme under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare to help farmers access post-harvest loans by leveraging electronic warehouse receipts (e-NWRs).

# Eligibility
- **Institutions:** All scheduled banks and all cooperative banks.
- **Borrowers:** Small and marginal farmers, women, SC/ST/PwD farmers, other farmers, MSMEs, traders, FPOs, and farmer cooperatives.

# Benefits
- Increase post-harvest lending: Currently, post-harvest lending stands at Rs. 40,000 crore out of total agricultural lending of Rs 21 lakh crore; lending against e-NWRs is just Rs 4000 crore.
- Improved trust for banks: It addresses the default arising from both credit risk and warehouseman risk.
- Access to vulnerable sections: The scheme focuses on small and marginal farmers, women, SC/ST, and PwD farmers with minimal guarantee fees.
- Avoid distress selling by farmers: Farmers can submit their produce and get issued e-NWRs which will act as collateral for banks to issue required loans. They can sell their produce when the price goes up.

# Funding
- Total Corpus: Rs. 1000 Crores
- Coverage:
  - Loans up to Rs. 75 lakhs for agricultural purposes.
  - Up to Rs. 200 Lakhs for non-agricultural purposes.
  - Guarantee coverage:
    - 85% for loans up to Rs. 3 Lakhs.
    - 80% for loans between Rs. 3 to 75 lakhs for small and marginal farmers, women, SC/ST/PwD.
    - 75% for other borrowers.
