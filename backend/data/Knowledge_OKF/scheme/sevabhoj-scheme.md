# Overview
The Scheme for Promotion of Culture of Science (SPOCS) aims to set up Science Cities and Science Centres in all the States of the country.

# Eligibility
Interested states have to provide land and share the cost of setting up of facility and corpus for upkeep and maintenance.

# Benefits
The scheme provides support for establishing Science Cities and Science Centres, fostering scientific education and public engagement with science.

# Funding
Not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is implied that financial assistance will be provided by the Central Government to states willing to participate.
