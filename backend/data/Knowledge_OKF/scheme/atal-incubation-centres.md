# Overview
The Atal Incubation Centres (AICs) scheme is part of the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) programme, which aims to support world-class incubators across universities, institutions, and private hosts.

# Eligibility
Host institutions/incubators and startups can benefit from the scheme through incubation services.

# Benefits
The scheme provides grant-in-aid to incubators, along with mentoring and networking opportunities for startups.

# Funding
Up to ₹10 crore per incubator is provided, subject to the guidelines of the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM).
