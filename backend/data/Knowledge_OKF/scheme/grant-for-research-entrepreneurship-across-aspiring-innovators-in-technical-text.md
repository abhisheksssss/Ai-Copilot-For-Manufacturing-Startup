# Overview
The GREAT scheme provides startup grants to individual innovators and startups in technical textiles who are associated with approved incubators, under the National Technical Textiles Mission.

# Eligibility
Eligible applicants include:
- Individual innovators
- Startups in technical textiles

These entities must be associated with approved incubators.

# Benefits
The GREAT scheme offers grants of up to ₹50 lakh for a period of up to 18 months. The grant is provided as grant-in-aid (no royalty/equity) and includes incubator support.

# Funding
Funding is available in the form of:
- Up to ₹50 lakh per project
- For a duration of up to 18 months

The funding does not involve any royalty or equity stake.
