# Overview
Mission Shakti is an umbrella scheme aimed at ensuring the holistic development of women in India through a unified citizen-centric lifecycle support system. It covers various components such as One Stop Centres, Women’s Helpline (181), Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, Kanya Shiksha Pravesh Utsav, Nari Adalats, National Hub for Women Empowerment, Shakti Sadan, Shakti Niwas, PM Matru Vandana Yojana, and Palna.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria vary depending on the specific component of Mission Shakti. Generally, women affected by violence or in need of support are eligible to access services provided through One Stop Centres, Women’s Helpline (181), Nari Adalats, and other schemes. The Beti Bachao Beti Padhao scheme targets families with girls under the age of 6.

# Benefits
Mission Shakti offers a range of benefits including:
- Emergency response services through One Stop Centres.
- Support and assistance to women affected by violence via Women’s Helpline (181).
- Awareness drives for bringing back out-of-school adolescent girls to formal education or skilling systems under Kanya Shiksha Pravesh Utsav.
- Alternative dispute resolution and gender justice facilitation through Nari Adalats.
- Rehabilitation support for women in difficult circumstances via Shakti Sadan and Shakti Niwas.
- Maternity benefits provided by PM Matru Vandana Yojana.
- Day care facilities for children of working mothers under Palna.

# Funding
Funding is provided through a combination of central government initiatives, with specific components like Beti Bachao Beti Padhao being centrally sponsored schemes. The exact funding mechanisms and allocation details are managed by the Ministry of Women & Child Development.
