# Overview
ONDC is an open, interoperable digital network that enables buying and selling of goods and services across platforms using open protocols, without platform monopolies. It was launched in April 2022 by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is aimed at democratizing digital commerce, particularly benefiting Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

# Benefits
- Democratization of digital commerce by breaking platform silos.
- Creation of a level playing field for sellers, buyers, and service providers.
- Promotion of inclusivity, competition, innovation, and cost efficiency in e-commerce.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. ONDC functions as a decentralized digital network that connects independent platforms through common digital rules using open protocols and standard APIs.
