# Overview
Launched in the Budget of 2023-24 as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme with a corpus of Rs 24,104 crores, PM-JANMAN focuses on bringing essential government services to PVTG communities living in remote villages.

# Eligibility
Eligible are PVTG (Scheduled Tribes) communities residing in remote villages across India.

# Benefits
The scheme aims to provide basic government services such as power, water, road connectivity, housing, education, and healthcare. Key features include:
- Implementation of 11 initiatives through 9 Ministries.
- Complete coverage for schemes like PM-Gram Sadak Yojana, PM-Gram Awaas Yojana, Jal Jeevan Mission, Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojna, Sickle Cell Disease Elimination, TB Elimination, 100% immunization, PM Surakshit Matritva Yojana, PM Matru Vandana Yojana, PM Poshan, and PM Jan Dhan Yojana.
- Establishment of Eklavya Model Residential Schools in blocks with 50% or more tribals.
- Construction of 2,500 Anganwadi centres, setting up 1,000 mobile medical units (10 per district), installation of mobile towers in 3,000 villages, and establishment of 500 Van Dhan Vikas Kendras for forest produce sales.
- Setting up Ayush Wellness Centres through mobile medical units by the Ministry of Ayush.
- Facilitating skill and vocational training by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.

# Funding
The scheme has a corpus of Rs 24,104 crores.
