# Overview
Component of Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY), ABHA is a 14-digit account used to link all health records of a person, facilitating cashless transactions and managing financial aspects related to healthcare services under the Ayushman Bharat scheme.

# Eligibility
Any individual can enrol in Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) to generate an ABHA Health ID free of cost. Users can use their Aadhaar number along with basic information like name, birthday, gender, and address or use a driver's license or mobile number if they do not have an Aadhaar card.

# Benefits
- Reduces out-of-pocket expenses for beneficiaries.
- Enhances access to quality healthcare by facilitating cashless transactions and electronic health records.
- Minimizes administrative hassles and delays, allowing healthcare providers to focus on patient care.
- Helps in addressing healthcare challenges effectively and improving the overall quality of care through data generation.

# Funding
ABHA manages the allocation and disbursement of funds for healthcare services availed by beneficiaries. It ensures that funds are transferred to healthcare providers promptly and securely.
