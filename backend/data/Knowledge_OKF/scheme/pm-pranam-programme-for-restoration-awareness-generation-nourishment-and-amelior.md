# Overview
The PM-PRANAM Programme for Restoration, Awareness Generation, Nourishment, and Amelioration of Mother Earth aims to encourage the balanced use of fertilizers in conjunction with bio-fertilizers and organic fertilizers.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is likely that farmers, panchayats, farmer producer organizations, and self-help groups involved in reducing fertilizer usage and awareness generation may be eligible for certain benefits under this scheme.

# Benefits
- Grants will be passed on to states that save money from reduced chemical fertilizer subsidies.
- 70% of the grant can be used for asset creation related to technological adoption of alternate fertilizers and production units at village, block, and district levels.
- The remaining 30% can be used for rewarding and encouraging farmers, panchayats, farmer producer organizations, and self-help groups involved in reducing fertilizer use and awareness generation.

# Funding
The scheme will have no separate budget and will be financed through the "savings of existing fertiliser subsidy" under schemes run by the Department of Fertilizers. Around 50% of these savings will be passed on as a grant to the state that saves the money.
