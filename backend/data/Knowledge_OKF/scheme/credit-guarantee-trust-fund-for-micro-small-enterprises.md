# Overview
The Credit Guarantee Trust Fund for Micro & Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) is a credit support guarantee scheme that enables collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises through member lending institutions.

# Eligibility
Micro and small enterprises, including startup MSMEs, borrowing from eligible banks/NBFCs/FIs are eligible for this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a guarantee coverage ceiling of up to ₹10 crore, with a varying guarantee percentage, allowing for easier access to debt without collateral, and providing a credit guarantee cover for the lender.

# Funding
The guarantee coverage ceiling is up to ₹10 crore, with a varying guarantee percentage.
