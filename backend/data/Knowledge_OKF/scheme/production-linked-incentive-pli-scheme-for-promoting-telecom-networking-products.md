# Overview
The Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for promoting telecom and networking products aims to boost domestic manufacturing, attract investments in the target segments of telecom and networking products, and encourage "Make in India." It also seeks to enhance exports of these products.

# Eligibility
Eligibility is subject to qualification criteria based on Global Manufacturing Revenues (GMR):
- **Global Companies:** GMR should be more than Rs. 10,000 crore in the base year.
- **Domestic Companies:** GMR should be more than Rs. 250 crore in the base year.
- **MSMEs:** GMR should be more than Rs. 10 crore in the base year.

# Benefits
Applicable incentives range from 7% to 4% over a period of five years, with support provided for investments made over four years.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Government and industry, including contributions from the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF).
