# Overview
NIDHI is an acceleration mechanism supported by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) to help incubated startups rapidly validate markets and scale.

# Eligibility
Technology startups selected through supported incubators/accelerators are eligible for this scheme.

# Benefits
Startups can benefit from market readiness, structured acceleration, mentoring, investor connects, and demo days.

# Funding
Up to ₹40 lakh is available (₹30 lakh to accelerators; ₹10 lakh to startups).
