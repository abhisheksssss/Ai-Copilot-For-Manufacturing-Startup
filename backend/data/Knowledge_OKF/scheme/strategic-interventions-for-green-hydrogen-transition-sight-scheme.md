# Overview
The Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition (SIGHT) Scheme is a flagship financial component under the National Green Hydrogen Mission, aimed at catalyzing India’s transition to green hydrogen.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria include meeting notified “Green Hydrogen” standards and participating in competitive reverse auctions conducted by SECI and Oil PSUs. Only hydrogen that meets these standards is eligible for incentives.

# Benefits
The scheme offers financial incentives to promote the adoption of green hydrogen, with rates decreasing over three years: ₹50/kg (Year 1), ₹40/kg (Year 2), and ₹30/kg (Year 3). It also supports infrastructure development through aggregated demand bidding processes.

# Funding
An outlay of ₹17,490 crore is allocated under SIGHT from a total mission budget of ₹19,744 crore. This funding is aimed at providing incentives and supporting infrastructure for green hydrogen production and use in India.
