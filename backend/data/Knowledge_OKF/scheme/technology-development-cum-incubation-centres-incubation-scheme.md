# Overview
The Technology Development-cum-Incubation Centres Incubation Scheme aims to incubate DAE spin-off technologies into market-ready products for startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs.

# Eligibility
Startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs are eligible to license/incubate DAE technologies under this scheme.

# Benefits
Benefits include technology transfer, lab access, scientist mentoring, and incubation support.

# Funding
There is no standard grant; support is provided based on incubation/technology access.
