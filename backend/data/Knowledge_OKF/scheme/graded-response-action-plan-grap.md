# Overview
The Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) is a statutory framework established to combat deteriorating air quality in the National Capital Region, particularly Delhi-NCR. It provides predefined actions based on the severity of pollution and was first introduced in 2017 under the directives of the Supreme Court of India and the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. GRAP was later revised by the CAQM in December 2024 to include predictive action based on IMD and IITM forecasts.

# Eligibility
GRAP applies to the National Capital Region, specifically Delhi-NCR, where air quality monitoring is conducted and measures are implemented.

# Benefits
The plan aims to create a graded and preemptive response system for air quality management by identifying specific interventions to be taken as the AQI worsens. Key benefits include dynamic implementation based on real-time data, cumulative approach ensuring progressive tightening of restrictions, inter-agency coordination among various authorities, and predictive enforcement to promote preventive rather than reactive air quality management.
