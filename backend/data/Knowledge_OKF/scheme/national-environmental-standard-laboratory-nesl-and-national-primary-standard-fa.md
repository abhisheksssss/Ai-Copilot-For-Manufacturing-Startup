# Overview

India has inaugurated the world’s second National Environmental Standard Laboratory (NESL) and the world’s fifth National Primary Standard Facility for Solar Cell Calibration at CSIR–NPL, New Delhi.

# Eligibility

Eligibility criteria are not specified in the provided text. However, these facilities support domestic manufacturing, startups, MSMEs, and regulators.

# Benefits

- Establishes India-specific standards for air pollution monitoring instruments.
- Supports effective implementation of pollution control policies such as National Clean Air Programme (NCAP).
- Ensures traceable, accurate, and standardised environmental data.
- Supports domestic manufacturing, startups, MSMEs, and regulators.
