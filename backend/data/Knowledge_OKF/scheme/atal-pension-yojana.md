# Overview
ATAL PENSION YOJANA is a scheme administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) to create a universal social security system for all Indians, particularly focusing on the poor, underprivileged, and workers in the unorganised sector.

# Eligibility
- All Indians aged between 18 and 40 years.
- Must not be an income tax payer.
- Must have a bank account and not be a member of any statutory social security scheme.
- In case of death before reaching 60 years, the spouse is entitled to receive the pension.

# Benefits
Subscribers will receive a guaranteed minimum monthly pension ranging from Rs. 1000 to Rs. 5000 at the age of 60 years based on their age-specific contributions.
