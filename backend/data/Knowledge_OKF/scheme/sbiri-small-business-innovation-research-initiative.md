# Overview
SBIRI (Small Business Innovation Research Initiative) is a public-private partnership scheme initiated by BIRAC to support the development of biotech products, particularly for early and late-stage biotech startups, SMEs, and industry-led consortia.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include:
- Biotech startups
- Small and medium enterprises (SMEs)
- Industry-led consortia

These entities must be undertaking innovative R&D projects.

# Benefits
SBIRI offers various forms of support to eligible applicants, including:
- Early-stage grant support commonly up to ₹50 lakh.
- Larger grants as per specific calls from the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) / BIRAC.
- Additional support in the form of R&D Grant-in-aid, soft loans, or cost-sharing arrangements.

# Funding
SBIRI provides financial assistance through:
- Grants: Up to ₹50 lakh for early-stage projects.
- Soft Loans and Cost-Sharing Support: Depending on specific project requirements as determined by DBT / BIRAC.
