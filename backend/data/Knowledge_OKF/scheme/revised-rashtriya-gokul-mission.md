# Overview
The Revised Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM) is a central sector scheme with an additional outlay of ₹1,000 crore, bringing the total allocation to ₹3,400 crore from FY22-26. The mission aims at developing and conserving indigenous bovine breeds while enhancing milk production.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the scheme are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text but typically include farmers and dairy cooperatives involved in the breeding and rearing of indigenous cattle.

# Benefits
The key benefits under RGM include:
- One-time assistance of 35% of capital cost for setting up 30 housing facilities for 15,000 heifers.
- 3% interest subvention on loans taken by farmers to purchase High Genetic Merit (HGM) IVF heifers from milk unions/financial institutions.

# Funding
The scheme is funded with 100% grant by the Central Government, except in few cases where partial funding might be required. The implementing agencies are National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) and State Livestock Development Boards.
