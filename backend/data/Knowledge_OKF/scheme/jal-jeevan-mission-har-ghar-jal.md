# Overview
JAL JEEVAN MISSION: HAR GHAR JAL is a centrally-sponsored scheme launched in 2019 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, subsuming the National Rural Drinking Water Programme (NRDWP). The mission aims to ensure every rural household has access to potable water at 55 litres per capita per day through tap water connections by 2024.

# Eligibility
The scheme is applicable to all rural households in India. Implementation lies with State/UT Governments, as drinking water is a state subject.

# Benefits
- Provision of adequate and quality drinking water on a regular and long-term basis.
- Improvement in the living standards of rural communities.
- Ensuring functionality of existing water supply systems and water connections.
- Water quality monitoring and testing.
- Sustainable agriculture through combined utilisation of conserved water, augmentation of sources, and improvement of supply systems.
- Rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, and household wastewater management for reuse.
- Creation of local infrastructure for source sustainability measures.

# Funding
The scheme is centrally-sponsored but implemented by State/UT Governments. The mission includes initiatives like Nal Jal Mitra to equip villagers with skills for minor repairs and maintenance, and a dashboard for real-time updates and transparency in water resource management.
