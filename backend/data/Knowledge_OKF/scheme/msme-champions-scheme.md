# Overview
The MSME Champions Scheme is a competitiveness umbrella scheme including ZED, Lean, and Innovative components to improve competitiveness of MSMEs, including startup MSMEs seeking quality, design, IP, lean or digital support.

# Eligibility
MSMEs, including startup MSMEs, seeking quality, design, IP, lean or digital support are eligible for the scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides quality, lean, innovation, and digital support to MSMEs, including subsidy, reimbursement, incubation, and advisory support.

# Funding
The funding for the scheme varies under ZED, Lean, and Innovative guidelines, and includes subsidy, reimbursement, incubation, and advisory support.
