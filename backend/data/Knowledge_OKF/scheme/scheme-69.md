### Definitions & Glossary

- **DPIIT-Recognised Startup**: A startup officially recognised by the Department for Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade (DPIIT).
- **Fund of Funds (FoF)**: Invests in other venture funds.
- **AIF (Alternative Investment Fund)**: SEBI-registered private investment fund that pools capital from investors and deploys into startups/companies.
- **Credit Guarantee**: Government-backed promise to repay a lender if the borrower defaults.
- **Grant**: Non-repayable financial assistance provided by the government.
- **Equity Investment**: Capital provided in exchange for ownership stake (shares) in the startup.
- **Convertible Debt**: A loan that can convert into equity at a later stage.
- **Proof of Concept (PoC)**: Early demonstration that an idea or technology works, typically in a lab or controlled setting.
- **TRL (Technology Readiness Level)**: Scale measuring how mature a technology is.
- **TBI (Technology Business Incubator)**: Institution providing physical space, mentoring, seed funding and networks to early-stage technology startups.
- **Incubator / Accelerator**: Organizations that support startups at early stages or fast-track growth of already-validated startups.
- **EMD (Earnest Money Deposit)**: Security deposit required in government tenders. DPIIT-recognised startups are exempt from EMD on GeM.

### Key Takeaways
1. **Access to Funding**: Various funds and schemes provide financial support through grants, equity investments, and credit guarantees.
2. **Support Services**: Incubators and accelerators offer mentorship, networking opportunities, and physical space.
3. **Regulatory Sandboxes**: These allow startups to test their products or services in a controlled environment with regulatory flexibility.
