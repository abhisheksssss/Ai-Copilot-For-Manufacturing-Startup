### National Program for Bovine Breeding (NPBB)

- **Objective**: To improve the genetic quality of indigenous cattle breeds.
- **Implementation**: MAITRI (Multi-purpose AI Technician in Rural India) will be established to deliver breeding inputs at the doorstep.
  
### National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)

- **Objective**: To provide technical and financial assistance for dairy development, creating infrastructure related to processing, production, marketing, and procurement by milk federations/unions while extending their activities through training facilities for farmers.
- **Implementing Agency**: National Dairy Development Board.

### Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)

- **Objective**: To improve the genetic makeup of indigenous breeds and increase their stock. 
- **Implementation**: Upgradation of nondescript cattle using elite indigenous breeds like Gir, Sahiwal, Rathi, Deoni, Tharparkar, Red Sindhi, etc.
- **Grant Basis**: 100% grant-in-aid.

### Gopal Ratna Awards

- **Objective**: To recognize farmers maintaining the best herd of Indigenous Breed(s) and practicing best management practices.

### Kamdhenu Awards

- **Objective**: To recognize institutions/trusts/NGOs/Gaushalas or breeders’ societies managing the best indigenous herds.
  
### Gokul Gram

- **Objective**: To promote indigenous cattle rearing and conservation in a scientific manner, propagate high genetic merit bulls of indigenous breeds, optimize modern farm management practices, and utilize animal waste economically (cow dung/urine).
- **Implementation**: Integrated cattle development centers.

### National Dairy Plan-I

- **Objective**: To increase productivity of milch animals and thereby increase milk production.
- **Implementing Agency**: National Dairy Development Board.
- **Focus States**: 18 major milk-producing states, accounting for over 90% of the country’s milk production.
- **Components**:
  - Productivity enhancement
  - Village-based milk procurement systems

### Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme (DEDS)

- **Objective**: To promote setting up of modern dairy farms and bring structural changes in the unorganized sector, upgrade quality and traditional technology to handle milk on a commercial scale, generate self-employment.
- **Implementing Agency**: NABARD as nodal agency.
- **Features**:
  - Restructured from Dairy Venture Capital Fund (DVCF) scheme in 2010
  - Central Sector Scheme

### Blue Revolution: Integrated Development and Management of Fisheries

- **Objective**: To increase overall fish production, modernize fisheries with new technologies, ensure food and nutritional security, generate employment and export earnings, and empower fishermen and aquaculture farmers.
- **Implementing Agency**: Centrally Sponsored Scheme
- **Features**:
  - National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB)
  - National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen
  - Strengthening database & geographical information system
  - Infrastructure development for marine fisheries, hatcheries, and ponds

### Quality Milk Programme

- **Objective**: To achieve global standards for domestic consumption of milk and ensure traceability.
- **Implementation**:
  - Phase I (2019-20): Equip 231 dairy plants with FTIR technology-based analyzers.

### National Livestock Mission (NLM)

- **Objective**: Sustainable development of the livestock sector, improving availability of quality feed and fodder.
- **Implementing Agency**: Centrally Sponsored Scheme
- **Features**:
  - Submissions: Livestock Development, Pig Development in North-Eastern Region, Fodder and Feed Development, Skill Development, Technology Transfer and Extension

### National Action Plan for Control and Eradication of Glanders

- **Objective**: To control and eradicate the contagious and fatal disease caused by Burkholderia mallei.
- **Implementation**:
  - Immediate elimination of infected animals
  - Transport to appropriate areas for destruction in closed vehicles
  - Strict zoo-sanitary measures during culling and disposal of carcasses

These schemes cover a wide range of initiatives aimed at improving the dairy, fisheries, and livestock sectors. Each scheme has specific objectives, implementing agencies, and components designed to address various challenges and opportunities within these industries.
