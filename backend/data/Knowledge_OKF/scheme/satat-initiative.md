# Overview
The SATAT (SUSTAINABLE ALTERNATIVE TOWARDS AFFORDABLE TRANSPORTATION) Initiative was launched in 2018 with the objective of promoting Compressed Biogas (CBG) production and encouraging its usage as an alternative clean fuel for transportation, thereby reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is implied that entities involved in CBG production and utilization, including private sector participants, may be eligible.

# Benefits
The initiative aims to achieve several benefits:
- Targets 5,000 CBG plants by 2025.
- Expected production capacity of 15 million metric tonnes per annum (MTPA).
- Utilization of various biomass sources such as agricultural residue, municipal solid waste, cattle dung, sewage sludge, and industrial organic waste.

# Funding
The initiative involves Viability Gap Funding (VGF) for bioenergy projects. Additionally, private sector participation is encouraged for establishing CBG plants.
