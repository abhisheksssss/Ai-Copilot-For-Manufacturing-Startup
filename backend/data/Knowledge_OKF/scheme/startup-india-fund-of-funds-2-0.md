# Overview
Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0 is a scaling follow-on fund-of-funds aimed at deepening domestic venture capital for deep tech, manufacturing, and growth-stage startups.

# Eligibility
Startups are supported indirectly through SEBI-registered AIFs under the new window.

# Benefits
The scheme provides equity investment via AIFs, with a corpus of ₹10,000 crore, and startup-level investment varies.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the DPIIT, with an equity investment approach.
