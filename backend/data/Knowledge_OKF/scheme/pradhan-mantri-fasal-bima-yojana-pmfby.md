# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY) was launched in 2016, making it the third largest insurance scheme globally in terms of premium and shields farmers from crop loss or damage arising from unforeseen events. It replaced the existing National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (NAIS) as well as the Modified NAIS.

# Eligibility
The scheme covers loanee farmers (those who have taken a loan), non-loanee farmers (on a voluntary basis), tenant farmers, and sharecroppers. Every state has notified crops for the Rabi and Kharif seasons with varying premium rates.

# Benefits
- Insurance coverage and financial support to farmers in case of crop failure due to natural calamities, pests & diseases.
- Stabilisation of farmer income to ensure their continuance in farming.
- Encouragement of innovative and modern agricultural practices.
- Ensuring flow of credit to the agriculture sector.

# Funding
- Uniform premium of 2% for Kharif crops and 1.5% for Rabi crops, with a 5% premium for annual commercial and horticultural crops.
- The difference between the premium rate and insurance charges payable by farmers is treated as Rate of Normal Premium Subsidy, shared equally by the Centre and State.
- No upper limit on Government subsidy; even if the balance premium is 90%, it will be borne by the Government.
