# Overview
The PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN URJA SURAKSHA EVAM UTTHAN MAHABHIYAN SCHEME FOR FARMERS (PM KUSUM) was launched in 2019 with the aim of providing farmers with access to cutting-edge technology for irrigation through solar pumps, thereby reducing dependency on diesel-driven pumps and enhancing their income.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to individual farmers, groups of farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Panchayats, cooperatives, and Water User Associations.

# Benefits
Farmers will receive a 60% subsidy for setting up tube wells and pump sets. Additionally, they can avail a loan covering 30% of the total cost from the government. The solar pumps offer more effective and eco-friendly irrigation compared to diesel-driven pumps. Farmers can also sell excess power generated by these pumps directly to the government, thereby enhancing their income.

# Funding
The scheme provides subsidies and loans for setting up tube wells and pump sets. Farmers receive a 60% subsidy on the total cost and an additional 30% as a loan from the government.
