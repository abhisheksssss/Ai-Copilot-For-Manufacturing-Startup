# Overview
The Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme (SVEP) is a sub-scheme of DAY-NRLM that aims to support rural entrepreneurs in setting up local enterprises. It provides community enterprise support to help rural poor create sustainable livelihoods.

# Eligibility
The scheme is relevant for rural entrepreneurs from the SHG/NRLM ecosystem, particularly those interested in starting or scaling up rural startups/micro-enterprises.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a range of benefits, including training, mentoring, community enterprise fund, and business support to help rural entrepreneurs establish and grow their enterprises.

# Funding
The scheme offers grant support, with varying amounts by block and enterprise plan, up to a maximum of ₹6.5 crore per block overall.
