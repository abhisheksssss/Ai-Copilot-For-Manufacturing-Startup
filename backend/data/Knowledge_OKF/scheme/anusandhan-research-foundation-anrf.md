# Overview
The ANUSANDHAN RESEARCH FOUNDATION (ANRF) aims to provide a high-level strategic direction for research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in natural sciences, with the goal of making India a global science and research player.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is implied that researchers, institutions, and private sector entities may be eligible based on the objectives stated.

# Benefits
The ANRF will offer several benefits:
- Equitable funding and democratisation of resources.
- Promotion of interdisciplinary research.
- Encouragement of private sector involvement in research and academics.

# Funding
Funding mechanisms include:
- Anusandhan National Research Foundation Fund for salaries, allowances, and administrative purposes.
- Innovation Fund to support outstanding creativity in the areas supported by the Foundation.
- Science and Engineering Research Fund for continuation of projects initiated under the Science and Engineering Research Board Act, 2008.
- Special purpose funds for specific projects or research.
