# Overview
The "Make In India" Initiative is based on four pillars: New Processes, New Infrastructure, New Sectors, and New Mindset. It aims to enhance export competitiveness by addressing sectoral and cross-cutting issues, promoting innovation, boosting services exports, skill training, and employment creation.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include Central and State Agencies, including Export Promotion Councils, Commodities Boards, SEZ Authorities, and Apex Trade Bodies recognized under the EXIM policy of the Government of India. Startups and service providers are also eligible for certain schemes.

# Benefits
- Increased manufacturing sector growth to 12-14% per annum.
- Boost in the share of manufacturing in the country’s Gross Domestic Production from 16% to 25% by 2022.
- Creation of 100 million additional jobs by 2022.

# Funding
Government funding is provided in the form of grant-in-aid, normally not more than the equity being put in by the implementing agency or 50% of the total equity in the project (subject to a ceiling of Rs 20 Cr for each infrastructure project). For projects located in North Eastern States and Himalayan States including J&K, up to 80% of the total equity can be funded.
