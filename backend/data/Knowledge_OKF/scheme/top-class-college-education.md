# Overview
The Top Class College Education scheme aims to recognize and promote quality education among students from Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and DNT categories by providing full financial support.

# Eligibility
Students who secure admission in the notified institutions are eligible for this scholarship. The scheme specifically targets students belonging to OBC, EBC, and DNT categories.

# Benefits
- Full tuition fee coverage
- Living expenses for the beneficiary
- Books and stationery provided
- A latest computer/laptop of a reputed brand will be provided during the course

# Funding
The scheme provides full financial support to meet the requirements mentioned above. The exact amount or funding mechanism is not specified in this text.
