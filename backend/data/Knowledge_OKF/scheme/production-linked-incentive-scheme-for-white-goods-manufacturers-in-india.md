# Overview
The Production Linked Incentive Scheme for White Goods Manufacturers in India aims to boost domestic manufacturing and attract large investments in the white goods manufacturing value chain, remove sectoral disabilities, create economies of scale, enhance exports, generate a robust component ecosystem, and employment. The scheme will be implemented by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade over FY 2021-22 to FY 2028-29 with a budgetary outlay of Rs. 6,238 crore.

# Eligibility
Incentive under the Scheme shall be provided to Companies making brown field or green field investments for manufacturing in target segments in India. Eligibility is subject to pre-qualification criteria and thresholds of cumulative incremental investment and incremental sales (net of taxes) over the base year for the respective year. The base year is FY 2019-20.

# Benefits
An incentive of 4% to 6% on incremental sales (net of taxes) over the base year will be provided to eligible companies for a period of five years subsequent to the base year and one year of gestation period.

# Funding
The scheme is fund-limited, with total payouts capped at the amount approved by Cabinet. The Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS), chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, will monitor the PLI scheme.
