# Overview
The scheme involves the sharing of data from e-Shram, a digital platform for construction workers, with Building and Other Construction Workers’ (BOCW) Welfare Boards to ensure that registered workers are properly registered with these boards and can access relevant welfare schemes.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals include construction workers who register on the e-Shram platform.

# Benefits
Construction workers benefit from being registered with BOCW Welfare Boards, which allows them access to various welfare schemes designed for them.
