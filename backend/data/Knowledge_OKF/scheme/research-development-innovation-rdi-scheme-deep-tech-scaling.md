# Overview
The Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme Deep-Tech Scaling is a long-term low-cost capital financing framework provided by the Department of Science & Technology (DST). It aims to support startups, companies, and innovation-led enterprises in strategic/deep-tech sectors.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include startups, companies, and innovation-led enterprises operating in strategic/deep-tech sectors.

# Benefits
The scheme offers long-term R&D finance through various forms of support including loans and equity-like investments. The exact level of project-level support varies depending on the specific needs and projects.

# Funding
The scheme has a corpus of ₹1 lakh crore, which will be utilized to provide long-term financing, loans, or equity-like support through designated channels.
