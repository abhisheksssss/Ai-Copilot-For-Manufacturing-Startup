# Overview
The Strategic Climate Fund (SCF) is designed to pilot and scale innovative programs aimed at enhancing climate resilience. These initiatives include the Pilot Program for Climate Resilience (PPCR), Forestry Investment Program, and Smart Cities Program.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text but typically such schemes target regions or projects that align with climate resilience goals.

# Benefits
The SCF offers a range of benefits through its innovative programs. These include enhanced climate resilience, improved infrastructure, and sustainable development practices.

# Funding
SCF uses a blended finance model to combine concessional funds from the Climate Investment Funds (CIF) with investments from Multilateral Development Banks (MDBs) and private sectors. This approach aims to reduce risks and attract commercial capital.
