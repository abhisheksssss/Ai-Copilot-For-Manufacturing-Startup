# Overview
The Government has officially recognised the Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) as the Recognised Security Organisation (RSO) for all major and minor seaports, empowering it to regulate, oversee, and enforce security standards across India’s maritime facilities.

# Eligibility
All Indian seaports are eligible under this scheme, including both major and minor ports.

# Benefits
- Uniform, professional security architecture for 250+ seaports.
- Standardised assessments, modern screening systems, and integrated security plans.
- Specialised paramilitary force with nationwide presence, expertise, and training to handle increasing threats such as smuggling, sabotage, and infiltration.
