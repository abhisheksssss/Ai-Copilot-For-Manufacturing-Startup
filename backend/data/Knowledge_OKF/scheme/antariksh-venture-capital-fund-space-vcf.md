# Overview
The Antariksh Venture Capital Fund (Space VCF) is a dedicated venture capital fund for Indian space-tech enterprises across the private space value chain, focusing on early and growth-stage Indian space-tech companies/startups.

# Eligibility
The fund is targeted towards early and growth-stage Indian space-tech companies/startups.

# Benefits
The fund provides equity/venture capital investment to space-tech companies, with a company ticket that varies.

# Funding
The target corpus of the fund is approximately ₹1,600 crore, with the first close being ₹1,005 crore.
