# Overview
The Department of Science & Technology (DST) is implementing the STI Hubs for SC/ST Communities Incubation / Livelihood Innovation Programme to establish technology-led livelihood and enterprise development hubs in SC/ST communities. This initiative aims to support Institutions/NGOs and community enterprises serving these communities through project-based grants.

# Eligibility
Institutions/NGOs and community enterprises that serve SC/ST communities are eligible for this programme.

# Benefits
Participants will receive project-based grants, technology deployment, training, and enterprise support.

# Funding
The funding is provided in the form of project-based grants with varying amounts sanctioned per STI hub. The DST is responsible for the financial management and disbursement of these funds.
