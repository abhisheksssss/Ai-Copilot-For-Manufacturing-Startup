# Overview
The Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS) is a scheme initiated by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, through Science and Technology Promotion Trust India (STPI), to support software product startups in selected Tier-II/III locations. The focus areas include incubation, mentoring, cloud credits, seed support, and market access.

# Eligibility
Tech/software product startups located in identified NGIS cities are eligible for this scheme.

# Benefits
Startups will receive support through various means including:
- Incubation
- Mentoring
- Cloud Credits
- Seed Support
- Market Access

The outlay for the scheme is ₹95.03 crore, with varying levels of support per startup and city.

# Funding
The total outlay for the Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS) is ₹95.03 crore. The level of funding varies based on the needs of each startup and the specific city involved in the scheme.
