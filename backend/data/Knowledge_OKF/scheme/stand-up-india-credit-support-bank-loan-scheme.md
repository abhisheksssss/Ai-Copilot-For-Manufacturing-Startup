# Overview
The Stand Up India Credit Support Bank Loan Scheme is designed to support greenfield enterprises that are promoted by Scheduled Castes (SC)/Scheduled Tribes (ST) and/or women entrepreneurs.

# Eligibility
Eligible applicants must be SC/ST borrowers or women borrowers, with at least one borrower per bank branch for greenfield non-farm enterprises.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a loan credit support of up to ₹1 crore. The minimum loan amount is ₹10 lakh.

# Funding
Funding is provided by the Department of Financial Services under the Ministry of Finance.
