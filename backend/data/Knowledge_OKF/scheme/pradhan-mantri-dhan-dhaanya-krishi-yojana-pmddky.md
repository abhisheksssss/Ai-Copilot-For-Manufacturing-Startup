# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI DHAN-DHAANYA KRISHI YOJANA (PMDDKY) is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Civil Aviation to provide financial assistance to farmers for the cultivation of oilseeds and pulses.

# Eligibility
Eligible farmers who are cultivating oilseeds and pulses can apply for this scheme.

# Benefits
Farmers receive financial support for the cultivation of oilseeds and pulses, which helps in improving their income and supporting agricultural productivity.

# Funding
The funding for PMDDKY is provided by the government to ensure that eligible farmers receive the necessary financial assistance.
