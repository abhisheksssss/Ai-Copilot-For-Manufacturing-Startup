# Overview
The Climate Resilience Building Among Farmers Through Crop Residue Management scheme aims to enhance the resilience of farmers against climate change impacts by promoting effective management and utilization of crop residues.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants include farmers who cultivate crops that generate significant amounts of residue, such as wheat, rice, sugarcane, etc.

# Benefits
Farmers will benefit from improved soil health, reduced air pollution due to stubble burning, and increased income through the sale or utilization of crop residues for various purposes like bio-energy, composting, and animal feed.

# Funding
The scheme provides financial support to farmers for adopting practices that manage and utilize crop residue effectively. The exact amount of funding per farmer is not specified in this text but can be obtained from official sources.
