# Overview
The National Livestock Mission (NLM) is a scheme under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairy that promotes entrepreneurship in sectors such as poultry, sheep, goat, piggery, and fodder.

# Eligibility
Eligible applicants include individuals, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), and Section 8 companies.

# Benefits
Entrepreneurs can avail a capital subsidy of up to 50% on loans/credit up to ₹50 lakh for project finance depending on the category of the project.

# Funding
The scheme provides a 50% capital subsidy routed with loan/project finance, depending on the project category.
