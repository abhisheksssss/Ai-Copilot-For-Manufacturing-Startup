# Overview
The Mission for Integrated Development of Tribal Areas (MIDTA) is a comprehensive initiative involving 17 ministries to enhance the socio-economic conditions and empower tribal communities through 25 interventions over a period of 5 years.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. Typically, this would include specific tribal communities or regions as beneficiaries.

# Benefits
The benefits include improvements in various sectors such as education, health, infrastructure, and livelihood opportunities for tribal populations.

# Funding
Funding will be sourced from the Development Action Plan for Scheduled Tribes (DAPST) over a 5-year period to support the implementation of the mission's interventions.
