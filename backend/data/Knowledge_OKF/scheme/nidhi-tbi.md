# Overview
The NIDHI-TBI scheme provides support for establishing and strengthening technology business incubators for tech startups, with a focus on host institutions/incubators that support knowledge-based and technology-driven startups.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to host institutions/incubators that support knowledge-based and technology-driven startups.

# Benefits
The scheme provides grant to incubator + incubation services for startups, with incubator-level support varying and generally being multi-crore per DST approval.

# Funding
The funding for the scheme is provided by the Department of Science & Technology (DST), with the amount varying based on DST approval.
