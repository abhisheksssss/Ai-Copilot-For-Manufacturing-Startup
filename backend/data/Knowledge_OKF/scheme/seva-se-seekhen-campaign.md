# Overview
A youth engagement and hands-on learning campaign designed to involve young citizens in public service environments like Jan Aushadhi Kendras, launched as part of India’s National Youth Development Framework in 2025.

# Eligibility
Volunteers are drawn from MY Bharat, NSS, pharmacy colleges, and other youth platforms.

# Benefits
- Foster experiential learning by placing youth in service environments.
- Enhance public health awareness and generic medicine outreach.
- Develop soft and technical skills related to inventory, logistics, and community service.
- Encourage nation-building values like discipline, empathy, and grassroots engagement.
