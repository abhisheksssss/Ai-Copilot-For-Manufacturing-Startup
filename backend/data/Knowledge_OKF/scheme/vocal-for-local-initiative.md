# Overview
Under the VOCAL FOR LOCAL INITIATIVE, indigenous local products from 500 Aspirational Blocks have been mapped and consolidated under the brand of Aakansha.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is implied that producers or sellers of local products within the 500 Aspirational Blocks may be eligible.

# Benefits
The initiative aims to provide a platform for promoting and selling local products through e-commerce. It also includes support for technical and operational aspects such as establishing linkages, financial/digital literacy, documentation/certification, and skill enhancement.
