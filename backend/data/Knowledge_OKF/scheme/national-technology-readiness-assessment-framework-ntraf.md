# Overview
The National Technology Readiness Assessment Framework (NTRAF) is a standardized, objective framework to assess the maturity of technologies from early laboratory research to full commercial deployment using 9 Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs).

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include researchers, investors, and policymakers. Self-assessment tools are available for researchers and startups.

# Benefits
- Establishes a common language between stakeholders.
- Enables evidence-based funding decisions under national R&D and mission-mode programmes.
- Reduces the "Valley of Death" by de-risking promising deep-tech innovations.

Not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it supports evidence-based funding decisions which can indirectly influence funding opportunities.
