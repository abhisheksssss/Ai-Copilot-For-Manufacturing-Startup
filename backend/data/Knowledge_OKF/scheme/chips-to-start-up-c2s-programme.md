# Overview
The Chips to Start-up (C2S) Programme is a national capacity-building and innovation initiative launched in 2022 by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). Its aim is to create a robust pipeline of skilled chip designers, enable hands-on chip fabrication, foster start-ups and IP creation, and support technological self-reliance in semiconductors.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it targets students and professionals interested in chip design and related fields.

# Benefits
- Development of industry-ready chip design talent.
- Hands-on training through shared EDA tools, HPC, FPGA boards, and SMART labs.
- Access to fabrication facilities via Semi-Conductor Laboratory (SCL), Mohali.
- Training with global EDA and semiconductor firms.
- Innovation outcomes such as start-up incubation, patents, IP cores, ASICs, and SoCs.

# Funding
Outlay: ₹250 crore over 5 years.
