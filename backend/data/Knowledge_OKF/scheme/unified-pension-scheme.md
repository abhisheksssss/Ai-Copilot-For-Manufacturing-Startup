# Overview
The Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) is a pension scheme managed by the Department of Financial Services under the Ministry of Finance. It offers assured benefits to government employees, including an assured pension, spousal benefits, and inflation indexation.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the Unified Pension Scheme include a minimum qualifying service period of 25 years or proportionate pay for lesser periods up to a minimum of 10 years of service. Additionally, a lump sum payment at superannuation in addition to gratuity is provided based on completed six months of service.

# Benefits
- **Assured Pension:** 50% of the average basic pay drawn over the last 12 months prior to superannuation for a minimum qualifying service period of 25 years. The pay is proportionate for lesser periods up to a minimum of 10 years.
- **Spousal Benefits:** 60% of the employee's pension immediately before her/his demise.
- **Assured Minimum Pension:** ₹10,000 per month on superannuation after a minimum of 10 years of service.
- **Inflation Indexation:** On assured pension, assured family pension, and assured minimum pension. Dearness relief based on the All-India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (CPI-IW) as in case of service employees.

# Funding
The Unified Pension Scheme involves contributions from both the government and the employee:
- **New Pension System (NPS):** Both by Government and Employee (14% government).
- **Old Pension Scheme (OPS):** Only the Government.
- **Unified Pension Scheme (UPS):** Both Government and Employee (Government 18.5%, Employee 10%).
