# Overview
The National Quantum Mission (NQM) is a national mission aimed at supporting startups in deep-tech research and development (R&D) in the fields of quantum computing, communication, sensing/metrology, and materials/devices.

# Eligibility
DPIIT-recognized startups working on quantum technology can apply for grants through 4 Thematic Hubs located at IISc, IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, and IIT Delhi.

# Benefits
Startups will receive up to ₹25 crore per startup. The mission outlay is ₹6,003.65 crore (FY2024–FY2031).

# Funding
The funding includes quantum technology commercialization R&D grants, incubation support, testbed access, and ecosystem mentoring via the Thematic Hubs.
