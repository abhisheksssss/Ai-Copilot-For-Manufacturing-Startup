# Overview
The PRITHVI VIGYAN scheme aims to enhance the understanding of the Earth and its vital signs through various research initiatives.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
- Augmentation and sustenance of long-term observations of the atmosphere, ocean, geosphere, cryosphere, and solid earth.
- Development of modelling systems for understanding and predicting weather, ocean, and climate hazards.
- Exploration of polar and high seas regions towards discovery of new phenomena and resources.
- Development of technology for exploration and sustainable harnessing of oceanic resources.
- Translation of knowledge and insights from Earth systems science into services for societal, environmental, and economic benefit.
