# Overview
The Top Class School Education scheme provides premium education to meritorious students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and Differently Abled Notified Trainees (DNT) categories. It funds their education from Class 9 onwards until they complete Class 12.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates must be meritorious students belonging to OBC, EBC, or DNT categories.

# Benefits
Scholarships are awarded for tuition fees, hostel fees, and other charges as required by the school. The maximum scholarship amount is Rs. 75,000/- per annum per student for Class 9 and 10, and Rs. 1,25,000/- per annum per student for Class 11 and 12.

# Funding
The scheme funds tuition fees, hostel fees, and other charges as required by the school up to a maximum of Rs. 75,000/- per annum for Classes 9 and 10, and Rs. 1,25,000/- per annum for Classes 11 and 12.
