# Overview
The SAGARMALA Programme aims to reduce logistics costs for EXIM and domestic trade with minimal infrastructure investment by enhancing port efficiency, connectivity, and industrialization.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, entities such as ports, industries, and coastal communities may benefit from this programme.

# Benefits
- Reducing cost of transporting domestic cargo through optimising the modal mix.
- Lowering logistics cost for bulk commodities by strategically locating future industrial capacities near the coast.
- Improving export competitiveness by developing port-based discrete manufacturing clusters.
- Optimising time/cost for EXIM container movement through efficient port infrastructure.

# Funding
The programme focuses on expanding port capacity to 3,300+ MMTPA by 2025, strengthening connectivity between ports and production/consumption centers via rail, road, and inland waterways, establishing Coastal Economic Zones (CEZs), Coastal Economic Units (CEUs), and maritime clusters, and promoting marine-sector activities, skill development, and fisheries to uplift coastal communities.
