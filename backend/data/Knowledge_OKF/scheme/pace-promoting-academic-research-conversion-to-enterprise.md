# Overview
The PACE programme, managed by BIRAC under the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), aims to facilitate the conversion of academic research into enterprise-relevant innovations through validation and translational research.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include academic researchers, institutions, and industry partners involved in biotech research projects.

# Benefits
Participants receive grant support for project-based activities, typically ranging from ₹50 lakh to ₹1 crore depending on the PACE component. The primary focus is on validating and translating biotech research into commercial pathways.

# Funding
Funding is provided through grants that are commonly up to ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore per project, contingent upon the specific PACE component.
