# Overview
The Biotechnology Ignition Grant Scheme (BIG) is a flagship grant provided by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) through BIRAC, aimed at translating innovative biotech ideas into proof-of-concept.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators and early biotech startups with commercially promising ideas are eligible to apply for this grant.

# Benefits
Applicants can receive up to ₹50 lakh for a period of up to 18 months. The grant-in-aid is accompanied by mentoring services, ensuring no equity dilution.

# Funding
The funding provided under the BIG scheme is in the form of a grant-in-aid and includes mentoring support from BIRAC without any equity dilution.
