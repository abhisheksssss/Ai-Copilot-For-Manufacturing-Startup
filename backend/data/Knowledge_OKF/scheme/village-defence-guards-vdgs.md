# Overview
Village Defence Guards (VDGs) are armed civilian defence groups constituted in vulnerable areas of Jammu & Kashmir to assist security forces in counter-terrorism, village protection, and intelligence gathering. Launched in March 2022, approved by the Union Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), replacing and restructuring the earlier Village Defence Committees (VDCs) established in 1995.

# Eligibility
VDGs are mainly composed of ex-servicemen and trained civilians identified at the panchayat level. Each group can have up to 15 members.

# Benefits
The key benefits include:
- Providing localised, immediate defence against militant threats.
- Acting as a force multiplier for police and armed forces in remote and border villages.
- Enhancing community participation in internal security.

# Funding
VDGs are trained by the CRPF/Army and equipped with Self-Loading Rifles (SLRs). Group heads receive ₹4,500/month, while members receive ₹4,000/month. This is an improvement over earlier VDCs where only SPOs were paid.
