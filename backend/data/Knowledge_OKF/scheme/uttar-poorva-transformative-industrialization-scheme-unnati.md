# Overview
The UTTAR POORVA TRANSFORMATIVE INDUSTRIALIZATION SCHEME (UNNATI) was launched in 2024 and will run till 2034 with a corpus of 10,037 crore. The scheme is aimed at generating direct employment opportunities of about 83,000 and significant indirect employment.

# Eligibility
All eligible industrial units are required to commence their production or operation within 4 years from the grant of registration. Industrial units in Zone A (Industrially Advanced Districts) and Zone B (Industrially Backward Districts) are categorized under this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme offers incentives for both new industrial units and expanding units. The outlay is divided such that 60% has been earmarked for the 8 Northeast states, while 40% is allocated on a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) basis.

# Funding
The total corpus of 10,037 crore has been allocated with specific distribution criteria. The scheme is managed by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade.
