# Overview
The Yuva Sahakar scheme is a credit scheme by the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) that supports newly formed innovative cooperative societies and young cooperative entrepreneurs.

# Eligibility
Cooperative societies in operation for a minimum period as per the scheme, with a focus on youth-led or new cooperatives, are eligible for the scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a loan up to project norms, often up to ₹3 crore project cost reference, along with a 2% interest subvention.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) under the Ministry of Cooperation, providing long-term loans with a 2% interest subvention.
