# Overview
The National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme launched by the Government of India to promote chemical-free and sustainable agricultural practices across the country.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for farmers to participate in NMNF are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is implied that willing farmers can benefit from this scheme.

# Benefits
The mission aims to encourage farmers to adopt natural farming methods, thereby reducing dependency on chemical fertilizers and pesticides, enhancing soil health, and providing safe and nutritious food.

# Funding
The scheme has a total budget of ₹2,481 crore. The Government of India contributes ₹1,584 crore, while the remaining ₹897 crore is contributed by state governments, covering the period up to the 15th Finance Commission (2025-26).
