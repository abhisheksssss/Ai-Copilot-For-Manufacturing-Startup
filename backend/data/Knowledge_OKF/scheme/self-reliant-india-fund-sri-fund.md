# Overview
The Self-Reliant India Fund (SRI Fund) is a scaling fund-of-funds that provides equity/quasi-equity infusion into growth-oriented MSMEs through daughter funds.

# Eligibility
Viable MSMEs and startup MSMEs with growth potential are eligible for funding indirectly through Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs).

# Benefits
The SRI Fund provides equity/quasi-equity infusion to MSMEs, supporting their growth and development.

# Funding
The overall provision for the SRI Fund is ₹50,000 crore, with the mother fund allocated ₹10,000 crore. The enterprise ticket size varies.
