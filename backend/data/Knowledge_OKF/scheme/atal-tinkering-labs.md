# Overview
Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) is a government scheme that aims to build creativity and problem-solving skills among students, with a focus on designing and innovating for the future startup pipeline.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to schools and students, with a focus on building innovation labs and fostering creativity and problem-solving skills.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a lab grant, equipment, mentoring, and innovation challenges to selected schools, with the goal of fostering innovation and design thinking skills among students.

# Funding
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹20 lakh per selected school over multiple years.
