### Government Schemes Comprehensive (Part 1)

#### 22.20 RASHTRIYA AROGYA NIDHI (RAN)
- **Intended Beneficiary:** Patients living below poverty line who are suffering from major life-threatening diseases.
- **Not Included:** Government servants and their families, Families covered under Ayushman Bharat scheme.
- **Salient Features:**
  - Implemented through schools and Aanganwadi centres.
  - Mass awareness about STH treatment using Albendazole tablets.
  - Behavior change practices in terms of cleanliness, hygiene, use of toilets, wearing shoes/chappals, washing hands.
  - National Centre for Diseases Control is the nodal agency to conduct STH mapping.
  - Single fixed-day approach: Intestinal worm infections treated every year on February 10 and August 10.

#### 22.21 INTEGRATED DISEASES SURVEILLANCE PROGRAM (IDSP)
- **Objectives:** Strengthen/maintain decentralized laboratory-based IT enabled disease surveillance system for epidemic-prone diseases.
- **Salient Features:**
  - Central & State Disease Surveillance Unit.
  - Early warning system to take timely preventive steps.
  - Covers both communicable and non-communicable diseases.
  - Data collected on epidemic-prone diseases weekly.
  - Focus on inter-sectoral coordination for zoonotic diseases.
  - Rapid Response Teams (RRT) investigate rising trends of illnesses.

#### 22.23 NATIONAL VIRAL HEPATITIS CONTROL PROGRAM (NVHCP)
- **Objectives:** Ensure high coverage of ORS and Zinc use rates in children with diarrhea throughout the country, inculcate appropriate behavior for prevention & management.
- **Salient Features:**
  - Enhance community awareness on hepatitis and preventive measures among general population, especially high-risk groups and hotspots.
  - Three-action framework:
    - Mobilize health personnel, state governments, NGOs.
    - Prioritize investment from government and international organizations.
    - Create mass awareness through ORS and Zinc therapy demonstrations.

#### 22.24 MISCELLANEOUS INITIATIVES

- **Project Sunrise:**
  - Aimed at diagnosing 90% of drug addicts with HIV by 2020 in 20 districts across eight North-Eastern states.
  - Steered by National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO), funded under the Centre for Disease Control, implemented in coordination with state AIDS control organizations and NGOs.

- **National AIDS Control Programme-IV (NACP-IV):**
  - NACP I: Started in 1992 to slow down HIV spread.
  - NACP II: Started in 1999 to reduce HIV infection spread and increase capacity for long-term response.
  - NACP III: Started in 2007 with the goal of halting and reversing the epidemic over five years.

- **National Programme for Control of Blindness (NPCB):**
  - Launched in 1976 as a 100% Centrally Sponsored Scheme, now 60:40 in all states and 90:10 in NE States.
  - Goal to reduce blindness prevalence from 1.4% to 0.3%.

### Summary
- **RAN:** Focuses on STH treatment and behavior change practices among poor patients.
- **IDSP:** Early warning system for epidemic-prone diseases, inter-sectoral coordination.
- **NVHCP:** Enhances awareness and management of viral hepatitis, ORS and Zinc therapy.
- **Project Sunrise & NACP-IV:** HIV prevention and control in North-Eastern states.
- **NPCB:** Reducing blindness prevalence through comprehensive health programs.

These schemes aim to improve public health outcomes by addressing specific diseases and behaviors, ensuring early detection and effective management. If you have any more questions or need further details on these schemes, feel free to ask!
