# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI MATSYA KISAN SAMRIDHI YOJANA is a Central Sector Sub-scheme under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY). It has an estimated outlay of Rs.6,000 crore with 50% public finance and the remaining 50% from anticipated investment by beneficiaries/private sector leverage. The scheme will be implemented for 4 years starting from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27 across all States and UTs.

# Eligibility
Intended beneficiaries include:
- Fishers, fish (aquaculture) farmers, fish workers, fish vendors or any other persons directly engaged in the fisheries value chain.
- Micro and small enterprises.
- Farmers Producer Organizations (FPOs).

# Benefits
The scheme aims to create a National Fisheries Digital Platform (NFDP) for 40 lakh small and micro-enterprises. It also focuses on:
- Gradual formalization of the fisheries sector.
- Enhanced access to institutional credit for micro-enterprises and fisheries cooperatives.
- Transition from conventional subsidies to performance-based incentives in fisheries.
- Supporting targeted MSEs to improve value chain efficiency and quality control.
- Enhancing export competitiveness through value addition, realization, and creation.
- Creating 1.7 lakh new jobs with a special emphasis on employing 75,000 women.

# Funding
The scheme has an estimated outlay of Rs.6,000 crore, consisting of 50% public finance (including World Bank and AFD external financing) and the remaining 50% from anticipated investment by beneficiaries/private sector leverage.
