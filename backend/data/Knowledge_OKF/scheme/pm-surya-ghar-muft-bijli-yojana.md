# Overview
PM SURYA GHAR: MUFT BIJLI YOJANA was launched in 2024 with the aim of providing free electricity to households by promoting the installation of solar panels.

# Eligibility
Households are eligible to apply for this scheme through the National Portal. They can select a suitable vendor for installing rooftop solar and will be assisted in their decision-making process by relevant information provided on the portal.

# Benefits
- Subsidy up to 40% of the cost of solar panels.
- Access to collateral-free low-interest loan products at 7% for installation of residential Rooftop Solar (RTS) systems up to 3 kW.
- Development of Model Solar Villages in each district as role models for rural areas.

# Funding
Households can access low-interest loan products of around 7% for the installation of residential Rooftop Solar (RTS) systems up to 3 kW.
