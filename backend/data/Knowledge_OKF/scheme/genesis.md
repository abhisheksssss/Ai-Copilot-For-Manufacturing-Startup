# Overview
GENESIS is a scheme by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) that aims to discover, support, and scale technology startups in Tier-II/III cities via implementing agencies. The scheme focuses on supporting technology startups and entrepreneurs in Tier-II/III locations, including deep-tech ventures.

# Eligibility
The scheme is targeted at technology startups and entrepreneurs in Tier-II/III cities, including deep-tech ventures.

# Benefits
The scheme provides startup-level support, which varies by component, including EiR, pilot, investment, and deep-tech funding support through implementing agencies.

# Funding
The scheme has an outlay of ₹490 crore, with startup-level support varying by component.
