# Overview
UDAN (UDE DESH KA AAM NAGRIK) Scheme was launched by the Ministry of Civil Aviation to improve air connectivity to remote and regional areas, making flights more accessible and affordable in tier-2 and tier-3 towns. The latest phase, UDAN 5.5, focuses on promoting last-mile connectivity in remote regions using sea planes, choppers, and small aircraft.

# Eligibility
Airlines and operators of aircraft with seating capacities as follows are eligible:
- Category ‘1A’: less than nine passengers.
- Category ‘1’: less than 20 passengers.

# Benefits
- Airlines must cap airfares for 50% of the total seats at Rs. 2,500 per hour of flight.
- Financial stimulus through concessions from Central and State governments and airport operators.
- Viability Gap Funding (VGF) provided by the government to bridge the gap between operational costs and expected revenue.
- Regional Connectivity Fund (RCF) created for VGF requirements under the scheme.

# Funding
- Partner State Governments contribute 20% to the RCF, with UTs and NER states contributing only 10%.
