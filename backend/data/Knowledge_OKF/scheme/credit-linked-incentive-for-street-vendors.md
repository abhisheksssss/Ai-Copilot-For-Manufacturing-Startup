# Overview
The Credit Linked Incentive for Street Vendors is a scheme implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to encourage credit discipline among street vendors through various incentives.

# Eligibility
Target beneficiaries include street vendors operating in statutory towns, including those using carts, stalls, or footpaths. The scheme involves collaboration with State/UT governments, Urban Local Bodies, and lending institutions.

# Benefits
- **Initial Loan:** A collateral-free loan of ₹10,000 as the first tranche.
- **Interest Subsidy:** 7% per annum subsidy on timely repayment.
- **Digital Incentives:** Cashback of up to ₹100 per month for digital transactions.
- **Credit Linkage:** Higher loan tranches are available based on successful repayment.

# Funding
The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) in collaboration with State/UT governments, Urban Local Bodies, and lending institutions. The funding mechanism includes interest subsidies and digital incentives provided through these entities.
