# Overview
The Mutual Credit Guarantee Scheme (MCGS) for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) is designed to provide credit facility up to Rs. 100 crore with a 60% guarantee coverage by the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Limited (NCGTC). This scheme aims to facilitate access to finance for MSMEs for purchasing equipment or machinery.

# Eligibility
Borrowers must be MSMEs with a valid Udyam Registration Number. The loan amount is up to Rs. 100 crore, and the repayment period varies based on the loan amount:
- For loans up to Rs. 50 crore: Repayment period of up to 8 years with a 2-year moratorium on principal installments.
- For loans above Rs. 50 crore: Higher repayment schedule and moratorium period may be considered.

# Benefits
The scheme offers several benefits, including:
- Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 100 crore.
- An upfront contribution of 5% of the loan amount at the time of applying for the guarantee cover.
- No annual guarantee fees during the sanction year; 1.5% per annum on outstanding loan for the next three years, and 1% per annum thereafter.

# Funding
The scheme provides a credit facility up to Rs. 100 crore with 60% guarantee coverage by NCGTC. The duration of the scheme is applicable to all loans for 4 years from the date of issue of operational guidelines or until a cumulative guarantee of Rs. 7 lakh crore is issued, whichever comes first.
