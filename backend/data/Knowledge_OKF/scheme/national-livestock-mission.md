# Overview
The National Livestock Mission (NLM) is a government scheme that aims to promote entrepreneurship in livestock sectors such as poultry, sheep, goat, piggery, and fodder.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to individuals, FPOs, SHGs, JLGs, and Section 8 companies who are interested in entrepreneurship in livestock sectors.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a 50% capital subsidy up to ₹50 lakh to eligible beneficiaries.

# Funding
The capital subsidy is routed with loan/project finance, depending on the project category.
