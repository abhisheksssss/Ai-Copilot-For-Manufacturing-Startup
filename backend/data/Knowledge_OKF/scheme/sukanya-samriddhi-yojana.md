# Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small deposit scheme aimed at motivating parents to open an account in the name of their girl child. The scheme allows for deposits until the girl attains 10 years of age, with a maximum of two accounts per family.

# Eligibility
The scheme can be opened from the birth of a girl child till she is 10 years old. A minimum deposit of Rs. 250 and a maximum of Rs. 1.5 lakh can be made during each fiscal year.

# Benefits
- The account matures in 21 years, either on the date of opening or upon marriage after attaining 18 years.
- The account cannot be operated by the girl child until she is 18 years old.
- Premature closure is allowed in case of death or compassionate grounds.
- Partial withdrawal for education can be done when the girl has cleared her 10th class or turned 18.

# Funding
The scheme does not specify any funding mechanism, as it is a government initiative aimed at encouraging savings among families with girl children.
