# Overview
The Production Linked Incentive Scheme (PLI) for White Goods Manufacturers in India is designed to incentivize the production of air conditioners and LED lights within the country.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include manufacturers of white goods such as air conditioners and LED lights.

# Benefits
Manufacturers will receive incentives based on their incremental manufacturing output.

# Funding
The scheme provides financial incentives linked to the production volume.
