# Overview
The E-BILL System for Fertiliser Subsidies is a scheme implemented by the Ministry of Mines to provide subsidies directly to farmers through electronic billing, ensuring transparency and efficiency in the distribution of fertilizer subsidies.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries include farmers who purchase fertilizers from participating retailers. The system ensures that only genuine purchases are eligible for subsidies.

# Benefits
Farmers benefit from direct cash subsidies credited to their bank accounts upon purchase of fertilizers through this electronic system, reducing the need for physical documentation and minimizing corruption.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the government through the allocation of specific budgetary provisions for fertilizer subsidies. The exact amount is not specified in the provided text but is managed within the annual budget allocated to the Ministry of Mines.
