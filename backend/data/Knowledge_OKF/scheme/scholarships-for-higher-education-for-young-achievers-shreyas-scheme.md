# Overview
The Scholarships for Higher Education for Young Achievers (Shreyas) Scheme comprises four central sector sub-schemes aimed at providing scholarships to young achievers from Scheduled Castes (SCs). These sub-schemes are "Top Class Education for SCs," "Free Coaching Scheme for SCs and Other Backward Classes (OBCs)," "National Overseas Scheme for SCs," and "National Fellowship for SCs."

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the Shreyas Scheme include being a young achiever from Scheduled Castes. Specific details on age, academic performance, and other conditions may vary among the sub-schemes.

# Benefits
The scheme offers various benefits such as financial assistance, coaching support, overseas opportunities, and fellowships to eligible SC students pursuing higher education.

# Funding
Funding for the Shreyas Scheme is provided by the government through central sector schemes. The exact amount of funding per student or per sub-scheme is not specified in this text.
