# Overview
The PMFME scheme aims to formalise and upgrade micro food-processing enterprises under the One District One Product initiative.

# Eligibility
Individual micro food-processing units, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), cooperatives, and food startups/micro-enterprises are eligible for this scheme.

# Benefits
- 35% credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 lakh per unit.
- SHG seed capital up to ₹40,000/member.
- Branding/marketing support.
- Capacity building support.

# Funding
The scheme provides a 35% credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 lakh per unit and seed capital of up to ₹40,000 per member for SHGs. Additional support includes branding and marketing assistance as well as capacity building.
