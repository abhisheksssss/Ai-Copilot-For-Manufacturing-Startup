# Overview
The BioNEST Programme is a BIRAC programme that aims to create bio-incubators with wet labs, equipment, mentoring, and commercialisation support for biotech startups. It supports incubators, academic institutions, and research parks that are dedicated to supporting biotech startups.

# Eligibility
Incubators, academic institutions, and research parks that support biotech startups are eligible for the BioNEST Programme.

# Benefits
The BioNEST Programme provides bio-incubation infrastructure and startup services, including wet labs, equipment, mentoring, and commercialisation support, to biotech startups.

# Funding
The incubator-level grant varies by call, and startup support is provided indirectly through the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) / BIRAC.
