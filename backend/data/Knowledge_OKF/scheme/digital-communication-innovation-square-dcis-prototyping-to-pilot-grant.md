# Overview
The Digital Communication Innovation Square (DCIS) Prototyping to Pilot Grant programme supports indigenous telecom and ICT product innovation and commercialisation, focusing on startups, MSMEs, academia, and innovators developing telecom/digital communication solutions.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to startups, MSMEs, academia, and innovators developing telecom/digital communication solutions.

# Benefits
The selected proposals will receive a grant-in-aid of up to ₹50 lakh, along with mentoring and testing support.

# Funding
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹50 lakh per selected proposal for startups.
