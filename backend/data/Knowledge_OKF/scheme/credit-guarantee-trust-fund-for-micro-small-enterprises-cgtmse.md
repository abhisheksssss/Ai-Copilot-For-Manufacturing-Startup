# Overview
The Credit Guarantee Trust Fund for Micro & Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) is a credit support guarantee scheme that enables collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises through member lending institutions.

# Eligibility
Micro, small, and medium enterprises including startup MSMEs can borrow from eligible banks/NBFCs/FIs under this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides credit guarantee coverage up to ₹10 crore with varying guarantee percentages. This facilitates easier access to debt without the need for collateral.
