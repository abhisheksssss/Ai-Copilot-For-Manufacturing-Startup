# Overview
Mission Vatsalya is a scheme formed by restructuring the Child Protection Scheme which was part of the erstwhile Umbrella ICDS scheme. It aims to create a safe and secure environment for comprehensive development of children who need care and protection, children in conflict with law, and other vulnerable children. The scheme also assists states in delivering the mandate of the Juvenile Justice Act, 2015, and achieving SDG goals.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text but generally include children who need care and protection, children in conflict with law, and other vulnerable children.

# Benefits
- Improving the functioning of statutory bodies.
- Strengthening service delivery services.
- Upscaling institutional care by childcare institutions.
- Supporting after-care programs for young adults (children aged 18 years and above) who have been discharged from Children’s Homes/Special Homes.
- Encouraging non-institutional community-based care, such as family-based non-institutional care through sponsorship, foster care, and adoption.
- Providing emergency outreach services through Childline and the Child Tracking System.
- Training and capacity building of duty-holders.
