# Overview
The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) facilitated the first-ever air shipment of Geographical Indication (GI)-tagged Indi Lime from Karnataka and Puliyankudi Lime from Tamil Nadu to the United Kingdom.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include producers, exporters, and traders of GI-tagged limes from the specified regions in India.

# Benefits
- Enhanced market value for regional products.
- Protection against unauthorized use of registered names.
- Promotion of authenticity and economic benefits for local producers.

# Funding
No specific funding details provided. However, APEDA facilitated the export process.
