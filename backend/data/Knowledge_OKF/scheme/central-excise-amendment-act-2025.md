# Overview
The Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025 amends the Central Excise Act, 1944 to revise excise duties on tobacco and related products.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, the scheme applies to manufacturers and sellers of tobacco and tobacco-related products.

# Benefits
The key benefits include:
- Revision of excise duty rates to maintain and enhance overall tax burden.
- Higher duties on various types of tobacco products.
- Public health objective aligned with global guidance for real cigarette prices to rise faster than incomes.
- Restructuring of GST rates for certain tobacco products.
- Introduction of a new valuation mechanism based on retail sale price declared on the package.

# Funding
The scheme is funded through excise duties and additional levies such as the GST compensation cess, which is being phased out from February 1, 2026.
