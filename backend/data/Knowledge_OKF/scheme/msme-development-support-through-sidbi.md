# Overview
Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), established under an Act of Parliament in 1990, acts as the Principal Financial Institution for Promotion, Financing and Development of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (MSME) sector. SIDBI has realigned its role through adoption of SIDBI Vision 2.0 which envisages an integrated credit and development support role by being a thought leader, adopting a credit-plus approach, creating a multiplier effect, and serving as an aggregator in MSME space.
