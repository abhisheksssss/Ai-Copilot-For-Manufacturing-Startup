# Overview

The First Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari (JSJB) Awards is a flagship initiative of the Department of Water Resources, River Development & Ganga Rejuvenation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti, launched in 2018. It aims to promote community participation, awareness, and innovation in water resource management.

# Eligibility

Eligible entities include States, Districts, Village Panchayats, Industries, NGOs, Institutions, Municipal Corporations, and philanthropists.

# Benefits

Winners receive recognition and financial incentives based on their performance. Top-performing districts can receive up to ₹2 crore as awards.

# Funding

The initiative is funded through the Rashtriya Puraskar Portal and involves collaboration between the Ministry of Jal Shakti and other relevant ministries for urban water recharge projects.
