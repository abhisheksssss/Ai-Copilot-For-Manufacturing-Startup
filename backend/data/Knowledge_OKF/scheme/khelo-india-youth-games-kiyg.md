# Overview
The Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) is an annual national-level multi-sport event under the Khelo India scheme, aimed at identifying, nurturing, and promoting grassroots sporting talent among India's youth.

# Eligibility
Target Group: Youth athletes under 17 and 21 years of age.

# Benefits
Winners receive scholarships under the Khelo India Scheme.

# Funding
Not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is part of the broader Khelo India scheme which includes funding for various initiatives to promote sports at grassroots levels.
