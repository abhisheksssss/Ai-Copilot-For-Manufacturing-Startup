# Overview
The Fintech Incentive Scheme Prototype to Market Entry is designed by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) to attract and support fintech entities, startups, and accelerators operating or engaging with the IFSC ecosystem at GIFT City.

# Eligibility
Indian and foreign fintech entities, startups, and accelerators that are operating or engaging with GIFT IFSC can apply for this scheme.

# Benefits
Participants in this scheme will receive grant support across various categories including sandbox access, proof-of-concept (PoC) funding, listing opportunities, accelerator programs, and special grants. The total grant amount can be up to ₹75 lakh, but the specific amount varies by incentive type.

# Funding
The scheme provides a range of financial incentives in the form of grants for different categories such as sandbox access, PoC, listing, accelerator programs, and special grants. The maximum grant amount is up to ₹75 lakh, with varying amounts based on the type of incentive.
