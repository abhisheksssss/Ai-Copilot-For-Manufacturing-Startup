# Overview
The Entrepreneurship & Skill Development Programme (ESDP) is a capacity building MSME training programme that aims to build entrepreneurial and managerial skills for prospective entrepreneurs. The programme is designed for prospective and existing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, youth, and startups that need business skills.

# Eligibility
The programme is open to prospective and existing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, youth, and startups that need business skills.

# Benefits
The programme provides training, workshops, and skill-development support to help entrepreneurs develop their business skills. The programme also supports the training cost per programme norms.

# Funding
The programme does not provide direct startup funding, but it supports the training cost per programme norms.
