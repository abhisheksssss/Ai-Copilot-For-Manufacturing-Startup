# Overview
BioAngels is a scaling biotech angel investment and investor-connect platform curated for BIRAC-supported life-science startups, focusing on biotech, medtech, healthtech, and life-sciences startups seeking angel capital.

# Eligibility
Biotech, medtech, healthtech, and life-sciences startups supported by BIRAC are eligible to apply for the BioAngels platform.

# Benefits
The BioAngels platform provides angel investment readiness and investor connects, facilitating private investment for eligible startups.

# Funding
The funding provided through the BioAngels platform varies by angel round and investor decision, with the specific amount of equity investment determined on a case-by-case basis.
