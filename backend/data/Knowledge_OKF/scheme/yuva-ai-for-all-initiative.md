# Overview
A free, 4.5-hour self-paced online course designed to introduce Artificial Intelligence to a broad audience, with content tailored to the Indian context.

# Eligibility
Open to students, professionals, and curious learners across India.

# Benefits
- Government-certified certificate upon completion.
- Available on platforms like FutureSkills Prime, iGOT Karmayogi, and other ed-tech portals.
- Six modules covering AI basics, real-world applications, safety, ethics, and future opportunities.
- Uses Indian examples to make learning relatable and practical.

# Funding
Completely free.
