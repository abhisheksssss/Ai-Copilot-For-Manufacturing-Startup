# Overview
The National Food Security Act (NFSA) 2013 provides a legal right to persons belonging to "eligible households" to receive food grains at subsidised prices under the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS). The scheme aims to ensure all beneficiaries, especially migrants, get ration across the nation from any Public Distribution System (PDS) shop of their own choice.

# Eligibility
Eligible households are categorized into two types:
- **Priority Households:** Entitled to 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month.
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana Families:** Entitled to 35 kg per month. 

The present Central Issue Prices (CIPs) for food grains are: rice at Rs 3/kg, wheat at Rs 2/kg, and coarse grain at Rs 1/kg.

# Benefits
- Pregnant women, lactating mothers, and children aged 6 months to 14 years receive nutritious meals free of cost through Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) centers.
- Cash maternity benefits of not less than Rs. 6,000 for pregnant women and lactating mothers.
- Food security allowance for non-supply of entitled quantities of food grains.

# Funding
The scheme is operated under the joint responsibility of the Central and State/UT Governments:
- The Centre allocates required food grains to States/UTs, transports them up to designated depots in each State/UT, and provides central assistance.
- States/UTs are responsible for implementing the Act, including identifying eligible households, issuing ration cards, distributing foodgrain entitlements through Fair Price Shops (FPS), and monitoring FPS dealers.
