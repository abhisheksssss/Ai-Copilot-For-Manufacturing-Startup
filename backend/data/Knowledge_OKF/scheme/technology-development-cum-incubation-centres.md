# Overview
The Technology Development-cum-Incubation Centres scheme aims to translate DAE spin-off technologies into market-ready products by supporting startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs.

# Eligibility
Startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs are eligible to license and incubate DAE technologies under this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides benefits such as technology transfer, lab access, scientist mentoring, and incubation support to the eligible entities.

# Funding
There is no standard grant provided under this scheme; instead, support is based on incubation and technology access.
