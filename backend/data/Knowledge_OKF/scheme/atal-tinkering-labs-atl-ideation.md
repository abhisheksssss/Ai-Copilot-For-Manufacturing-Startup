# Overview
Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) Ideation is a scheme under the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), initiated by NITI Aayog, aimed at fostering creativity and innovation among school students.

# Eligibility
The scheme targets schools to build innovation labs that can nurture future startup talent through design thinking and problem-solving skills development in students.

# Benefits
Selected schools receive grants for setting up innovation labs, along with equipment, mentoring, and support for participating in various innovation challenges over multiple years.

# Funding
Up to ₹20 lakh per selected school is provided as a grant over the course of the scheme.
