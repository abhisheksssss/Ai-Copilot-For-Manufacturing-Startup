# Overview
PMSMA is a maternal health programme launched by the Government of India in June 2016, under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare. It provides free and quality antenatal care (ANC) to pregnant women on the 9th of every month.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals are pregnant women who can access services at government health facilities on fixed ANC days.

# Benefits
- Ensures at least one ANC check-up in the 2nd or 3rd trimester by a trained specialist.
- Promotes early detection and management of high-risk pregnancies (HRPs).
- Improves quality of care through diagnostics, counselling, and risk stratification.
- Identifies and tracks HRPs through a name-based monitoring system.
- Raises awareness and mobilizes private sector participation to supplement public services.

# Funding
The scheme provides free antenatal care services. Financial incentives are provided for additional ANC visits beyond the standard coverage under e-PMSMA.
