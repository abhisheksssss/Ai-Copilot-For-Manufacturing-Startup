# Overview
AgriSURE is a Category-II AIF that catalyses equity and debt capital for agri-tech, rural, and allied sector ventures. It is jointly sponsored by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoAFW) and NABARD.

# Eligibility
Agri-tech startups, rural enterprises, FPO-linked innovations, and value-chain ventures in agriculture are eligible for funding under AgriSURE.

# Benefits
The scheme provides direct equity up to ₹25 crore per startup, with a total fund corpus of ₹750 crore.

# Funding
The funding is provided in the form of equity, debt, or blended capital, through both direct and fund routes.
