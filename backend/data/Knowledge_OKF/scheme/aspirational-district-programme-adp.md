# Overview
ADP is the government of India’s flagship programme launched in 2018 and anchored by NITI Aayog, supported by central ministries and state governments. The programme aims to address economic, social, and developmental gaps in these districts and unlock their potential for growth and development.

# Eligibility
The ADP focuses on the 112 most backward districts in India.

# Benefits
- Convergence of central and state schemes.
- Addressing economic, social, and developmental gaps.
- Holistic change through improvements in six thematic areas.
- Measurable progress with data live on the Champions of Change portal.
- Promotion of best practices for replication across districts.
- Application of behavioural insights to improve service uptake.
