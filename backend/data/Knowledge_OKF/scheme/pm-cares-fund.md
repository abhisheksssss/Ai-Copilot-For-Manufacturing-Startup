# Overview
PM Cares (Prime Minister’s Citizen Assistance and Relief in Emergency Situations Fund) was created on 27 March 2020, following the COVID-19 pandemic in India.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is a fund aimed at providing assistance during emergencies or distress situations for any affected population.

# Benefits
Donations to PM CARES Fund would qualify for 80G benefits for 100% exemption under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Donations can also be counted as Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) expenditure under the Companies Act, 2013.

# Funding
The fund consists entirely of voluntary contributions from individuals/organisations and does not get any budgetary support. It is exempted from audit by India's Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG). The Prime Minister is the ex-officio Chairman of the PM CARES Fund, with the Minister of Defence, Minister of Home Affairs, and Minister of Finance as ex-officio Trustees.
