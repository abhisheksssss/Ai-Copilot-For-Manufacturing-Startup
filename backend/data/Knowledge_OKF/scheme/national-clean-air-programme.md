# Overview
The National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) was launched by the Ministry of Environment, Forest, and Climate Change in 2019. It aims to improve air quality in 131 cities across 24 states/UTs through a comprehensive strategy involving all stakeholders.

# Eligibility
Eligible cities are non-attainment cities and million plus cities within 24 states/UTs.

# Benefits
The programme targets a 20% to 30% reduction in PM10 and PM2.5 concentrations by 2024, with the new target being up to 40% reduction of PM10 concentration or achieving National Ambient Air Quality standards for PM10 by 2025-2026.

# Funding
The programme involves stringent implementation of mitigation measures, effective ambient air quality monitoring, and public awareness initiatives. Financial support is provided through the allocation of resources to city action plans.
