# Overview
SPARSH is a Social Innovation Programme for Products Affordable & Relevant to Social Health Ideation to Validation under the BIRAC programme. It supports startups and innovators developing affordable products for priority public-health and social needs.

# Eligibility
Startups and innovators working on healthcare/biotech product innovations that are affordable and socially relevant can apply.

# Benefits
Participants receive grant funding of up to ₹50 lakh, subject to call guidelines, along with mentoring and social innovation immersion opportunities.

# Funding
Up to ₹50 lakh is available for eligible projects. The exact amount depends on the specific call guidelines.
