# Overview
The Livestock Security Scheme aims at achieving universal animal vaccination coverage to ensure the security and health of livestock across India.

# Eligibility
All livestock in participating districts are eligible for this scheme, with a focus on ensuring comprehensive vaccination coverage.

# Benefits
The primary benefit is the prevention of diseases through widespread vaccination, thereby enhancing the overall health and productivity of livestock.
