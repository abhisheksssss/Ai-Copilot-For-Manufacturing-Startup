# Overview
ARISE (Accelerating Resilience Investments and Innovations for Sustainable Economies) is a next-generation resilience program launched under the Climate Investment Funds (CIF) to help developing countries strengthen their economic and institutional resilience against climate shocks and transform climate risks into opportunities for sustainable growth.

# Eligibility
The ARISE Program aims to mainstream climate resilience into national economic planning, mobilize catalytic finance for adaptation, and empower communities and institutions to withstand and recover from compounding climate risks like floods, droughts, and storms. The program is targeted at developing countries that are vulnerable to climate shocks.

# Benefits
Countries participating in the ARISE Program will benefit from enhanced resilience against climate-related economic and institutional challenges, as well as opportunities for sustainable growth through innovative investments and transformative climate actions.

# Funding
The ARISE Program was launched with initial funding of $100 million from Germany and Spain. The program is managed by the Climate Investment Funds (CIF), a multilateral climate finance mechanism housed within the World Bank.
