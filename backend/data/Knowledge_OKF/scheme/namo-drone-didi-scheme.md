# Overview
Namo Drone Didi is a central sector scheme to empower women-led Self-Help Groups (SHGs) by equipping them with drone technology to provide agricultural services.

# Eligibility
Women SHGs are eligible for this scheme, and 15,000 selected Women SHGs will be provided drones during the period from 2024-25 to 2025-2026. The drones will be used for providing rental services to farmers for agriculture purposes (application of liquid fertilisers and pesticides at present).

# Benefits
Women SHGs receive substantial financial assistance, covering 80% of the drone and accessory costs, up to Rs. 8 lakhs. For the remaining 20% cost, SHGs can seek loans from the National Agriculture Infrastructure Financing Facility (AIF) with a 3% interest subvention.
