# Overview
E-SHRAM PORTAL is a flagship initiative by the Government of India launched in 2021 to support and empower unorganized workers.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals include those working in sectors such as agriculture, construction, domestic work, and street vending.

# Benefits
- Establishment of a centralized database for effective policy implementation.
- Enhanced access to social security schemes and benefits.
- Facilitation of job matching and skill development opportunities.
- Strengthening labor market resilience by integrating unorganized workers into the formal economy.
- Promotion of financial inclusion through direct benefit transfers and digital payments.
