# Overview
The Prime Minister’s Development Initiative for North East (PM-DEVINE) is a central sector scheme introduced in the Budget of 2022-23, with a duration until 2025-26. It aims to address development gaps in the North Eastern Region (NER).

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text.

# Benefits
The scheme offers several benefits:
- Infrastructure Development aligned with PM GatiShakti.
- Support for social development projects based on local needs.
- Livelihood activities for youth and women.
- Filling gaps in various sectors without substituting existing Central and State Schemes.

# Funding
The budget allocation for the scheme is Rs. 6,600 crore.
