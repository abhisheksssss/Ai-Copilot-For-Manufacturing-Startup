# Overview
The PM-RKVY Agri-Entrepreneurship Programme is a scheme aimed at supporting pre-seed and seed-stage agripreneurs through R-ABIs (Rural Agri-Business Incubators). It provides grant-in-aid, incubation, and mentoring support to agri and allied-sector startups.

# Eligibility
Eligible applicants include individuals or groups who are interested in starting an agripreneurship venture. The programme specifically targets pre-seed and seed-stage agripreneurs through RKVY-supported agri-business incubators.

# Benefits
Participants in the PM-RKVY Agri-Entrepreneurship Programme can benefit from:
- Grant-in-aid of up to ₹5 lakh for pre-seed stage.
- Grant-in-aid of up to ₹25 lakh for seed-stage.

Additionally, they receive support through agri-business incubators and mentoring services.

# Funding
The programme offers financial assistance in the form of grants. The funding amounts are as follows:
- Up to ₹5 lakh for pre-seed stage.
- Up to ₹25 lakh for seed stage.

Funding is provided through RKVY-supported agri-business incubators.
