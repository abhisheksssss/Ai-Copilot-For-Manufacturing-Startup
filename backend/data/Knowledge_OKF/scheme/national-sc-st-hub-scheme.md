# Overview
The National SC-ST Hub Scheme is a government initiative that aims to support SC/ST entrepreneurs through capacity building, procurement linkages, and mentoring. The scheme focuses on MSMEs owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs, including startup MSMEs.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for SC/ST-owned MSMEs and entrepreneurs, including startup MSMEs.

# Benefits
The benefits of the scheme include training, market linkages, vendor development, and subsidy support under various components.

# Funding
The scheme provides a direct grant amount, which varies component-wise.
