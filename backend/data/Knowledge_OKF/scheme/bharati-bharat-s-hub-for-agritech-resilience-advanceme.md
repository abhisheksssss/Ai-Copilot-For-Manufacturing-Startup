# Overview
BHARATI is a scheme initiated by APEDA under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry to support agri-food and agri-tech startups, high-value producers, and tech solution providers aiming for global markets.

# Eligibility
Agri-food and agri-tech startups, high-value producers, and tech solution providers are eligible to participate in this scheme.

# Benefits
Participants receive acceleration, mentoring, compliance, and market-access support.

# Funding
No fixed grant amount is disclosed. The pilot cohort supports 100 startups.
