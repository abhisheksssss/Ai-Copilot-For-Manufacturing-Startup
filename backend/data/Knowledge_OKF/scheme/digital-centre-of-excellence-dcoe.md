# Overview
The Digital Centre of Excellence (DCoE) is a partnership between the Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways and the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing. Its primary purpose is to spearhead digital transformation in maritime logistics by leveraging advanced technologies.

# Benefits
The DCoE offers several benefits including:
- Utilization of AI, IoT, and Blockchain for smart ports.
- Focus on sustainable and green operations.
- Support for real-time port operation upgrades.
