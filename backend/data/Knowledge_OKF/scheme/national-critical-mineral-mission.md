# Overview
The National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM) will be implemented from FY 2024-25 to 2030-31 with a financial outlay of ₹16,300 crore and additional funding support of ₹18,000 crore. The mission focuses on increasing domestic production, acquiring critical mineral assets abroad, recycling minerals, enhancing trade, promoting scientific research, and developing human resources.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text but likely include companies involved in exploration, mining, processing, and recycling of critical minerals.

# Benefits
- Increased domestic production through expanded exploration and mining activities.
- Support for private participation in exploration activities.
- Formation of Critical Mineral Processing Parks.
- Acquisition of critical mineral assets abroad with government support.
- Incentives for setting up mineral recycling facilities.
- Development of national stockpiles and enhancing trade with resource-endowed countries.

# Funding
The mission will have a financial outlay of ₹16,300 crore and an additional funding support of ₹18,000 crore. The funds will be raised from different PSUs and used for various components such as exploration, mining, processing parks, and international acquisitions.
