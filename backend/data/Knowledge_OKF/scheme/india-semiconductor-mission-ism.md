# Overview
A government initiative to develop a robust semiconductor and display manufacturing ecosystem in India.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
- Financial support for fabs, compound semiconductors, ATMP/OSAT units, and display fabs.
- Assistance to startups, MSMEs, and academia in chip design.
- Training over 60,000 skilled professionals.
- Reduces import dependency and supports self-reliance under Atmanirbhar Bharat.

# Funding
Financial support for fabs, compound semiconductors, ATMP/OSAT units, and display fabs is provided.
