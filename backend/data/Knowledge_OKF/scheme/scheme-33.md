### **Atmanirbhar Bharat Package 3.0: Key Highlights**

#### **1. AatmaNirbhar Bharat Rozgar Yojana**
- **Objective:** To incentivize job creation during the COVID-19 recovery.
- **Duration:** Effective from October 1, 2020, and operational till June 30, 2021.
- **Eligibility:**
  - Any EPFO-registered establishment that brings in new employees or employees who have lost their jobs between March 1, 2020, and September 30, 2020.
- **Subsidy:** Subsidy for each new candidate enrolled.

#### **2. Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS)**
- **Objective:** To provide 100% guaranteed coverage to banks, NBFCs, and other lenders to extend emergency credit to businesses/MSMEs hit by the COVID-19 pandemic.
- **Key Features:**
  - **ECLGS 4.0:**
    - Removed cap of Rs 500 crore loan outstanding for eligibility (subject to a maximum additional ECLGS assistance of 40% or Rs 200 crore, whichever is lower).
    - Provided 100% guarantee cover to loans up to Rs 2 crore to hospitals/nursing homes/clinics/medical colleges for setting up on-site oxygen generation plants.
    - Interest rate capped at 7.5%.
    - Additional ECLGS assistance of up to 10% of the outstanding as on February 29, 2020, to borrowers covered under ECLGS 1.0.
    - Civil Aviation sector eligible under ECLGS 3.0.
    - Validity extended to September 30, 2021, or till guarantees for an amount of Rs 3 lakh crore are issued; disbursement permitted up to March 2022.

#### **3. Additional Outlay for PM Awaas Yojana – Urban (PMAY-U)**
- **Objective:** To provide housing for all in urban areas by the year 2022.
- **Outlay:** Rs 18,000 crore provided.
- **Key Features:**
  - Grounds 12 lakh houses and completes 18 lakh houses.
  - Creates additional 78 lakh jobs.
  - Improves production and sale of steel and cement, resulting in a multiplier effect on the economy.

#### **4. Platform for Infra Debt Financing**
- **Objective:** To provide long-term capital to the country’s infrastructure sector through NIIF (National Investment and Infrastructure Fund).
- **Key Features:**
  - Government will make an equity investment of ₹6,000 crore in the debt platform.
  - This will help NIIF provide a debt of ₹1.1 lakh crore for infrastructure projects by 2025.

#### **5. Support for Agriculture**
- **Objective:** To ensure increased supply of fertilizers to farmers.
- **Outlay:** ₹65,000 crore provided.

#### **6. Boost for Rural Employment (PM Garib Kalyan Rozgar Yojana - PMGKRY)**
- **Objective:** To accelerate the rural economy and provide livelihood opportunities in areas/villages affected by returnee migrant workers.
- **Outlay:** ₹10,000 crore provided.

#### **7. Support for Construction & Infrastructure**
- **Key Features:**
  - Relaxation of Earnest Deposit Money & Performance Security on Government Tenders (reduced from 5-10% to 3%) has been given to contractors.
  - Extension to ongoing contracts and Public Sector Enterprises.
  - Provides ease of doing business and relief to contractors.

#### **8. Income Tax Relief for Developers & Home Buyers**
- **Objective:** To provide an incentive to the middle class to buy homes.
- **Key Features:**
  - Differential between circle rate and agreement value in real estate income tax increased from 10% to 20%.

#### **9. Boost for Project Exports (IDEAS)**
- **Objective:** To promote project exports under Indian Development and Economic Assistance Scheme (IDEAS).
- **Outlay:** ₹3,000 crore provided.

#### **10. Capital and Industrial Stimulus**
- **Objective:** To provide additional budget stimulus for capital and industrial expenditure.
- **Outlay:** ₹10,200 crore provided.

#### **11. R&D Grant for COVID Vaccine Development**
- **Objective:** To support the development of an Indian COVID vaccine.
- **Outlay:** ₹900 crore provided to the Department of Biotechnology.
