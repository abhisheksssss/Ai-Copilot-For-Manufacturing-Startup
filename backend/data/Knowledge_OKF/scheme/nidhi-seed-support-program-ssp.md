# Overview
The NIDHI – Seed Support Program (SSP) is a government scheme that provides seed support to incubated startups to help them bridge the prototype to market stage. The scheme is deployed through DST-supported TBIs.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for technology startups incubated in eligible NIDHI/DST-supported TBIs.

# Benefits
The scheme provides seed funding to eligible startups, which can be in the form of debt, equity, or convertible instruments as per the incubator's guidelines.

# Funding
The scheme provides up to ₹1 crore per startup, subject to TBI guidelines.
