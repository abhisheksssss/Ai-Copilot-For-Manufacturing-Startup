# Overview
The VIBRANT VILLAGE PROGRAMME is a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at identifying and developing economic drivers based on local, natural, human, and other resources in border villages along the northern border of India.

# Eligibility
Eligible states include Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, and Ladakh Union Territory.

# Benefits
- Development of essential infrastructure such as all-weather roads, drinking water, round-the-clock electricity using renewable sources (solar and wind energy).
- Creation of livelihood opportunities.
- Promotion of social entrepreneurship and empowerment of youth and women through skill development and entrepreneurship via the "Hub and Spoke Model".
- Promotion of local cultural, traditional knowledge, and heritage through community-based organizations, cooperatives, SHGs, NGOs, etc., under the 'one village-One product' model.
- Development of tourist centers, multi-purpose centers, and health and wellness centers.

# Funding
Funding is provided for development activities as outlined above. There will be no overlap with the Border Area Development Programme.
