# Overview
The Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC) provides grant in aid to Non-Governmental/ Voluntary Organizations for running and maintenance of Senior Citizens' Homes, continuous care homes, etc. Facilities like shelter, nutrition, medicare and entertainments are provided free of cost to indigent senior citizens.

# Eligibility
Indigent senior citizens residing in the homes run by NGOs/VOs can avail the benefits.

# Benefits
Free provision of shelter, nutrition, medicare, and entertainment services for senior citizens.

# Funding
Grant-in-aid is provided through this programme.
