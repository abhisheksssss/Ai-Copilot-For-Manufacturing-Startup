# Overview
The IN-SPACe Seed Fund Scheme is a grant program that encourages startups and non-government entities (NGEs) to develop space technologies and applications.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to Indian space-tech startups and non-government entities that are developing space-sector products and applications.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹1 crore per startup or NGE for research and development (R&D) and prototype development.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Department of Space and provides grant-in-aid for R&D and prototype development.
