# Overview
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) is a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at providing all-weather road connectivity to eligible, previously unconnected rural habitations, thereby integrating villages with markets, schools, and healthcare facilities. The scheme has completed 25 years in December 2025.

# Eligibility
Eligible rural habitations that are not connected by an all-weather road can apply for the PMGSY scheme.

# Benefits
The key benefits of the PMGSY include:
- Universal rural connectivity to unconnected habitations.
- Upgradation and consolidation of existing rural road networks.
- Strengthening through routes and major rural links connecting markets, schools, and health facilities.
- Connectivity to 25,000 habitations via 62,500 km of roads under PMGSY-IV (2024–29).

# Funding
The scheme is centrally sponsored with significant financial support from the Government of India. The funding mechanism includes:
- Phased implementation: PMGSY-I, II, III, and IV.
- Large-scale coverage: Over 8.25 lakh km of roads sanctioned, with nearly 95% completed by December 2025.
- Technology-driven monitoring: Use of OMMAS, e-MARG, GPS-based tracking, and geo-tagged inspections for real-time monitoring and transparency.
- Quality assurance: Institutionalised three-tier quality monitoring system involving executing agencies, State Quality Monitors, and National Quality Monitors.
- Sustainability focus: Adoption of eco-friendly materials like waste plastic, fly ash, bio-bitumen, and climate-resilient construction techniques.
