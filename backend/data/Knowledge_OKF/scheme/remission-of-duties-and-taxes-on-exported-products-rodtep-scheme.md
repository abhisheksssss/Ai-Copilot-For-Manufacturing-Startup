# Overview
The Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP) Scheme is administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade under the Department of Commerce, replacing the Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS). Introduced in 2021, it offers refunds for various duties and taxes levied at central, state, and local levels.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria include sectors such as pharmaceuticals, chemicals, and iron & steel, which were initially excluded due to fiscal constraints but have since been included following a decline in global export demand.

# Benefits
The scheme provides refunds for duties and taxes including:
- Central and State Excise Duty on fuel for transportation of export goods (petrol, diesel, CNG, PNG, etc.)
- Coal cess or duty levied by States on electricity consumed for manufacturing of export goods
- Mandi tax levied by APMCs
- Toll tax and stamp duties on import-export documentation
- Value added tax (VAT) wherever applicable

These refunds are issued in the form of transferrable e-scrips, which can be used to pay Basic Customs Duty on imported goods or transferred electronically to another party.

# Funding
The scheme is funded through the government's budget allocation for export incentives and support.
