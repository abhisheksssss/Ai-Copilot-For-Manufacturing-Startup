# Overview
The Atal Community Innovation Centres (ACICs) scheme is part of the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) programme, which aims to set up community innovation centres in underserved regions. These centres will support community innovators and early-stage startups, providing them with the necessary resources to grow and develop.

# Eligibility
Institutions and organisations can apply to set up community innovation centres under this scheme, with the goal of supporting community innovators and early-stage startups in underserved regions.

# Benefits
The scheme provides grant support to host centres and incubation ecosystems, with funding of up to ₹2.5 crore over a prescribed period. This support will help create a conducive environment for community innovators and startups to thrive.

# Funding
The scheme provides funding of up to ₹2.5 crore over a prescribed period to support the setup and operation of community innovation centres.
