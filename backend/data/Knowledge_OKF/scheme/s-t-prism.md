# Overview
S&T-PRISM is a scheme supporting startups and MSMEs developing innovations in mining, mineral processing, metallurgy and recycling, providing support from prototype to pilot stage.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to early-stage startups in the mining and metals industry.

# Benefits
The scheme provides grant/project support, technical mentoring, and funding of up to ₹2 crore per approved project.

# Funding
The scheme offers a grant of up to ₹2 crore per approved project.
