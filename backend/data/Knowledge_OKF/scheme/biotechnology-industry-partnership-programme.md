# Overview
The Biotechnology Industry Partnership Programme is a partnership programme for high-risk, high-impact industry-led biotech innovation, implemented in collaboration with BIRAC. It aims to support biotech companies, startups, and academia-industry consortia with transformational R&D proposals.

# Eligibility
Biotech companies, startups, and academia-industry consortia with high-risk, high-impact biotech innovation proposals are eligible for the programme.

# Benefits
The programme provides grant, soft loan, or cost-sharing support to selected projects, with the aim of validating and commercialising biotech innovations.

# Funding
The funding for the programme is project-based and varies by approved cost and scheme terms.
