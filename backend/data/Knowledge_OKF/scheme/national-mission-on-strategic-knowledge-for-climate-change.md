# Overview
The National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change (under MoS&T) is a regional project aimed at poor and vulnerable sections of society through an inclusive development strategy. It focuses on achieving national growth by promoting qualitative change, economic direction that enhances ecological sustainability, demand-side management, better technology, and market mechanisms.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
The mission benefits include:
- Reducing stress of climate change through a poverty-growth linkage.
- Enhancing ecosystem services like carbon sequestration and storage.
- Improving hydrological services and biodiversity.
- Increasing forest/tree cover to enhance livelihood income for households.

Funding details are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is part of the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC).
