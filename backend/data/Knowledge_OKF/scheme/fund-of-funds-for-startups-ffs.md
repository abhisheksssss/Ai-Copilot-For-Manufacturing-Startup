# Overview
The Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) is a government-backed fund-of-funds that commits capital to SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs). These AIFs then invest in growth-stage startups indirectly through supported venture capital funds.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it can be inferred that startups must be registered and eligible for investment from SEBI-registered AIFs.

# Benefits
Startups benefit from indirect equity investments via supported venture capital funds, which can provide them with much-needed growth-stage funding.

# Funding
The FFS has a corpus of ₹10,000 crore. The level of startup-level investment varies by the specific AIF involved.
