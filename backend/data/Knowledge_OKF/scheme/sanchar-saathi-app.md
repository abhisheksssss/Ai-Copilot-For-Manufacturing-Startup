# Overview
Sanchar Saathi is a telecom security and citizen-protection platform developed by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Government of India, aimed at safeguarding mobile users through identity management, fraud reporting, device verification, and enhancing awareness on telecom and cyber risks.

# Eligibility
All Indian citizens using mobile services are eligible to use Sanchar Saathi.

# Benefits
Users can benefit from various features such as:
- Fraud & Scam Reporting: Using the Chakshu tool to report fake KYC alerts, impersonation scams, phishing links, and suspicious WhatsApp/SMS messages.
- SIM & Identity Protection: Checking all SIMs/mobile connections linked to their identity to detect unauthorized usage.
- Lost/Stolen Phone Blocking: Blocking lost or stolen devices with IMEI blocking; over 7 lakh devices recovered since launch.
- Device Authenticity Verification: Confirming the authenticity of a handset’s IMEI, crucial for India's large second-hand phone market.
- Reporting Illegally Masked International Calls: Reporting cases where foreign calls appear as +91 numbers to aid anti-fraud investigations.
- Reporting Spam & Malicious Links: Reporting telemarketing spam, unsafe APKs, phishing websites, and fraudulent apps.
- Additional Utilities: Accessing a local ISP locator, trusted helpline directory, and updated awareness material on telecom security.
