# Overview
RASHTRIYA UDYAMITA VIKAS PARIYOJANA (RYVP) is a scheme aimed at equipping individuals with comprehensive entrepreneurship training, creating job providers rather than job seekers. It partners with Flipkart and focuses on reskilling and upskilling employees to enhance their competitiveness and adaptability.

# Eligibility
The scheme is tailored specifically for beneficiaries of the PM SVANidhi scheme.

# Benefits
- Comprehensive entrepreneurship training over a period of 22 weeks.
- Training conducted through offline, online, and hybrid modes with certificates awarded.
- Under the pilot program, street vendors of 10 big cities will be given a stipend along with training to expand their businesses.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the government in partnership with private sector entities like Flipkart.
