# Overview
India Rankings is an annual ranking of higher education institutions based on the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), introduced in 2015. It covers universities, colleges, and specialized institutions across disciplines.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text but typically include all recognized higher education institutions within India.

# Benefits
The scheme aims to promote accountability, transparency, and quality benchmarking among Higher Education Institutions (HEIs). It also guides students, parents, and policymakers with credible performance indicators and aligns higher education with NEP 2020 goals and India’s vision of becoming a knowledge superpower by 2047.
