# Overview
Ayushman Bharat is a flagship scheme of the Government of India launched as recommended by the National Health Policy 2017, to achieve Universal Health Coverage (UHC). It comprises two inter-related components.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is designed to provide health cover to families across India.

# Benefits
The scheme offers a health cover of Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across public and private empanelled hospitals in India.

Funding details are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text, but it is implied that the scheme involves significant government investment to support both Health and Wellness Centers (HWCs) and Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY).
