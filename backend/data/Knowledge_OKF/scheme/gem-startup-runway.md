# Overview
GeM Startup Runway is a dedicated marketplace window on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) that enables startups to sell innovative products and services directly to government buyers.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to DPIIT-recognised startups, as well as all other startups for listing.

# Benefits
The scheme provides exemption from prior experience, turnover, and Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) requirements, and offers a platform for showcasing innovations for public procurement.
