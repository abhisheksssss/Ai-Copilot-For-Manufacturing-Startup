# Overview
AgriSURE – Agri Fund for Startups & Rural Enterprises Scaling is a scheme jointly sponsored by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoAFW) and NABARD, aimed at catalysing equity/debt capital for agri-tech, rural, and allied-sector ventures.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include agri-tech startups, rural enterprises, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPO)-linked innovations, and value-chain ventures in agriculture.

# Benefits
The scheme provides direct equity up to ₹25 crore per startup. The fund corpus is ₹750 crore.

# Funding
The funding mechanism includes both direct equity and through the fund route for equity/debt/blended capital.
