# Overview
The Small Business Innovation Research Initiative (SBIRI) is a public-private partnership scheme that supports early and late-stage biotech product development, undertaken by biotech startups, SMEs, and industry-led consortia.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to biotech startups, SMEs, and industry-led consortia undertaking innovative R&D.

# Benefits
The scheme provides financial support in the form of grants, with early-stage support commonly up to ₹50 lakh grant, and larger support as per call.

# Funding
The funding for the scheme is provided by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) / BIRAC, and includes grant-in-aid, soft loan, and cost-sharing support.
