### Government Schemes for Agriculture and Farmers

#### **1. Agri-Market Infrastructure Fund (AMIF)**
- **Objective**: Develop and upgrade agricultural marketing infrastructure.
- **Corpus**: Rs.2000 crore with NABARD.
- **Aim**: Provide financial assistance for:
  - Upgradation of Rural Haats to Gramin Agriculture Markets (GrAMs).
  - Creation of electronic display mechanisms.
  - Linking GrAMs with APMCs.
  - Upgradation of 585 e-NAM enabled APMCs.
- **Eligibility**: State Governments can avail loans through NABARD.

#### **2. Gramin Agriculture Markets (GrAMs)**
- **Objective**: Develop retail markets in agriculture marketing sectors.
- **Infrastructure**: Strengthened using MGNREGS and other government schemes.
- **Benefits**:
  - Electronically linked to e-NAM.
  - Exempt from APMC regulations.
  - Facilitate direct sale to consumers and bulk purchasers.

#### **3. Agricultural Market Information Network (AGMARKNET) Portal**
- **Objective**: Provide agricultural marketing-related information through a single window.
- **Features**:
  - Web-based information flow of daily arrivals and prices in markets across the country.
  - Facilitates communication between farmers, industry, policy makers, and academic institutions.

#### **4. E-Krishi Samvad**
- **Objective**: Provide effective solutions to farmers through ICAR specialists.
- **Features**:
  - Online interface for direct interaction with ICAR.
  - Instant diagnostics and remedial measures via SMS or web.

#### **5. e-Rashtriya Kishan Agri Mandi (e-RAKAM)**
- **Objective**: Help farmers get reasonable prices through an auction platform.
- **Features**:
  - Direct payment into bank accounts of farmers.
  - Focus on farmer’s Farm, Innovations, Resources, Science and Technology (FIRST).
  - Enriching farmer-scientist interface.

#### **6. Farmer FIRST (FARM, Innovations, Resources, Science and Technology) Initiative**
- **Objective**: Enhance earnings for farmers through technology and innovation.
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Enriching farmer-scientist interface.
  - Technology assemblage, application, and feedback.
  - Partnership and institutional building.
  - Content mobilization.

#### **7. Hortinet – Farmer Connect App**
- **Objective**: Provide traceability for export of agricultural products to the EU.
- **Features**:
  - Internet-based electronic services for farm registration, testing, and certification.
  - Capture real-time details from farms directly by state departments.

#### **8. Zero Hunger Program**
- **Objective**: Address intergenerational and multifaceted malnutrition through sectoral coordination.
- **Approach**: Integrated approach to deal with hunger and malnutrition.

#### **9. Meghdoot App**
- **Objective**: Provide weather-based agro advisories in local languages.
- **Features**:
  - Launched by Ministry of Agriculture and Earth Sciences.
  - Updated twice a week on Tuesdays and Fridays.

#### **10. AGRI UDAAN**
- **Objective**: Scale-up food & agribusiness start-ups through rigorous mentoring, training, industry networking, and investor pitching.
- **Details**:
  - First Food & Agribusiness accelerator by ICAR-NAARM and IIM-A’s incubator centres.

#### **11. MERA GAON-MERA GAURAV**
- **Objective**: Provide technical information to farmers through multidisciplinary scientists.
- **Implementation**: ICAR and state agricultural universities.
- **Details**:
  - Each group of 4 scientists will select 5 villages within a radius of 100 km.

#### **12. Unified Package Insurance Scheme (UPIS)**
- **Objective**: Provide insurance-based financial protection to farmers in the agriculture sector.
- **Coverage**:
  - Crop insurance, personal accident insurance, life insurance, student safety insurance, and asset insurance.
- **Duration**: One-year cover, renewable annually.

#### **13. Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyay Unnat Krishi Shiksha Yojana (PDDUUKSY)**
- **Objective**: Provide professional support in organic farming, natural farming, rural economy, and sustainable agriculture.
- **Implementation**: ICAR since 2016.

#### **14. Centralized Farm Machinery Performance Testing Portal**
- **Objective**: Facilitate seamless application, communication, and monitoring of testing for agricultural machinery.
- **Features**:
  - Integrated management within organizations to improve efficiency in testing institutes.

#### **15. Horticulture Cluster Development Programme (CDP)**
- **Objective**: Ensure holistic growth of horticulture clusters.
- **Details**:
  - Pilot phase in 12 out of 53 clusters covering nearly 10 lakh
