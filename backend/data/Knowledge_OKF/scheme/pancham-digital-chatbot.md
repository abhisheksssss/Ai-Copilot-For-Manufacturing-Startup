# Overview
PANCHAM (Panchayat Assistance and Messaging Chatbot) is a WhatsApp-based AI tool designed as a digital companion for rural local bodies, eliminating the need for complex apps or middlemen.

# Eligibility
Sarpanches and ward members of rural local bodies are eligible to use PANCHAM.

# Benefits
- Direct Connectivity: Linking the Government of India directly with village-level decision-makers.
- Information Saturation: Ensuring 100% awareness of central and state welfare schemes.
- Feedback Loops: Creating a real-time channel for field-level issues to reach senior policymakers.
