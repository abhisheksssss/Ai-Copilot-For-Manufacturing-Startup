# Overview
The Stand Up India scheme is a credit support bank loan scheme for greenfield enterprises promoted by SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs. The scheme aims to provide loans to at least one SC/ST borrower or one woman borrower per bank branch for greenfield non-farm enterprises.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs who are promoting greenfield non-farm enterprises.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a composite bank loan of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance.
