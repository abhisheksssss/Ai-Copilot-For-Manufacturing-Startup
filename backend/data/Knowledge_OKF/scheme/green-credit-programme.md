# Overview
The GREEN CREDIT PROGRAMME envisions the issue of Green Credits for plantations on waste/degraded lands and river catchment areas, to rejuvenate and revive natural eco-systems. This fully digital and tradable instrument is part of the government's broader Lifestyle for Environment or LIFE Movement.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include individuals, industries, and local bodies that can engage in specific environmental activities such as tree plantation, water conservation, sustainable agriculture, waste management, air pollution reduction, mangrove conservation and restoration, Eco Mark (a government scheme for identifying environmentally friendly products), and sustainable building and infrastructure.

# Benefits
Participants earn green credits by undertaking these activities. These credits function as tradable commodities that can be sold on a domestic market platform. The program incentivizes sustainable actions across various sectors.

# Funding
The program is administered by the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE), which involves defining methodologies for calculating green credits and managing a trading platform for credit exchange.
