# Overview
Viksit Bharat 2047 is a vision introduced by the Government of India aiming to transform the country into a developed and self-reliant nation by the year 2047, marking the centenary of India's Independence.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. The scheme targets various sectors including economic growth, human resource development, infrastructure, sustainable growth, good governance, inclusive growth, and global leadership.

# Benefits
- Economic growth and self-reliance through 'Make in India' and Atma Nirbhar Bharat schemes.
- Human resource development with National Education Policy 2020 and PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana for skill training.
- Infrastructure development under the Smart City initiative, covering physical, social, and digital infrastructure.
- Sustainable growth by reducing carbon emissions through alternative energy sources to achieve carbon neutrality by 2070.
- Promoting good governance with administrative reforms using Mission Karmyogi and e-governance.
- Fostering inclusive growth by raising women's participation in the economy and ensuring equitable growth across regions.
- Robust global leadership by strengthening India's position in organizations like the UN, G20, and BRICS.
