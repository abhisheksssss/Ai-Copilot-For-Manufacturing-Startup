# Overview
PRAGATI is a centralised, ICT-enabled governance platform for grievance redressal, programme implementation, and project monitoring, enabling real-time review of projects of national importance.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it primarily targets infrastructure and development projects across various ministries and states.

# Benefits
- Ensures timely implementation of infrastructure and development projects.
- Resolves inter-ministerial and Centre–State coordination issues.
- Promotes e-transparency, accountability, and outcome-based governance.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. PRAGATI is a platform that facilitates project monitoring and coordination rather than providing direct funding.
