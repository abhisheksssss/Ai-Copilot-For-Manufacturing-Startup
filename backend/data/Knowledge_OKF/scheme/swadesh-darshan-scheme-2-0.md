# Overview
SWADESH DARSHAN SCHEME 2.0 is a Central sector scheme launched in 2014-15, aimed at enhancing tourist attractiveness through sustainable development of world-class infrastructure in circuit/destinations.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include State Governments, UT Administrations, and Central Agencies for tourism infrastructure development.

# Benefits
The scheme focuses on developing theme-based circuits such as Culture and Heritage, Adventure Tourism, Eco-Tourism, Wellness Tourism, MICE Tourism, Rural Tourism, Beach Tourism, and Cruises – Ocean & Inland. It also aims to achieve convergence with other schemes and leverage voluntary funding from Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives.

# Funding
The scheme is 100% funded by the Central government through financial assistance provided for tourism infrastructure development. State/UT governments are responsible for Operation & Maintenance (O&M) of sanctioned projects.
