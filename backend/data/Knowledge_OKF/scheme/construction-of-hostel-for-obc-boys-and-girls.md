# Overview
The scheme aims to construct hostels for students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC), both boys and girls, from rural areas. This initiative seeks to facilitate their access to secondary and higher education in government schools, universities, institutions, and institutes located within a reasonable distance.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates are those who belong to socially and educationally backward classes, specifically from rural backgrounds.

# Benefits
The scheme provides hostel facilities to enable students to pursue their education without the constraints of distance. This includes accommodation, meals, and other amenities necessary for their stay during educational pursuits.
