# Overview
The Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana was launched in February 2025 with the aim of enhancing agricultural productivity through the convergence of existing schemes and specialized measures. The scheme will be implemented in partnership with states, focusing on 100 districts with low crop productivity, moderate crop intensity, and below-average credit parameters.

# Eligibility
The scheme benefits 1.7 crore farmers spread across targeted districts.

# Benefits
- Enhanced agricultural productivity.
- Adoption of crop diversification and sustainable agriculture practices.
- Augmentation of post-harvest storage at the panchayat and block level.
- Improved irrigation facilities.
- Facilitation of long-term and short-term credit availability.

# Funding
- Rs 1,000 crore for the mission on pulses.
- Rs 500 crore for the mission on vegetables and fruits.
- Rs 100 crore support for the Makhana Board.
- Rs 100 crore for the mission on hybrid seeds.
- Rs 500 crore for the cotton technology mission.
