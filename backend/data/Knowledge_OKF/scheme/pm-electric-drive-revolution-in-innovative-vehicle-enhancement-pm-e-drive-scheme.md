# Overview
The PM E-Drive Scheme is a government initiative under the Ministry of Heavy Industries with a total outlay of Rs 10,900 crore over two years to promote electric vehicles (EVs).

# Eligibility
Eligible vehicles include e-2Ws, e-3Ws, and e-buses. Applicants must have signed and Aadhaar authenticated e-vouchers generated on the scheme portal for claiming incentives.

# Benefits
The scheme offers various benefits such as:
- Subsidies/demand incentives for e-2Ws, e-3Ws, and e-buses.
- Promotion of comfortable patient transport through e-Ambulances.
- Procurement of 14,028 e-buses for State Transport Undertakings (STUs) or public transport agencies. Demand aggregation will be managed by CESL in cities with populations over 40 lakh.
- Incentives provided to e-Trucks with a scrapping certificate from MoRTH approved RVSFs.
- Installation of fast chargers for e-4Ws, e-buses, and e-2W/3Ws at public charging stations (EVPCS).
- Modernization of testing agencies to handle new and emerging EV technologies.

# Funding
The scheme has a total outlay of Rs 10,900 crore over two years. Specific funding details for each component are not provided in the given text.
