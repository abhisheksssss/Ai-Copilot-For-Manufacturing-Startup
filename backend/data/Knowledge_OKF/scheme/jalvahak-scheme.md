# Overview
The JALVAHAK SCHEME is a three-year initiative by the Ministry of Shipping, Ports, and Inland Waterways to provide financial incentives for shifting cargo from road and rail to inland waterways, inspired by Europe's Marco Polo initiative.

# Eligibility
Financial incentives are provided directly to cargo owners who shift their cargo from road/rail to inland waterways. The incentive is applicable only on long haul movements of more than 300 km. Currently, the scheme covers National Waterways 1 (river Ganga), National Waterways 2 (Brahmaputra river), and National Waterways 16 (River Barak).

# Benefits
Cargo owners can receive up to 35% of the total actual operating expenditure incurred on waterways as an incentive.

# Funding
The financial incentives are provided directly by the Inland and Coastal Shipping Limited (ICSL) based on the cargo owner's shift towards inland waterways.
