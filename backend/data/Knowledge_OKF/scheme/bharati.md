# Overview
BHARATI stands for Bharat's Hub for Agritech, Resilience, Advancement & Incubation for Export Innovation Market Access. It is an APEDA export-readiness accelerator for agri-food and agri-tech startups targeting global markets.

# Eligibility
Agri-food and agri-tech startups, high-value producers, and tech solution providers for exports are eligible for BHARATI.

# Benefits
The benefits of BHARATI include export acceleration, mentoring, compliance support, and market-access support.

# Funding
The pilot cohort of BHARATI supports 100 startups, but no fixed grant has been disclosed.
