# Overview
The Export Promotion Mission (EPM) is a mission-mode, unified national framework established in the Union Budget 2025–26 to strengthen India’s export competitiveness by integrating financial support, market access, compliance readiness, and digital governance for exporters. The mission period runs from FY 2025–26 to FY 2030–31 and is implemented by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) as the nodal implementing agency.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include MSMEs, first-time exporters, labour-intensive sectors, and non-traditional districts. The scheme aims to ensure affordable trade finance and global market readiness for these categories.

# Benefits
The EPM offers a range of benefits through its two integrated sub-schemes:
- **Niryat Protsahan (Financial enablers):** Interest subvention on pre/post-shipment credit, export factoring, deep-tier financing, collateral support, e-commerce exporter credit cards.
- **Niryat Disha (Non-financial enablers):** Quality certification, branding, trade fairs, logistics & warehousing support, inland transport reimbursement, cluster and district capacity building.

# Funding
Key funding mechanisms include:
- Interest subvention under Niryat Protsahan: RBI-guided interest support on export credit for eligible exporters.
- Credit guarantee support via the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Exporters (CGSE) with ₹20,000 crore provided by NCGTC, offering 100% government-backed collateral-free credit.
- Digital implementation through an end-to-end DGFT digital platform aligned with customs and trade systems for transparent, paperless processing.
- RBI regulatory support including extended export credit tenure, moratoriums, asset-classification forbearance, and FEMA relaxations on export realisation.
