# Overview
Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA) is a nationwide anti-drug initiative launched on 15 August 2020 by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE). The scheme aims to reduce substance abuse through prevention, awareness, treatment, and rehabilitation, focusing on India’s most vulnerable districts.

# Eligibility
The scheme targets individuals residing in 272 high-risk districts identified via national surveys and NCB data. It also involves community members, youth, women, and local institutions across these districts.

# Benefits
NMBA offers several benefits including:
- Curb Drug Demand: Reduction of substance use through prevention, education, and outreach.
- Strengthen Community Response: Involvement of youth, women, and local institutions in anti-drug campaigns.
- Rehabilitation & Treatment: Timely support for victims of addiction.

# Funding
The scheme integrates a three-pronged approach:
- Supply reduction (National Crime Branch - NCB)
- Demand reduction (Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment outreach)
- Treatment (Health Department)

Key features include the use of technology such as an NMBA app, website, and active social media campaigns for outreach. Additionally, mass mobilization efforts involve partnerships with organizations like Art of Living, Brahma Kumaris, and ISKCON.
