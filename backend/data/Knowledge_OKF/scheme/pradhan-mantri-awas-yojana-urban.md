# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA (URBAN) was launched in 2015 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA). The scheme aims to provide all weather pucca houses to eligible beneficiaries in urban areas through States/UTs/Central Nodal Agencies. It covers the entire urban area, including statutory towns as per Census 2011 and towns notified subsequently.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries include Economically Weaker Section (EWS)/Low Income Group (LIG) and Middle Income Group (MIG), including slum dwellers.

# Benefits
- **BLC (Beneficiary Led Construction/ Enhancement)**: Subsidy for beneficiary-led individual house construction/enhancement.
- **AHP (Affordable Housing in Partnership)**: Assistance to eligible beneficiaries of EWS/LIG and MIG on home loans from banks.
- **ISSR (In-situ Slum Redevelopment)**: Promotion of affordable housing through Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, with participation of private developers using land as a source.
- **CLSS (Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme)**: Assistance to eligible families belonging to EWS categories who do not have land.

# Funding
- **GoI Grant for BLC**: Rs. 1 Lakh per house.
- **Benefits under CLSS**: Upto Rs.2.67 lakh through interest subsidy of 3-6.5%.
- **GoI Grant for AHP and ISSR**: Rs. 1.5 Lakhs per house.

# Related Schemes
- Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHCs) for Migrant Workers/Urban Poor: Sub-scheme under PMAY-U to provide ease of living to urban migrants/poor.
- CLSS Awas Portal (CLAP): Common platform integrating all stakeholders in real-time, facilitating application processing and subsidy tracking by beneficiaries.
- ANGIKAAR Campaign: Focuses on best practices such as water & energy conservation, waste management, health, sanitation, and hygiene for PMAY(U) beneficiaries.
- Technology Sub Mission (TSM): Facilitates adoption of modern, innovative, and green technologies for faster and quality construction.
- Global Housing Technology Challenge - India: Identifies and mainstreams sustainable, eco-friendly, and disaster-resilient construction technologies.
