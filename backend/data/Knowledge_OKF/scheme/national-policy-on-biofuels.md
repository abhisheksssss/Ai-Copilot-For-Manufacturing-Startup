# Overview
The National Policy on Biofuels was notified in 2018 with the objective of promoting the production, usage, and blending of biofuels to reduce dependence on fossil fuels, lower emissions, and boost the rural economy.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, entities involved in biofuel production, such as farmers, industries, and research institutions, may be eligible for benefits under this policy.

# Benefits
The policy offers several benefits including:
- Advanced targets for ethanol blending: The target for 20% ethanol blending in petrol was advanced to 2025-26 from the original 2030.
- Expanded feedstock scope: The policy now includes surplus food grains, sugarcane, and forestry residues as potential biofuel feedstocks.
- Encouragement of Second Generation (2G) ethanol plants: There is a push for efficient utilization of agricultural waste through the establishment of 2G ethanol plants.
