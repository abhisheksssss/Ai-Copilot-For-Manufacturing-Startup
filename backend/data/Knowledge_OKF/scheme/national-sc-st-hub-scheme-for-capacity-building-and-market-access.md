# Overview
The National SC-ST Hub Scheme for Capacity Building and Market Access is designed to support Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) entrepreneurs, particularly those in Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), through capacity building, procurement linkages, and mentoring.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include SC/ST-owned MSMEs and entrepreneurs, including startup MSMEs.

# Benefits
The scheme offers training, market linkages, vendor development, and subsidy support under various components.

# Funding
Component-wise direct grant amounts vary based on the specific needs of the beneficiaries.
