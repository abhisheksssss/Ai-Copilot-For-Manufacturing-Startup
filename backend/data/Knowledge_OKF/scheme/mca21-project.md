# Overview
MCA21 is the first Mission Mode e-Governance project of the Government of India, aimed at providing easy and secure access to MCA services for corporate entities, professionals, and citizens.

# Eligibility
Eligible corporate entities, professionals, and citizens can benefit from this scheme.

# Benefits
- Strengthened enforcement
- Promotion of ease of doing business
- Enhanced user experience
- Additional modules for e-adjudication, e-consultation, and compliance management system
- MCA Lab with corporate law experts to ensure correctness of results
- Cognitive chatbot-enabled helpdesk, mobile apps, interactive user dashboards
