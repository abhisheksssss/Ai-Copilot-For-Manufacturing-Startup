# Overview
Apna Ghar Initiative is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas to provide rest facilities for truck drivers along national and state highways, addressing fatigue, hygiene, and well-being during long-haul journeys.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals include truck drivers who operate on major highways in India.

# Benefits
Truck drivers can access various amenities such as dormitories, clean toilets and bathing areas (Houdas), self-cooking and dining zones, restaurants/dhabas, and purified drinking water. The scheme also includes a mobile app for booking, feedback, and driver engagement.

# Funding
The initiative is operated at fuel retail outlets by Public Sector Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) under public-private implementation.
