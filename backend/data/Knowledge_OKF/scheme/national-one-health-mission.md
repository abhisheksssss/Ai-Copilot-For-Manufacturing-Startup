# Overview
The National One Health Mission was launched in 2022 to develop strategies for integrated disease surveillance and joint outbreak response, coordinated research and development (R&D), and ensure seamless information sharing for better control of routine diseases as well as those of a pandemic nature. It aims to institutionalize the One Health approach across multiple sectors.

# Benefits
The mission benefits include:
- Developing strategies for integrated disease surveillance.
- Joint outbreak response and coordinated R&D.
- Enhanced preparedness against emerging diseases through strong R&D efforts.
- Mainstreaming approaches like genomic surveillance from wastewater to broader sets of diseases.
