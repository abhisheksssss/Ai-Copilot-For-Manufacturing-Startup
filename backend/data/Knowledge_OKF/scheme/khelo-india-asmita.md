# Overview
Khelo India ASMITA is a dedicated affirmative action sports initiative under the Khelo India programme launched in 2025–26, with the inaugural football league in Jalgaon, Maharashtra. It focuses on increasing women's participation in sports, particularly among underrepresented communities.

# Eligibility
The target group includes girls aged 13 and under, including first-time players and hidden talent from marginalised communities such as tribal, rural, and minority backgrounds.

# Benefits
- Provides a dedicated platform for young girls to showcase and develop sporting skills.
- Addresses historical gender imbalances in sports participation.
- Fosters social inclusion by engaging talent from diverse backgrounds.
- Contributes to nation-building through women's empowerment in sports.

# Funding
Supported by the Sports Authority of India (SAI), All India Football Federation (AIFF), and Western India Football Association (WIFA).
