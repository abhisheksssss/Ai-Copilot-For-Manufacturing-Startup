# Overview
The Maulana Azad National Fellowship is a scheme launched in 2009 by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, implemented through the University Grants Commission (UGC). It aims to provide educational empowerment to students from six notified minority communities.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries include students belonging to Buddhist, Christian, Jain, Muslim, Parsi, and Sikh communities. The fellowship is available for those pursuing regular and full-time research studies leading to M.Phil/Ph.D. degrees within India only.

# Benefits
The fellowship provides a monthly stipend of INR 31,000 for the first two years (Junior Research Fellowship) and INR 35,000 per month thereafter (Senior Research Fellowship). The duration varies from two years for M.Phil students to five years for Ph.D. students.

# Funding
The fellowship is funded by the Government of India through the University Grants Commission (UGC).
