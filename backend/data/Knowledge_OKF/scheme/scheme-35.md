### **Operation Greens**

**Objective:**
- Enhance value realization of top farmers by targeted interventions to strengthen TOP production clusters and their Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs).
- Price stabilization for producers and consumers through proper production planning in the TOP clusters.
- Reduce post-harvest losses by creating farm gate infrastructure, developing suitable agro-logistics, and increasing shelf life.
- Increase food processing capacities and value addition in TOP (Tomato, Potato, Onion) value chain with farm linkages.
- Establish a market intelligence network to collect real-time data on demand, supply, and prices of TOP crops.

**Eligible Organizations:**
- State Agriculture and other Marketing Federations
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Cooperatives
- Companies
- Self-help groups
- Food processors
- Logistic operators
- Service providers
- Supply chain operators
- Retail and wholesale chains
- Central and state governments and their entities/organizations

**Financial Assistance:**
- Rs. 50 crores per project (70% grant-in-aid for FPOs)

**Salient Features:**

1. **Market Intelligence and Early Warning System (MIEWS) Portal:**
   - Provides real-time monitoring of prices of TOP crops.
   - Generates alerts for timely market interventions to stabilize prices during a glut.

2. **Indo-Israel Cooperation:**
   - Demonstration of new technologies in production.
   - Supply of quality planting material.
   - Capacity building for farmers under the scheme.

3. **Extension to Other Crops (December 2020):**
   - Extended from TOP crops to other notified horticulture crops (TOP to Total).
   - Aimed at protecting growers from distress sales due to lockdowns and reducing post-harvest losses.

4. **Eligible Crops:**
   - Fruits: Mango, Banana, Guava, Kiwi, Lichi, Papaya, Citrus, Pineapple, Pomegranate, Jackfruit.
   - Vegetables: French beans, Bitter Gourd, Brinjal, Capsicum, Carrot, Cauliflower, Chillies (Green), Okra, Onion, Potato, and Tomato.

5. **Duration of Scheme:**
   - Six months from the date of notification (11/06/2020).

6. **Pattern of Assistance:**
   - Subsidy @ 50% of the cost for transportation and storage.
   - Maximum period of 3 months for hiring appropriate storage facilities.

### **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Sampada Yojana (PMKSY)**

**Objective:**
- Supplement agriculture, modernize processing, and decrease agri-waste.

**Eligible Organizations:**
- Farmer groups
- Self-help groups
- Individual farmers
- Primary Processing Centers (PPCs)
- Central Processing Centers (CPCs)

**Salient Features:**

1. **Component Schemes:**
   - Mega Food Parks
   - Integrated Cold Chain, Value Addition and Preservation Infrastructure
   - Creation/Expansion of Food Processing/Preservation Capacities (CEFPPC)
   - Food Safety & Quality Assurance Infrastructure
   - Infrastructure for Agro Processing Clusters

2. **Human Resources and Institutions:**
   - Demand-driven Research & Development (R&D), promotional activities, and sector-specific skill development.

3. **Duration of Scheme:**
   - Extended to 2025-26.

### **Miscellaneous Initiatives**

1. **Nivesh Bandhu:**
   - Investor facilitation portal providing information on investor-friendly policies, agro-producing clusters, infrastructure, and potential areas for investment in the food processing sector.

2. **Scheme of Cold Chain, Value Addition & Preservation Infrastructure:**
   - Aimed at reducing post-harvest losses by providing integrated cold chain facilities from farm gate to consumer.
   - Components include processing centers, distribution hubs, mobile pre-cooling vans, refrigerated trucks, and irradiation facility.

3. **Eat Right Station:**
   - Chandigarh Railway Station received the 'Eat Right Station' certification as part of FSSAI's 'Eat Right India' movement.
   - Promotes food safety and hygiene at static catering units in railway stations.

These schemes collectively aim to enhance agricultural productivity, reduce post-harvest losses, increase value addition, and ensure better market access for farmers.
