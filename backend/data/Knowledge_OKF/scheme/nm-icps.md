# Overview
The NM-ICPS scheme is part of the government's initiatives to support startups, including the GeM Startup Runway, International Cooperation Scheme, and BHARATI.

# Eligibility
Information on eligibility for the NM-ICPS scheme is not provided in the given text.

# Benefits
The scheme provides various benefits to startups, including access to funding opportunities through initiatives such as iDEX, ADITI, TDF, IN-SPACe Seed Fund, and NQM.

# Funding
The NM-ICPS scheme offers funding opportunities to startups through various initiatives, including those focused on defence, space, quantum, agriculture, telecom, semiconductor, biotech, energy, livestock, and rural development.

# Related Schemes
Related schemes include iDEX, ADITI, TDF, IN-SPACe Seed Fund, NQM, RKVY, AgriSURE, DCIS, DLI, Chips to Startup, BioNEST, MAHIR, NLM, and SVEP.
