# Overview
The FIRST OVERSEAS ATAL INNOVATION CENTRE is a scheme aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship among the Indian diaspora by providing them with resources, support, and networking opportunities.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants include individuals of Indian origin residing outside India who are engaged in or interested in starting innovative ventures.

# Benefits
Participants can benefit from mentorship, funding, access to technology, and a platform for collaboration and networking.

# Funding
The scheme offers financial assistance and grants to support the development and commercialization of innovative ideas and startups.
