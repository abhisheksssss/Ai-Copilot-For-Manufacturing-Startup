# Overview
The International Cooperation Scheme - Market Access supports Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), industry associations, and startup MSMEs in participating in international exhibitions, trade fairs, and buyer-seller meets.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include:
- MSMEs
- Industry Associations
- Startup MSMEs

These entities are seeking export/market linkages.

# Benefits
The scheme provides assistance for global exposure to MSMEs. The type of assistance varies by event category and guidelines.

# Funding
Funding is provided in the form of reimbursement/subsidy for:
- Travel expenses
- Stall costs
- Registration fees
- Event-related costs
