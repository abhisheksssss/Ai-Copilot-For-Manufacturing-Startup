# Overview
The Space Technology Adoption Fund (TAF) aims to move space technologies from lab validation towards commercialisation and adoption. It supports space startups, industry, and NGOs that are advancing space technologies and applications.

# Eligibility
Space startups, industry, and NGOs advancing space technologies and applications are eligible for the TAF.

# Benefits
The TAF provides grant/funding support for technology development and commercialisation.

# Funding
The TAF has a corpus of ₹500 crore, with project-level funding varying.
