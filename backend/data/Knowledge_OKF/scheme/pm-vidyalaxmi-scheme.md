# Overview
The PM VIDYALAXMI SCHEME is designed to provide financial assistance to meritorious students studying in top higher education institutions across India.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates must be pursuing their studies at one of the following institutions:
- Top 100 Higher Education Institutions (both Government and Private) ranked under the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).
- State Government Higher Education Institutions ranked between 101 to 200 in NIRF.
- All Central Government Institutions, which include approximately 660 institutions.

# Benefits
The scheme offers financial assistance through loans that are facilitated via a digital portal. This portal simplifies the application process and enables students to track their loan status efficiently.

# Funding
Students can apply for loans through the Vidyalaxmi portal, which is integrated with major public and private banks. The portal streamlines the application process and ensures transparency in loan tracking.
