# Overview
The NIDHI – Centres of Excellence (Incubation Excellence) scheme is a DST initiative aimed at supporting high-performing incubators to transform into Centers of Excellence and scale deep startup support.

# Eligibility
Mature incubators with a strong track record are eligible for this scheme. Startups benefit indirectly from the enhanced services provided by these incubators.

# Benefits
The scheme offers large incubator grants, infrastructure development, seed/scale funding, and networking opportunities to foster innovation and entrepreneurship.

# Funding
Up to ₹50 crore over 5 years is allocated to selected incubators for their growth and expansion.
