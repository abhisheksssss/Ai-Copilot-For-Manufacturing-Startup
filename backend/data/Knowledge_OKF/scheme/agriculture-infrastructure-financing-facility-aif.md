# Overview
The Agriculture Infrastructure Financing Facility (AIF) is a government scheme that provides scaling medium to long-term debt financing for post-harvest and community farming assets.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for farmers, FPOs, agri-entrepreneurs, startups, cooperatives, and related entities.

# Benefits
The scheme offers loans up to ₹2 crore eligible for 3% interest subvention, with a scheme corpus of ₹1 lakh crore. It also provides interest subvention and credit guarantee support on loans.

# Funding
The scheme provides loans up to ₹2 crore with a 3% interest subvention.
