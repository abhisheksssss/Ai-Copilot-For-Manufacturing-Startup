# Overview
The Chips to Startup (C2S) programme is an R&D to prototype initiative by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) for chip design, VLSI training, and development of working silicon prototypes.

# Eligibility
The programme is open to academic institutions, startups/MSMEs, and R&D organisations in the field of semiconductor design.

# Benefits
The benefits of the programme include design tools, mentoring, prototyping, and project support for the development of semiconductor design talent and startups.

# Funding
The funding for the programme varies under approved C2S projects, with support provided on a project/institution-based basis, including incubation.
