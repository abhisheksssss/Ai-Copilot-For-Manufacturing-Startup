# Overview
The Post-Matric Scholarship is a government scheme aimed at providing financial support to students who are studying post-matriculation or post-secondary education.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the scholarship include students pursuing post-matriculation studies. The exact eligibility requirements may vary, but generally, it is awarded based on academic performance and financial need.

# Benefits
The Post-Matric Scholarship offers an academic allowance ranging from Rs. 5000 to Rs. 20000 per annum, depending on the category of course pursued by the student.

# Funding
The funding for this scholarship comes directly from the government budget allocated to the Ministry of Human Resource Development.
