# Overview
The Established Incubation Centres (EICs) scheme provides support for established incubators to scale their startup support capabilities.

# Eligibility
Existing incubators and institutions supporting startups are eligible for the scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹10 crore, with a matching private contribution required.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog, and requires a matching private contribution.
