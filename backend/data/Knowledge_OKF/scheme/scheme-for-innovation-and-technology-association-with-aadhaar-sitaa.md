# Overview
The Scheme for Innovation and Technology Association with Aadhaar (SITAA) is an initiative by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) aimed at fostering innovation in biometric, AI, and cybersecurity technologies to secure Aadhaar-based authentication systems against emerging digital threats.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants include startups, academia, and industry partners interested in co-developing advanced solutions for Aadhaar authentication.

# Benefits
Participants can benefit from:
- Co-development of scalable Aadhaar solutions.
- Access to three innovation challenges: Face Liveness Detection, Presentation Attack Detection (PAD), and Contactless Fingerprint Authentication.
- Mentorship, funding, and global outreach through collaboration with MeitY Startup Hub and NASSCOM.
- Development of technologies that enhance security against deepfakes, spoofing, and presentation attacks.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. Participants can expect support from UIDAI for co-development efforts.
