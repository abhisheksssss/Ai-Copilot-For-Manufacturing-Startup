# Overview
SITARE is a student innovation programme initiated by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC, aimed at encouraging translational biotech ideas and early validation from students and young innovators.

# Eligibility
Students and young innovators with biotech/life-science innovations are eligible to apply for this scheme.

# Benefits
Participants receive a fellowship/award/translational grant along with mentoring support.

# Funding
Translational support is commonly up to ₹15 lakh, subject to the specific call.
