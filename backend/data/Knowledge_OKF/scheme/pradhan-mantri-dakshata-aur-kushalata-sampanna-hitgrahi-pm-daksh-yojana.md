# Overview
The PRADHAN MANTRI DAKSHATA AUR KUSHALATA SAMPANNA HITGRAHI (PM-DAKSH) YOJANA is a Central Sector Scheme aimed at enhancing the competency level of target groups to make them employable in both self-employment and wage-employment. The scheme targets SCs, OBCs, EBCs, DNTs, Safai Karamcharis including waste pickers.

# Eligibility
The target group includes:
- SCs, OBCs, EBCs, DNTs, Safai Karamcharis, and waste pickers.
- Age criteria: 18-45 years.
- Income criteria: No income limit for SCs, Safai Karamcharis. For OBCs and EBCs, the annual family income should be below Rs.3 lakh (OBCs) and Rs.1 lakh (EBCs).

# Benefits
The scheme offers various types of training with different durations:
- Up-skilling/Reskilling: 35 to 60 hours / 5 days to 35 days.
- Short Term Training: 300 hours / 3 months.
- Entrepreneurship Development Programme: 90 hours / 15 days.
- Long Term Training: 650 hours / 7 months.

The training is provided free of cost. Additionally, the scheme provides stipends and wage compensation:
- Rs.1,500/- per month to SCs and Safai Karamcharis for non-residential Short Term and Long Term training courses.
- Rs.1,000/- per month to OBCs/EBCs/DNTs for non-residential Short Term and Long Term training courses.
- Wage compensation of Rs.2500/- per candidate is given to SCs/OBCs/EBCs/DNTs candidates for Upskilling/Reskilling programme.
- Wage compensation of Rs.500/- per candidate is given to Safai Karamcharis candidates for Upskilling programme.

# Funding
The scheme provides funding through stipends and wage compensations as mentioned above, ensuring that the training is accessible and affordable for the target groups.
