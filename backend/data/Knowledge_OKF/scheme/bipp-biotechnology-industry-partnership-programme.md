# Overview
BIPP is a programme initiated by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) in collaboration with BIRAC, aimed at fostering high-risk, high-impact biotech innovation through partnerships between biotech companies/startups and academia-industry consortia.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include biotech companies/startups and academia-industry consortia that propose transformational R&D projects.

# Benefits
Participants receive project-based support in the form of grants, soft loans, or cost-sharing support, depending on the approved cost and scheme terms.

# Funding
Funding is provided on a project basis with varying amounts based on the approved cost and scheme terms. The support can be in the form of grants, soft loans, or cost-sharing arrangements.
