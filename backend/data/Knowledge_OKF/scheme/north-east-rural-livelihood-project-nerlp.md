# Overview
The North East Rural Livelihood Project (NERLP) is a World Bank aided, multi-state livelihood project aimed at improving rural livelihoods, especially those of women, unemployed youth, and the most disadvantaged in four North Eastern States.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries include women, unemployed youth, and the most disadvantaged communities residing in two districts each in Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and five districts in Tripura. The project also works with expert organizations to focus on cluster development through value chains.

# Benefits
The project aims to build capacity for self-governance, bottom-up planning, democratic functioning with transparency and accountability, increase economic and livelihood opportunities, develop partnerships for natural resource management, microfinance, market linkages, and sectoral economic services. It also focuses on social empowerment, economic empowerment, and sustainable community institutions around women Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Youth Groups of men and women (YG), and Community Development Groups (CDG).

# Funding
The project works in partnerships with expert organizations to focus on cluster development through value chains.
