# Overview
The National Innovations on Climate Resilient Agriculture (NICRA) scheme aims to promote and support climate-resilient agricultural practices.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and research institutions involved in promoting climate-resilient agriculture.

# Benefits
Beneficiaries receive financial assistance for adopting innovative technologies and practices that enhance the resilience of crops against adverse climatic conditions.

# Funding
Funding is provided through a combination of central government grants and contributions from state governments.
