# Overview
The Ayushman Bharat - National Health Protection Mission (AB-NHPM) is a national health protection mission launched by the Government of India to provide financial protection against high out-of-pocket expenditure on healthcare.

# Eligibility
Beneficiaries are individuals and families who receive an Ayushman card, issued by the National Health Authority (NHA). The cards are distributed through family health folders maintained at Health Wellness Centres (HWC) or nearby Primary Healthcare Centres (PHC).

# Benefits
Cardholders can avail cashless treatment and hospitalisation services at network hospitals across India. This ensures that every family is aware of their entitlement to healthcare, both through HWCs and the Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana or equivalent state and central government health schemes.

# Funding
The scheme does not specify funding details but implies that it operates with a network of public and private hospitals across India.
