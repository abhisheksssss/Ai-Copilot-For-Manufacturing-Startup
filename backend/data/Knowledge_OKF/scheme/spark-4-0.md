# Overview
SPARK–4.0 (Studentship Program for Ayurveda Research Ken) is the fourth edition of CCRAS’s flagship initiative to encourage research orientation among BAMS undergraduates.

# Eligibility
Open to students from National Commission for Indian System of Medicine NCISM-recognized colleges across India.

# Benefits
300 BAMS students will be awarded ₹50,000 studentships (₹25,000 per month for two months). Students receive a certificate after successful completion. Involves faculty-guided, short-term independent research projects.

# Funding
Each student will receive ₹50,000 in total, with ₹25,000 per month for two months.
