# Overview
The EKLAVYA MODEL RESIDENTIAL SCHEME (EMRS) is a Central sector scheme aimed at providing quality education to Scheduled Tribes students from Class 6 to Class 12 in remote areas, enabling them to access the best opportunities in education and bringing them at par with the general population.

# Eligibility
Eligible students are those belonging to Scheduled Tribes residing in blocks with more than 50% ST population and at least 20,000 tribal population as per the 2011 census. In areas where the density of ST population is higher than 90%, Eklavya Model Day Boarding Schools (EMDBS) can be set up on an experimental basis.

# Benefits
Students in EMRS receive free education and benefit from a holistic development approach, including academic education, preservation of local art & culture, sports training, and skill development. Each school has a capacity of 480 students and is comparable to Navodaya Schools.

# Funding
The scheme involves the establishment of one EMRS in every block with more than 50% ST population and at least 20,000 tribal population as per the 2011 census. The government plans to provide suitable land for these schools, which can also be run in alternative buildings, preferably Government buildings, until permanent structures are constructed.
