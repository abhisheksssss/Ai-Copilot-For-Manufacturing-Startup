# Overview
Skill India Mission is a flagship program of the Government of India under the Ministry of Education aimed at providing skill training and employment opportunities to the youth.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates are individuals aged 15-45 years who wish to acquire vocational skills for better employability.

# Benefits
Participants receive free or subsidized training, certification, and job placement assistance. The mission also provides financial support in the form of stipends and scholarships.

# Funding
The program is funded through a combination of central government funds, state governments, private sector partnerships, and international aid.
