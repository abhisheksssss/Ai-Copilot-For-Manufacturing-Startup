# Overview
SITARE is a student innovation programme that encourages translational biotech ideas and early validation, providing support for biotech and life-science innovations.

# Eligibility
Students and young innovators with biotech or life-science innovations are eligible to apply.

# Benefits
The scheme provides translational support, commonly up to ₹15 lakh, subject to call, along with mentoring.

# Funding
The scheme offers a grant of up to ₹15 lakh.
