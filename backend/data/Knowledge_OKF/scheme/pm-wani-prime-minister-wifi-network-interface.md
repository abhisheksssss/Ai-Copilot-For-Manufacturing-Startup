# Overview
PM-WANI (PRIME MINISTER WIFI NETWORK INTERFACE) was launched in 2020 with the objective to enhance the proliferation of public Wi-Fi hotspots, thereby creating a robust digital communications infrastructure in the country, especially in rural areas. The scheme consists of four parts: Public Data Office (PDO), Public Data Office Aggregator (PDOA), App Provider, and Central Registry.
