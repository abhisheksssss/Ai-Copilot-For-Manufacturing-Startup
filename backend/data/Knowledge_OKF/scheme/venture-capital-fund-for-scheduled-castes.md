# Overview
The Venture Capital Fund for Scheduled Castes is a social sector venture fund that provides concessionary finance to SC entrepreneurs and companies.

# Eligibility
The fund is available to companies promoted by Scheduled Caste entrepreneurs, including startups and MSMEs.

# Benefits
The fund provides equity/debt support, including equity, quasi-equity, and debt, to eligible companies.

# Funding
The fund offers ₹10 lakh to ₹15 crore in funding, subject to fund guidelines.
