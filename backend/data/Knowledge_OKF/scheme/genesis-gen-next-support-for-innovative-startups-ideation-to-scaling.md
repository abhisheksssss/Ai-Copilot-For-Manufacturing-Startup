# Overview
GENESIS is a MeitY scheme aimed at discovering, supporting, and scaling technology startups in Tier-II/III cities.

# Eligibility
Technology startups and entrepreneurs located in Tier-II/III cities, including deep-tech ventures.

# Benefits
Support includes Early R&D (EiR), pilot projects, investment support, and funding for deep-tech ventures through implementing agencies.

# Funding
The scheme outlay is ₹490 crore. The level of startup support varies by component.
