# Overview
The Defence Testing Infrastructure (DTI) Scheme aims to promote indigenous defence production by setting up new test facilities and providing uniform pension benefits.

# Eligibility
Private entities registered in India and State Government agencies are eligible to form Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) for the implementation of the scheme. The SPVs will be registered under the Companies Act 2013.

# Benefits
- Setting up 6-8 new test facilities in partnership with private industry.
- Bridging gaps in defence testing infrastructure and reducing imports of military equipment.
- Facilitating indigenous defence production, making the country self-reliant.
- Providing uniform pension benefits to Armed Forces personnel retiring in the same rank with the same length of service.

# Funding
Projects under the scheme will receive up to 75% government funding in the form of 'Grant-in-Aid'. The remaining 25% of the project cost will be borne by the SPV. The equipment/systems tested will be certified as per appropriate accreditation.
