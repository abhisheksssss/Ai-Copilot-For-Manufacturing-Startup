# Overview
The PRADHAN MANTRI ANNADATA AAY SANRAKSHAN ABHIYAN (PM-AASHA) scheme aims to ensure remunerative prices for farmers and control price volatility of essential commodities, benefiting consumers.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. Typically, this would include farmers who produce crops covered by the scheme.

# Benefits
- Ensures remunerative prices for farmers.
- Protects consumers from extreme price volatility of agri-horticultural commodities through a strategic buffer stock mechanism.
- Ensures affordable supply to consumers and discourages hoarding and speculation.

# Funding
The total financial outgo for PM-AASHA is ₹35,000 crore during the 15th Finance Commission cycle (up to 2025-26). The Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) rates for the rabi season (phosphatic and potassic fertilizers) are set at a tentative budget of ₹24,475.53 crore.
