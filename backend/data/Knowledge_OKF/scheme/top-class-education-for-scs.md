# Overview
The Top Class Education for SCs Scheme aims at recognizing and promoting quality education amongst students belonging to Scheduled Castes (SCs), by providing financial support.

# Eligibility
- The scheme covers selected meritorious SC students pursuing studies beyond the 12th class.
- The ceiling of the total family income under the scheme is 8 lakhs per annum.
- Applications are processed through the National Scholarships Portal for both fresh and renewal students.

# Benefits
Financial support will be provided to eligible students until the completion of their course, subject to satisfactory performance.
