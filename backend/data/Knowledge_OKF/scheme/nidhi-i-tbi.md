# Overview
NIDHI-i-TBI is an initiative by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) to create inclusive technology incubators in underserved institutions/regions, supporting local innovators and early-stage technology startups.

# Eligibility
No specific eligibility criteria are mentioned, but the scheme focuses on underserved institutions/regions.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹5 crore over 3 years to the incubator, along with maker-space/mentoring for startups.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Department of Science & Technology (DST), with a grant of up to ₹5 crore over 3 years.
