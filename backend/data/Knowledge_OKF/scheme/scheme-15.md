### Summary of Key Pharmaceutical Sector Schemes in India

#### 3.8 PHARMACEUTICAL CLUSTER DEVELOPMENT SCHEME (PCDS)

**Objectives:**
- Make India a global leader in the pharmaceutical sector.
- Strengthen existing infrastructure facilities.

**Salient Features:**
- **Common Facilities:** Includes solvent recovery plants, distillation units, power & steam units, common effluent treatment plants, etc.
- **Funding:** Grants-in-Aid by the Government of India up to Rs. 1000 crore per park.
- **Implementing Agency:** State Implementing Agencies (SIA) set up by respective state governments.

**Recent Changes:**
- Replacement of minimum threshold investment with committed investment.
- Extension of scheme tenure for FY 2021-22, with sales incentives applicable from FY 2022-23 to FY 2026-27.

#### 3.9 PRADHAN MANTRI BHARTIYA JANAUSHADIPARIYOJANA (PM-BJP)

**Objectives:**
- Provide quality medicines at affordable prices.
- Create awareness about generic medicines.
- Generate employment by engaging entrepreneurs in opening PMBJP Kendras.

**Salient Features:**
- **Mission:** Provide all related health care products under the scheme.
- **Assistance:** Rs. 2.5 lakhs for NGOs/agencies/individuals establishing Janaushadhi Kendras (JAK) in government hospital premises.
- **Sanitary Napkin Program:** Oxo-biodegradable sanitary napkins launched to ensure 'Swachhta, Swasthya, and Suvidha' for underprivileged women.

#### 3.10 SCHEME STRENGTHENING OF PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRY (SPI)

**Objectives:**
- Strengthen existing infrastructure.
- Upgrade production facilities of SMEs/MSMEs to meet regulatory standards.
- Promote knowledge and awareness in the pharmaceutical industry.

**Salient Features:**
- **Common Facilities:** Creation of Common Facility Centers (CFCs) including testing, training, R&D centers, effluent treatment plants, etc.
- **Sub-schemes:**
  - Assistance to Pharmaceutical Industry for Common Facilities
  - Strengthening MSMEs through Technology Upgradation Assistance Scheme
  - Promotion and Development of Pharmaceutical & Medical Devices Sector

#### 3.11 SCHEME FOR PROMOTION OF MEDICAL DEVICES PARK (MDP)

**Objectives:**
- Create world-class infrastructure facilities.
- Reduce production costs to improve availability and affordability.

**Salient Features:**
- **Medical Device Park:** Designated area with common infrastructure for exclusive manufacturing of medical devices.
- **Financial Assistance:** 70% of project cost (90% in North Eastern States and Hilly States) for common infrastructure facilities.
- **Duration:** FY 2020-21 to FY 2024-25.
- **Selection Criteria:** Based on state policy incentives, total area, land lease rate, connectivity, ease of doing business.

#### 3.12 MISCELLANEOUS INITIATIVES

**Pharma Jan Samadhan:**
- Web-enabled system for redressal of consumers' grievances related to pricing and availability of medicines.
- Effective implementation tool for Drugs (Price Control) Order 2013.

**Pharma SahiDaam Mobile App:**
- Developed by NPPA, shows MRP fixed by NPPA for various scheduled drugs in real-time.

### Key Points:
- **PCDS:** Focuses on common facilities and state-level implementation.
- **PM-BJP:** Aims to make medicines affordable and create awareness about generics.
- **SPI:** Enhances infrastructure and technology upgradation for pharmaceuticals.
- **MDP:** Promotes medical devices parks with significant financial support.
- **Pharma Jan Samadhan & SahiDaam:** Digital tools for consumer redressal and transparency in pricing.

These schemes collectively aim to strengthen the pharmaceutical sector, improve quality, reduce costs, and increase accessibility of medicines and medical devices.
