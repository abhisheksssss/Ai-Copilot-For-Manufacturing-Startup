# Overview
The Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme is a deep-tech scaling large RDI financing framework that provides long-term, low-cost capital for private-sector research and innovation.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to startups, companies, and innovation-led enterprises in strategic/deep-tech sectors.

# Benefits
The scheme offers long-term R&D finance, including long-term financing, loans, and equity-like support through designated channels.

# Funding
The scheme corpus is ₹1 lakh crore, with project-level support varying.
