# Overview
SAKSHAM ANGANWADI AND POSHAN 2.0 (MISSION POSHAN 2.0) is a centrally-sponsored scheme that merges Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS), POSHAN Abhiyaan, and Scheme for Adolescent Girls (SAG). The mission aims to address malnutrition in children, adolescent girls, and pregnant/lactating women through strategic shifts in nutrition content, delivery, and the creation of a convergent ecosystem.

# Eligibility
The scheme benefits:
- Children up to 6 years old.
- Adolescent girls aged 14-18 years in Aspirational Districts and North Eastern States.
- Pregnant and lactating women.

# Benefits
Key features include:
- Technology platform POSHAN TRACKER for enhanced transparency.
- Dynamic identification of nutrition deficiencies using growth monitoring devices (Stadiometer, Infantometer, Weighing scale).
- Mandatory inclusion of millets at least once a week in Take Home Ration and Hot Cooked Meals.
- Establishment of POSHAN Vatikas (kitchen gardens) near Anganwadi Centres.
- Reduction in child wasting, under-weight prevalence, stunting, and anaemia through AYUSH practices.
- District Magistrate as the Nodal Point for monitoring nutritional status and quality standards.
- Scheme evaluation by a reputable third party nominated by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.

# Funding
The scheme has a corpus of 600 crore.
