# Overview
The Central Sector Interest Subsidy for Students scheme provides full interest subvention to students with an annual family income up to Rs. 4.5 lakhs.

# Eligibility
Students with an annual family income of up to Rs. 4.5 lakhs are eligible for full interest subvention under this scheme.

# Benefits
Eligible students receive full interest subvention on their education loans.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) and Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Education Loans (CGFSEL), both implemented by the Department of Higher Education under the Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan (PM-USP).
