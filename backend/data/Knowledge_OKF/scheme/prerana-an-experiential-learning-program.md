# Overview
Prerana is an experiential learning program initiated by the Department of School Education & Literacy, under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Headquartered at a vernacular school established in 1888 in Vadnagar, Gujarat, this weekly residential program aims to provide students with a profound and motivational experience that fosters leadership qualities.

# Eligibility
The program is designed for students in Classes 9 to 12 who are interested in participating. Interested students can register online through the portal [prerana.education.gov.in](http://prerana.education.gov.in), where they will undergo a selection process outlined on the platform.

# Benefits
Participants of Prerana benefit from a curriculum developed by IIT Gandhinagar, which integrates principles of the Indian education system and value-based education. The program focuses on nine core values: self-respect and humility, courage and determination, hard work and dedication, compassion and service, diversity and unity, truthfulness and cleanliness, innovation and curiosity, faith and trust, and independence and responsibility.
