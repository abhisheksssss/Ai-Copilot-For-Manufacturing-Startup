# Overview
The Pradhan Mantri–Ayushman Bharat Health Infrastructure Mission (PM-ABHIM) is a pan-India health infrastructure programme aimed at creating a resilient, accessible, and self-reliant public health system. It was launched in October 25, 2021 by the Prime Minister of India under the broader Ayushman Bharat initiative.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
The programme aims to build robust public health infrastructure capable of managing pandemics and health emergencies. It also develops an IT-enabled disease surveillance system integrated across national, regional, and local levels, contributing to Universal Health Coverage (UHC) and Sustainable Development Goal-3 (SDG-3).

# Funding
The financial outlay for PM-ABHIM is ₹64,180 crore for 2021–26. It has both Centrally Sponsored and Central Sector components in a hybrid model.
