# Overview
The Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) was launched by the Ministry of Jal Shakti to eliminate open defecation in rural areas and make urban India free from open defecation and achieve 100% scientific management of municipal solid waste.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for SBM are not explicitly stated but generally include rural and urban areas that aim to become Open Defecation Free (ODF), ODF+, or ODF++.

# Benefits
SBM aims to improve sanitation infrastructure, promote behavioral change towards using toilets, and ensure the functional maintenance of community and public toilets. It also focuses on safely managing faecal sludge/septage and sewage.

# Funding
SBM is funded by the Government of India through various budgets allocated for its implementation phases. The mission has received significant financial support from both central and state governments.
