# Overview
The Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) provides seed and early-stage funding through approved incubators for proof-of-concept (PoC), prototype development, market entry, and commercialisation.

# Eligibility
DPIIT-recognised early-stage startups with technology-enabled business ideas are eligible to apply.

# Benefits
Eligible startups can receive up to ₹20 lakh as a grant and up to ₹50 lakh in convertible debt through incubators.

# Funding
Funding is disbursed through approved incubators, combining grants and convertible debt.
