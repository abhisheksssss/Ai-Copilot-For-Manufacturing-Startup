# Overview
This scheme aims to open the nuclear power sector to non-government entities such as Indian private companies and government-private joint ventures, with specific activities regulated by the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB).

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include:
- Indian private companies
- Government–private joint ventures
- Other entities expressly permitted by the Centre

Excluded are foreign-incorporated companies.

# Benefits
Entities can engage in:
- Building nuclear plants
- Owning and operating nuclear plants
- Decommissioning nuclear plants
- Fabrication, transport, trade, and storage of nuclear fuel

All radiation-related activities require mandatory safety authorisation from the AERB.
