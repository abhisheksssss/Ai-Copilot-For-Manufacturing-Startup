# Overview
The Startups Intellectual Property Protection (SIPP) Scheme is a facilitated IPR filing scheme where the government pays the facilitator's professional fee on behalf of startups. This scheme is available to DPIIT-recognised startups filing patents, trademarks, or designs in India or abroad.

# Eligibility
DPIIT-recognised startups at any stage are eligible for the SIPP Scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides several benefits, including:
* 80% rebate on patent statutory fees
* 50% rebate on trademark/design fees
* End-to-end IP facilitation at no charge to the startup

# Funding
The government pays the facilitator's professional fee on behalf of the startups.
