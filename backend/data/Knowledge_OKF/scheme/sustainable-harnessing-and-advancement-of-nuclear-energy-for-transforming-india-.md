# Overview
The SHANTI Act, 2025 is a comprehensive legislation that replaces the Atomic Energy Act, 1962 and the Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act, 2010, creating a unified framework for regulation, licensing, safety, and liability in India’s civil nuclear sector.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is implied that entities involved in the civil nuclear sector may be eligible under strict regulatory conditions.

# Benefits
The SHANTI Act aims to:
- Accelerate nuclear power capacity for clean energy transition.
- Enable private and joint venture (JV) participation under strict regulation.
- Modernize safety, liability, and oversight mechanisms.
