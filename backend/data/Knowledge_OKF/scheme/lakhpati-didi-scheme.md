# Overview
The LAKHPATI DIDI SCHEME was launched in 2023 by the Ministry of Rural Development with the aim to catalyse economic empowerment and financial independence among women in rural areas.

# Eligibility
- A Self Help Group (SHG) member who has completed a minimum of two years and has availed of the Community Investment Fund (CIF).
- A beneficiary of livelihood intervention through DAY-NRLM and practising at least two livelihood activities.

# Benefits
- Training 3 crore women to become influencers both at home and in their communities.
- Aimed at training SHG members to earn a sustainable income of at least Rs 1 lakh per annum per household.
- Providing opportunities for women to earn income using the skills they acquire.
- Facilitating diversified livelihood activities through convergence across all Government departments, Private sector, and Market players.

# Funding
The scheme aims to train women in various skills and provide them with opportunities to earn income. It also includes providing drones to approximately 15,000 women SHGs for agricultural activities, which can revolutionize agriculture by enabling precision farming, crop monitoring, and pest control.
