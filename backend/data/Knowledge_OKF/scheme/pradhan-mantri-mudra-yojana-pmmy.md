# Overview
Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a credit support programme under Shishu, Kishor and Tarun categories for non-corporate, non-farm micro/small enterprises including early-stage businesses and startups.

# Eligibility
Non-corporate, non-farm micro/small enterprises including early-stage businesses and startups are eligible to apply. Specifically, the scheme covers:
- Shishu: Loans up to ₹10 lakh
- Kishor: Loans up to ₹10 lakh
- Tarun: Loans up to ₹20 lakh for eligible repeat borrowers

# Benefits
The benefits include access to micro-enterprise loans with flexible terms and working capital support through banks, Microfinance Institutions (MFIs), and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

# Funding
Funding is provided through term loans and working capital through banks, MFIs, and NBFCs.
