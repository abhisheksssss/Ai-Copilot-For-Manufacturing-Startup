# Overview
The Innovation & Agri-Entrepreneurship Program (RKVY) is an initiative that supports agri-startups and agripreneurs through RKVY-supported agri-business incubators, providing them with the necessary resources to grow and develop.

# Eligibility
The program is designed to support pre-seed and seed-stage agripreneurs and startups in the agri and allied sectors.

# Benefits
The benefits of the program include grant-in-aid and incubation/mentoring services, with financial support of up to ₹5 lakh for pre-seed stage and up to ₹25 lakh for seed stage.

# Funding
The program provides funding in the form of a grant, with the amount varying depending on the stage of the startup, up to ₹5 lakh for pre-seed and up to ₹25 lakh for seed stage.
