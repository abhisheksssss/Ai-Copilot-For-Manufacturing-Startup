# Overview
The NATIONAL ACTION FOR MECHANISED SANITATION ECOSYSTEM (NAMASTE) SCHEME is a joint initiative by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) and the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA). The scheme aims to eradicate unsafe sewer and septic tank cleaning practices through various key features.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals are Sewer/Septic Tank Workers (SSWs).

# Benefits
- Occupational Training for SSWs.
- Distribution of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Kits to SSWs.
- Assistance for Safety Devices to Sanitation Response Units (SRUs).
- Health Insurance Scheme benefits under the Ayushman Bharat-Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) extended to identified SSWs and their families.
- Funding support and subsidy (capital + interest) provided for sanitation workers to procure sanitation-related equipment, promoting mechanization and enterprise development.

# Funding
The scheme provides funding support and subsidies (both capital and interest) to sanitation workers to purchase sanitation-related equipment. Additionally, massive awareness campaigns will be conducted jointly by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) and National Sanitation Knowledge Facilitation and Development Corporation (NSKFDC).
