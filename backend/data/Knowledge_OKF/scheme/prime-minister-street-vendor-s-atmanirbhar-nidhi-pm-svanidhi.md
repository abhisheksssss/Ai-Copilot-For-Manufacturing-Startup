# Overview
The PRIME MINISTER STREET VENDOR’S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI) is a Central sector scheme aimed at providing handholding support to street vendors during the pandemic-induced economic stress.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries are street vendors who can be identified by the Town Vending Committee (TVC).

# Benefits
- Collateral-free loans of ₹10,000 with one-year tenure.
- Subsequent loans of ₹20,000 and ₹50,000 available.
- 7% interest subsidy on the loans.
- Graded Guarantee Cover for the loans provided by the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE).

# Funding
The scheme is funded through the Central government with support from CGTMSE.
