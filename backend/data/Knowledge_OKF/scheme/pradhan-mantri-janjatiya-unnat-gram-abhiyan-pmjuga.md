# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI JANJATIYA UNNAT GRAM ABHIYAN (PMJUGA) aims to enhance the socio-economic conditions of tribal communities in tribal-majority villages and aspirational districts through saturation coverage. The scheme has a total outlay of ₹79,156 crore, with a central share of ₹56,333 crore and state share of ₹22,823 crore.

# Eligibility
The beneficiaries are over 5 crore tribal people across 549 districts and 2,740 blocks in 30 States/UTs.

# Benefits
Key objectives include:
- **Housing**: Construction of 20 lakh pucca houses for eligible Scheduled Tribe (ST) households under PMAY-Gramin.
- **Infrastructure**: Construction of 25,000 km of roads to connect tribal-majority villages, installation of off-grid solar power for unelectrified households, and providing piped water to eligible villages.
- **Healthcare**: Setting up 1,000 mobile medical units, building 2,000 new Anganwadi centers, upgrading 6,000 others, and establishing facilities for diagnosing sickle cell disease.
- **Education**: Increasing the Gross Enrolment Ratio by setting up hostels, upgrading tribal schools, and ensuring access to quality education.
- **Economic Empowerment**: Promoting sustainable agricultural practices for 22 lakh FRA patta holders and creating 100 Tribal Multipurpose Marketing Centers (TMMCs) to market tribal products.
- **Tourism**: Developing 1,000 homestays in tribal villages with tourism potential, providing financial support of ₹5 lakh per new construction and ₹3 lakh for renovations.

# Funding
The total outlay is ₹79,156 crore, with a central share of ₹56,333 crore and state share of ₹22,823 crore.
