# Overview
The NIDHI – Centres of Excellence Scaling (Incubation excellence) scheme is an initiative by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) to support high-performing incubators in becoming Centres of Excellence and scaling deep startup support.

# Eligibility
Mature incubators with a strong track record are eligible for this scheme, with the ultimate beneficiaries being startups that indirectly benefit from the support provided to the incubators.

# Benefits
The scheme provides high-impact incubator support, including large incubator grants, infrastructure support, seed and scale support, and access to networks.

# Funding
The scheme provides funding of up to ₹50 crore over 5 years to the incubator.
