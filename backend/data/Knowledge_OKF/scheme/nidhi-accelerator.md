# Overview
NIDHI – Accelerator is a DST-supported accelerator mechanism to help incubated startups rapidly validate markets and scale. It is designed for technology startups selected through supported incubators/accelerators.

# Eligibility
Technology startups selected through supported incubators/accelerators are eligible for the NIDHI – Accelerator scheme.

# Benefits
The benefits of the NIDHI – Accelerator scheme include market readiness, structured acceleration, mentoring, investor connects, and demo days.

# Funding
The funding for the NIDHI – Accelerator scheme is up to ₹40 lakh, with ₹30 lakh allocated to the accelerator and ₹10 lakh allocated to the startups.
