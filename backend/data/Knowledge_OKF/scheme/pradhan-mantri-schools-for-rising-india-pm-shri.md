# Overview
The PRADHAN MANTRI SCHOOLS FOR RISING INDIA (PM-SHRI) is a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at strengthening select existing schools to provide high-quality education in an equitable, inclusive, and joyful environment. The scheme leverages the existing administrative structure available for Samagra Shiksha, KVS & NVS.

# Eligibility
The scheme focuses on 14500 selected schools across India.

# Benefits
- Development of schools as 'Green Schools' incorporating environmental-friendly aspects like solar panels, LED lights, nutrition gardens with natural farming, water conservation and harvesting, climate change related hackathons.
- Adoption of experiential, holistic, inquiry-driven, discovery-oriented, and learner-centred pedagogy.
- Linkage with sector skill councils and local industry to enhance employability and provide better employment opportunities.
- Implementation of the School Quality Assessment Framework (SQAF) for regular evaluation.
- Provision of Science and Math kits under the RTE Act.
- Focus on early childhood care and education, including Balvatika and Foundational Literacy and Numeracy.
- Encouragement of local languages/mother tongue as medium of instruction with technological interventions to bridge language barriers.
- Convergence with existing schemes/Panchayati Raj Institutions/Urban Local Bodies for infrastructure upgradation.
