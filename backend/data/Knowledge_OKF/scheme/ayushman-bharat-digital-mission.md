# Overview
The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission aims to create a national digital health ecosystem that supports universal health coverage by 2030 in an efficient, accessible, inclusive, affordable, timely and safe manner through the use of digital technologies.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is implied that all individuals can benefit from this mission as it aims to cover universal health coverage.

# Benefits
The benefits include:
- Creation of a 14-digit unique ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) number using personal details like name, mobile, and Aadhar.
- A database for healthcare professionals in both traditional and modern medicines with unique IDs.
- A registry of all healthcare facilities such as hospitals, clinics, diagnostic labs, and pharmacies.
