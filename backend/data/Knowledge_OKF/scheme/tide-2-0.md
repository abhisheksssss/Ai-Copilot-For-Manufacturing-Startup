# Overview
TIDE 2.0 is a Technology Incubation and Development of Entrepreneurs programme, an incubator-led programme supporting ICT startups using emerging technologies for societal challenges. It focuses on ICT startups and entrepreneurs working in AI, IoT, blockchain, robotics, and emerging technologies.

# Eligibility
ICT startups and entrepreneurs working in AI, IoT, blockchain, robotics, and emerging technologies are eligible for TIDE 2.0.

# Benefits
The benefits of TIDE 2.0 include Proof of Concept (PoC) grants up to ₹7 lakh, as well as larger support that varies by centre/component. Additionally, it provides grant, incubation, and technical mentoring through TIDE centres.

# Funding
TIDE 2.0 provides funding in the form of PoC grants up to ₹7 lakh, with larger support available depending on the centre/component.
