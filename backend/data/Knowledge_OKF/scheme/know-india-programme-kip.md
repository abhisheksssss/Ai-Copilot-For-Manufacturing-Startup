# Overview
The KNOW INDIA PROGRAMME (KIP) is a three-week orientation programme aimed at Indian-origin youth aged 18 to 30 years, designed to familiarize them with their roots and contemporary India.

# Eligibility
Preference is given to Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) from Girmitiya countries. Non-Resident Indians are not eligible for KIP.

# Benefits
Participants will gain awareness on different facets of life in India and the progress made by the country in various fields. They will also have the opportunity to interact with ministry officers, gaining insights into the workings of the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) and potential career opportunities in diplomacy.

# Overview
The PRAVASI KAUSHAL VIKAS YOJANA (PKVY) is a skill development initiative aimed at training and certifying Indian workers for overseas employment, aligning with international standards.

# Eligibility
Indian workforce keen on overseas employment in select sectors and job roles are eligible.

# Benefits
Participants will receive holistic training to take up challenging assignments abroad. The programme focuses on communication, trade-specific knowledge, cultural orientation, and other relevant skill sets.

# Funding
Funded by the Government of India through the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC).
