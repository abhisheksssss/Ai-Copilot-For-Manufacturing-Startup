# Overview
The NM-ICPS – Technology Innovation Hubs (National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems) aims to create 25 Technology Innovation Hubs for Research and Development (R&D), translational research, and startup incubation in Cyber-Physical System (CPS) technologies.

# Eligibility
Deep-tech startups, researchers, and innovators in AI, IoT, robotics, cyber security, and autonomous systems are eligible to participate.

# Benefits
The mission provides deep-tech CPS incubation, grants/seed support, testbeds, mentoring, and market connections through the Technology Innovation Hubs (TIH).

# Funding
The Mission outlay is ₹3,660 crore. The startup support varies by TIH/call.
