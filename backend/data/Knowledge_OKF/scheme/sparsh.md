# Overview
SPARSH is a Social Innovation Programme for Products Affordable & Relevant to Social Health, supporting affordable and socially relevant healthcare/biotech product innovations through the ideation to validation stage.

# Eligibility
Startups and innovators developing affordable products for priority public-health and social needs are eligible for the programme.

# Benefits
The programme offers grant support of up to ₹50 lakh, subject to call guidelines, along with mentoring and social innovation immersion.

# Funding
The programme is supported by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC, offering a combination of grant and mentoring support.
