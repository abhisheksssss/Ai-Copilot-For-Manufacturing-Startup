# Overview
The PAN 2.0 Project aims to modernize the existing Permanent Account Number (PAN) system by introducing new features such as a QR code and transforming it into a common identifier for businesses and digital systems.

# Eligibility
All entities using PAN data, including banks, insurance companies, and individuals required to quote PAN on filing income tax returns. TAN is mandatory for all persons responsible for deducting or collecting tax at source.

# Benefits
- Upgrade from the current PAN/TAN 1.0 ecosystem to a unified, paperless, online system.
- Integration of QR codes in all new and old PAN cards, with existing card holders able to upgrade their cards free of cost.
- Making PAN a common business identifier for digital systems of specified government agencies and businesses.
- Clubbing of PAN/TAN/TIN under this system.
