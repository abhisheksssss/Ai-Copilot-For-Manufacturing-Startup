# Overview
The New Aadhaar App is a secure, next-generation mobile platform developed by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). It focuses on consent-based control and data minimization in line with the DPDP Act.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated but generally includes all Aadhaar cardholders who have access to smartphones.

# Benefits
- Eliminate Photocopies: Prevents misuse of Aadhaar data during routine checks.
- Resident-Centricity: Provides identity at fingertips and allows users to choose what data they share.
- Ease of Living: Reduces physical visits to Aadhaar Seva Kendras for updates.

# Funding
A nominal fee of ₹75 is applicable for certain services, such as updating mobile number & address.
