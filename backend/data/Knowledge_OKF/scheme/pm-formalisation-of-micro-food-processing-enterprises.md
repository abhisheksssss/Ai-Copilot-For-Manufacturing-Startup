# Overview
The PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises scheme aims to formalise and upgrade micro food-processing enterprises under the One District One Product initiative.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to individual micro food-processing units, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), cooperatives, and food startups/micro-enterprises.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a 35% credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 lakh per unit, as well as seed capital up to ₹40,000 per member for SHGs. Additionally, it offers support for branding, marketing, and capacity building.

# Funding
The scheme provides a credit-linked subsidy of up to ₹10 lakh per unit, with a 35% subsidy component.
