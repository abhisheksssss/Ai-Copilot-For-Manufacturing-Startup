# Overview
CoWIN (COVID Vaccine Intelligence Network) is a government digital platform for registration, scheduling, tracking, and certification of COVID-19 vaccinations. Built on earlier eVIN (Electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network), it serves as an end-to-end public health management tool from the national to the vaccinator level.

# Eligibility
Eligible individuals can register through CoWIN for vaccination. The platform supports both self and bulk registration.

# Benefits
The key benefits include:
- A transparent, efficient, and real-time system for vaccination delivery.
- Equitable access, identity verification, tracking of doses, and certification for every beneficiary.
