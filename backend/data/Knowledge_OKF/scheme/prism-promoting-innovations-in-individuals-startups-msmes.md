# Overview
The PRISM (Promoting Innovations in Individuals, Startups & MSMEs) scheme is a Department of Scientific & Industrial Research (DSIR) initiative aimed at supporting individual innovators, startups, and micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) to develop prototypes and commercialize their innovations.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators, startups, and MSMEs with technology or product innovation ideas are eligible to apply for this scheme.

# Benefits
Participants in the PRISM scheme receive grant-in-aid/prototype support along with mentoring services from DSIR.

# Funding
The funding provided under the PRISM scheme can be up to ₹50 lakh depending on the PRISM phase/category.
