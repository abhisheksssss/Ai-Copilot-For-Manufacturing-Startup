### Government Schemes Comprehensive (Part 1)

#### Operation Digital Board (ODB)
- **Objective**: To set up one digital and interactive board per classroom in government and government-aided schools by 2022.
- **Implementation**: Introduced in schools from class 9th onwards, as well as higher education institutions. 
- **Implementing Agency**: University Grant Commission for higher education.

#### SPARC (Scheme for Promotion of Academic and Research Collaboration)
- **Objective**: To boost joint research with global universities from 28 countries.
- **Eligibility**: All Indian institutions ranked in the top 100 of National Institutional Ranking Framework, targeting PhD and postdoctoral researchers. Foreign institutions in the top 100 to 200 of global academic rankings are eligible.
- **Nodal Institutions**: Identified for each participating foreign country to help coordinate with willing Participating Indian (PI) Institutions.
- **Implementing Agency**: IIT Kharagpur as the National Coordinating Institution.

#### Integrated National School Education Treasury (INSET)
- **Objective**: To create an easily accessible multi-layered information network for all parameters related to students, teachers, and schools in the country.
- **Implementation**: Aim is to provide a fully integrated, instantly accessible system at various levels: school-wise, block-wise, district-wise, constituency-wise, state-wise, and region-wise.

#### Madhyamik and Uchchtar Shiksha Kosh (MUSK)
- **Objective**: To manage all proceeds of the "Secondary and Higher Education Cess" for educational schemes.
- **Details**:
  - A cess @1% on central taxes was levied through Finance Act, 2007.
  - Initially incurred from gross budgetary support (GBS), then financed from MUSK after GBS is exhausted.
  - Fund maintained as a Reserve Fund in the non-interest-bearing section of Public Accounts of India.

#### Utilization of Funds
- **Secondary Education**: Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan Scheme, National Means-Cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme, and National Scheme for Incentives to Girls for Secondary Education.
- **Higher Education**: Schemes of interest subsidy and contribution for guarantee funds, scholarship for college & university students, Rashtriya Uchchtar Shiksha Abhiyaan, scholarship (from block grant to institutions), and National Mission on Teachers and Training.

#### National Educational Alliance for Technology (NEAT)
- **Objective**: To use AI to personalize learning based on the learner's requirements.
- **Implementation**: EdTech companies recognized through a PPP model.

#### Vidyanjali 2.0 Portal
- **Objective**: Facilitate donations, contributions from Corporate Social Responsibility funds, and volunteering for school development.

#### Indian Sign Language Dictionary (ISLD)
- **Objective**: To provide audio and text embedded sign language videos and talking books for the hearing impaired.
- **Details**: Conforms to Universal Design of Learning principles.

#### Educational Tools for Differently Abled
- **Programme**: NISHTHA, launched under Samagra Shiksha in 2019-20.
- **Aims**:
  - To motivate and equip teachers to foster critical thinking and handle diverse situations.
  - Recently, joint mission with NCERT for EMRS (Eklavya Model Residential Schools) teachers and principals.

#### School Quality Assurance and Assessment Framework (SQAFA)
- **Objective**: To provide global parameters of attainment as standards for various dimensions in schools affiliated to it.

#### National Digital Education Architecture (NDEAR) 2021
- **Objective**: To create a digital education ecosystem that benefits students, parents, teachers, and educational communities.
- **Details**: Acts as a 'super connect' between academic activities like UPI interface revolutionized the banking sector.

#### National Education Technology Forum (NETF)
- **Objective**: To provide a platform for free exchange of ideas on technology use in learning, assessment, planning, administration, etc., both for school and higher education.

#### Pradhan Mantri YUVA Scheme
- **Objective**: To train 75 aspiring writers below 30 years old.
- **Details**:
  - Part of India@75 Project (Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav).
  - Aims to bring perspectives on themes like Unsung Heroes, Freedom Fighters, etc., globally.
  - Consolidated scholarship of Rs. 50,000 per month for six months.

These schemes aim to enhance the quality and accessibility of education across various levels in India.
