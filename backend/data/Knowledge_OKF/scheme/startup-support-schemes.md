# Overview
The government has introduced various schemes to support startups, catering to different needs such as funding, incubation, and market access. These schemes aim to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and economic growth.

# Eligibility
The eligibility criteria for these schemes vary, but they are generally designed for startups, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and entrepreneurs. Some schemes may have specific requirements, such as being in a particular sector or having a certain level of revenue.

# Benefits
The benefits of these schemes include access to funding, incubation, and mentorship, as well as support for intellectual property protection, market access, and sector-specific support. Startups can use these schemes to grow their business, develop new products and services, and expand into new markets.

# Funding
The government provides various funding options for startups, including grants, equity investment, and loans. Some notable schemes include the Startup India Fund of Funds, the India Aspiration Fund, and the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) Seed Fund.
