# Overview
The ADOPT A HERITAGE SCHEME (APNI DHAROHAR, APNI PEHCHAN) is an initiative of the Ministry of Tourism in collaboration with the Ministry of Culture and the Archaeological Survey of India. Launched on World Tourism Day 2017, it invites entities such as public sector companies, private firms, and individuals to develop selected monuments and heritage sites across India.

# Eligibility
Entities eligible under this scheme include:
- Public sector companies
- Private sector firms
- Individuals

These entities can adopt a site for an initial period of five years. There is no financial bid involved; the corporate sector is expected to use CSR funds for the upkeep of the adopted site.

# Benefits
The benefits include:
- Development and maintenance of heritage sites, including provision of basic amenities such as drinking water, ease of access for differently abled individuals, and senior citizens.
- The opportunity to contribute to the tourism infrastructure development of selected monuments.

# Funding
There is no financial bid involved. Corporate entities are expected to use their CSR funds for the upkeep and maintenance of the adopted site.
