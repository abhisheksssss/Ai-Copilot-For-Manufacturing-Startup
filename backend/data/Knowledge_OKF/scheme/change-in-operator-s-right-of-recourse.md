# Overview
Removes the right of recourse against suppliers for defective equipment or materials in nuclear contracts. Recourse remains only where contractually provided or in cases of deliberate acts causing damage, aligning India with global nuclear liability norms.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not specified in the given text.

# Benefits
- Removes the right of recourse against suppliers for defective equipment or materials.
- Recourse is limited to contractually provided terms and cases of deliberate damage.
