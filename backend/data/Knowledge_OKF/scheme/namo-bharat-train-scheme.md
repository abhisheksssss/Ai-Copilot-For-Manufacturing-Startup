# Overview
Namo Bharat is a regional rapid rail system designed for fast, intercity travel across NCR cities within 100–250 km range.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it can be inferred that any citizen or organization within the National Capital Region (NCR) may benefit from this scheme.

# Benefits
- Enhance commuter mobility in NCR.
- Promote decongestion, urban integration, and green transit.
- Support the Make in India initiative through indigenous rail production.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. The implementation is carried out by NCRTC – a joint venture company of the Government of India and States of Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, and U.P., under the administrative control of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.
