# Overview
A Centrally Sponsored Scheme launched to upgrade 14,500 schools into model institutions that demonstrate best practices under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, integrating technology, sustainability, and inclusivity. Launched in September 2022 by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

# Eligibility
Eligible are all government and aided schools across India, with a competitive challenge-mode selection process through UDISE+ data, field verification, and expert committee approval.

# Benefits
- High-quality, holistic, and skill-based education through experiential learning.
- Infrastructure upgrade including smart classrooms, computer labs, integrated science labs, skill labs, and Atal Tinkering Labs.
- Green initiatives such as emphasis on solar power, rainwater harvesting, and waste management for eco-friendly campuses.
- Competency-based learning with a shift from rote methods to inquiry-driven, experiential pedagogy.
- Holistic assessment through continuous, competency-linked evaluation instead of exam-centric grading.
- Teacher empowerment with dedicated modules for digital pedagogy and professional growth.
- Inclusive education with special facilities for barrier-free access, gender equity, and multilingual learning.

# Funding
- Centre–State Share: 60:40 (General States/UTs with legislature); 90:10 (NE & Himalayan States, J&K); 100% Central for UTs without legislature.
- Implemented jointly by the Department of School Education & Literacy (MoE) with participation from States/UTs, Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan, and Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti.
