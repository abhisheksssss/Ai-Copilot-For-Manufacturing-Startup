# Overview
SAMRIDH is a programme by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) that enables accelerators to support promising software/product startups with services and matching funding.

# Eligibility
Existing IT/software product startups can apply to the scheme through SAMRIDH accelerators.

# Benefits
The scheme provides acceleration and matching funding support to selected startups.

# Funding
The scheme offers matching funding up to ₹40 lakh per startup via selected accelerators.
