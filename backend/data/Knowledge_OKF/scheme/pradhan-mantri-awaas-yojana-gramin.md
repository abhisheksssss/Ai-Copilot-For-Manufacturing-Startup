# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI AWAAS YOJANA-GRAMIN (PMAY-G) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched in 2016 with the aim of ensuring the provision of pucca housing for all individuals who are homeless or living in dilapidated houses in rural areas, except Delhi and Chandigarh, by March 2024. The scheme aims to construct 2.95 crore houses.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries include houseless households living in zero, one, or two-room houses with kutcha walls and kutcha roofs. Criteria for automatic/compulsory inclusion are:
- Households without shelter
- Destitute/living on alms
- Manual scavengers
- Primitive Tribal Groups
- Legally released bonded labourers.

Beneficiaries are selected through a three-stage validation process involving the Socio Economic Caste Census 2011, Gram Sabha, and geo-tagging.

# Benefits
Financial assistance of ₹ 1,20,000 per unit for plain areas and ₹ 1,30,000 per unit for hilly areas, difficult areas, and Integrated Action Plan districts is provided. Beneficiaries can avail institutional finance (loan) of up to ₹ 70,000 at a 3% lower interest rate. The minimum size of the house shall be 25 sq m including a dedicated area for hygienic cooking. In convergence with Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin (SBM-G), financial assistance of up to ₹ 12,000 is provided for constructing toilets. Beneficiaries are entitled to employment as unskilled labour at ₹ 90.95 per day for 95 days under MGNREGA and one LPG connection per house is provided in convergence with Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana. Payments are made electronically directly to bank accounts or post office accounts linked to Aadhaar.

# Funding
Funding is shared between the Central and State Governments in a ratio of 60:40 for plain areas, and 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan States.
