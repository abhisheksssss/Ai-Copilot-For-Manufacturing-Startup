# Overview
CITIIS 2.0 is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs in partnership with the French Development Agency (AFD), Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau (KfW), the European Union (EU), and National Institute of Urban Affairs (NIUA). It is part of the Smart Cities Mission, focusing on promoting integrated waste management and climate-oriented reform actions.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it supports competitively selected projects in smart cities.

# Benefits
The scheme provides financial and technical support for developing projects focused on building climate resilience, adaptation, and mitigation in up to 18 smart cities. It also supports climate action at the state level and interventions at all three levels—Centre, State, and City—to further climate governance in urban India.

# Funding
Component 1: Financial and technical support for developing projects focused on building climate resilience, adaptation, and mitigation in up to 18 smart cities.
Component 2: Support for climate action provided to the States.
Component 3: Interventions at all three levels; Centre, State, and City, to further climate governance in urban India.
