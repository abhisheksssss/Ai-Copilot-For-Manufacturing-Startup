# Overview
The Biotechnology Innovation Fund – Accelerating Entrepreneurs (AcE) is a 'fund of funds' that aims to partner with fund managers and invest in SEBI-registered AIFs to support biotech startups.

# Eligibility
The AcE Fund invests and partners with SEBI-registered Category I and II AIFs in the biotech sector, as well as startups and SMEs in subsectors such as Healthcare, Life Sciences, Pharma, Biopharma, Med-tech, Diagnostics, Industrial-Biotech, Agri-tech, Bio-IT/Services, and allied areas.

# Benefits
The AcE Fund provides a maximum capital commitment of up to ₹30 crore or up to 30% of the total aggregate capital commitment amount in each Daughter Fund, with a ticket size of up to ₹7 crore and additional follow-on funding of up to ₹3.5 crore.

# Funding
The AcE Fund is a 'fund of funds' that invests in SEBI-registered AIFs, which in turn invest in biotech startups.
