2. **Incentives for MSMEs in Uttar Pradesh:**
   - **Eligibility Criteria:** Minimum investment in plant & machinery up to INR 500 million and turnover up to INR 2,500 million.
   - **Key Incentives:**
     - **Capital Subsidy:** 15-25%, limited to INR 40 million.
     - **Interest Subsidy:** 50% for five years, subject to a ceiling of INR 2.5 million (applicable only for micro units).
     - **Stamp Duty Exemption:** 50-100% exemption.
