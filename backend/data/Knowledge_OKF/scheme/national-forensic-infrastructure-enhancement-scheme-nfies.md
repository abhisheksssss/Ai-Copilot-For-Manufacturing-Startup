# Overview
The NATIONAL FORENSIC INFRASTRUCTURE ENHANCEMENT SCHEME (NFIES) will be operated by the Union Home Ministry and is a central sector scheme with 100% financing from the Central Government, having a total outlay of Rs 2254.3 crores. The scheme will run from 2024-25 to 2028-29.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not specified in the provided text.

# Benefits
The benefits include:
- Establishment of campuses of National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU) in India.
- Establishment of Central Forensic Science Laboratories in India.
- Enhancement of existing infrastructure at the Delhi Campus of NFSU.

# Funding
100% financing from the Central Government with a total outlay of Rs 2254.3 crores.
