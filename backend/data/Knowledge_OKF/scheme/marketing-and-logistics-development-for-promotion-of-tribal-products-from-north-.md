# Overview
The Marketing and Logistics Development for Promotion of Tribal Products from North Eastern Region (PTP-NER 2.0) is a Central sector scheme aimed at enhancing the livelihood opportunities for tribal artisans by improving the efficiency in procurement, logistics, and marketing of tribal products from North Eastern States.

# Eligibility
This scheme applies to the states of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, and Sikkim. The primary beneficiaries are tribal artisans within these regions.

# Benefits
The key features include organizing Taems in consultation with District Administration and other relevant organizations/departments to facilitate backward and forward linkages through incubation support, aggregation, skill and entrepreneurship development, sourcing and procurement, marketing, transportation, and publicity.

# Funding
The scheme is implemented by the Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation (TRIFED) along with North Eastern Handicrafts and Handlooms Development Corporation (NEHHDC), India, and other post and state government departments/agencies.
