# Overview
The Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a credit support programme under three categories: Shishu, Kishor, and Tarun.

# Eligibility
The scheme is available to non-corporate, non-farm micro/small enterprises, including early-stage businesses and startups.

# Benefits
The scheme offers loans up to ₹10 lakh, with Tarun Plus offering up to ₹20 lakh for eligible repeat borrowers.

# Funding
The scheme provides term loans and working capital through banks, microfinance institutions (MFIs), and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).
