# Overview
The PM YOUNG ACHIEVERS’ SCHOLARSHIP AWARD SCHEME FOR VIBRANT INDIA (PM YASASVI) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme aimed at awarding scholarships to eligible candidates from Other Backward Class (OBC), Economically Backward Class (EBC), and De-Notified, Nomadic & Semi Nomadic Tribes (DNT/SNT) categories.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates must have an annual income not exceeding Rs. 2,50,000 from all sources. Students can avail Pre-Matric Scholarship from Class 9 to 10 and Post Matric Scholarship for their higher studies at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage.

# Benefits
Under this Scheme, students who excel in their studies get an opportunity to study in top-class schools and colleges. Hostel facilities are also provided to OBC students.

# Funding
The scheme provides scholarships ranging from Rs 75,000 to Rs 1,25,000.
