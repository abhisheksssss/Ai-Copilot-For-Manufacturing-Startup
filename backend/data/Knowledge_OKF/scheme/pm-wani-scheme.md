# Overview
PM-WANI (Prime Minister’s Wi-Fi Access Network Interface) is a national public Wi-Fi framework enabling affordable, widespread broadband access through decentralized Wi-Fi hotspots operated by small entrepreneurs. It was approved by the Union Cabinet on 9 December 2020 and aims to support the goals of the National Digital Communications Policy (NDCP) 2018.

# Eligibility
Small shops and local businesses are eligible to operate Wi-Fi hotspots under PM-WANI without needing any license, fee, or formal registration. They can use regular fibre-to-the-home broadband connections to reduce operational costs.

# Benefits
- Democratization of internet access.
- Promotion of digital inclusion.
- Nationwide network of public Wi-Fi hotspots.
- Seamless roaming between hotspots operated by different PDO Aggregators.
- Mobile data offload from telecom operators to Wi-Fi networks.
- Affordable bandwidth for PDOs as per TRAI rules.
