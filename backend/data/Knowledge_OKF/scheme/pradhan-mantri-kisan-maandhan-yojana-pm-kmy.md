# Overview
Launched in 2019, the PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN MAANDHAN YOJANA (PM-KMY) aims to provide social security to all land-holding Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) across the country. This is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme managed by the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC).

# Eligibility
Small and marginal farmers aged between 18 and 40 years are eligible for this scheme if they have cultivable land holdings of up to 2 hectares and are listed in state/UT land records. Contributions range from Rs. 55 to Rs. 200 per month until the farmer turns 60, with the Central Government contributing an equal amount.

# Benefits
Once farmers reach the age of 60, they receive a monthly pension of Rs. 3,000. If a subscriber passes away while receiving their pension, their spouse will be entitled to a family pension of Rs. 1,500 per month (only if the spouse is not already a beneficiary).

# Funding
The scheme involves contributions from both eligible subscribers and the Central Government, with the LIC managing the pension fund.
