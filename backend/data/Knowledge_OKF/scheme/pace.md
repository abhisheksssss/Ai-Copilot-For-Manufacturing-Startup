# Overview
The PACE programme, which stands for Promoting Academic Research Conversion to Enterprise, is a BIRAC programme that aids in the translation of academic biotech research into commercial pathways.

# Eligibility
Academic researchers, institutions, and industry partners are eligible to participate in the PACE programme, which focuses on translating biotech research.

# Benefits
The PACE programme provides project-based grants, commonly up to ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore, depending on the PACE component, to support the validation and commercialization of academic biotech research.

# Funding
The funding for the PACE programme is provided by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) in partnership with BIRAC, offering grant and partnership support for validation and transnational research.
