# Overview
ECMS is a flagship incentive scheme to promote domestic manufacturing of electronic components, sub-assemblies, and capital equipment, reducing import dependence in India’s electronics sector.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it targets specific segments such as PCBs, Camera Modules, Copper-Clad Laminates, Polypropylene Films, and electronics capital equipment.

# Benefits
Incentives include:
- Turnover-linked incentives over a 6-year period (including a 1-year gestation period)
- Capital expenditure (Capex) incentives for 5 years
- Performance-based payouts linked to incremental production and employment

Strategic targets aim to achieve 100% domestic demand for Copper-Clad Laminates, 20% for PCBs, and 15% for Camera Modules.

# Funding
The scheme has a total outlay of ₹22,919 crore. The tenure includes:
- Turnover-linked incentive: 6 years (including 1-year gestation period)
- Capex incentive: 5 years
