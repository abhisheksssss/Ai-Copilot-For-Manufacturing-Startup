# Overview
NAP-AMR 2.0 is India’s updated five-year strategic framework (2025–29) to combat rising antimicrobial resistance through a One Health approach covering human, animal, agriculture, and environmental sectors.

# Benefits
- Creates a coordinated, multi-sectoral national response to AMR using a One Health framework.
- Reduces misuse/overuse of antimicrobials.
- Strengthens laboratory surveillance, stewardship, and infection control.
- Focuses on expanding diagnostic networks, AMR labs, pathogen tracking, and infection prevention and control (IPC) across hospitals.
- Controls irrational antibiotic use; promotes prescription auditing.
- Supports state actions like banning OTC antibiotic sales.
- Nationwide awareness campaigns, medical curriculum integration, and training programs for healthcare providers, veterinarians & pharmacists.
- Regulates antimicrobial/pesticide usage in crops; monitors pharmaceutical waste; promotes safe livestock practices.
- Strengthens the India AMR Innovation Hub to develop new diagnostics, technologies, and collaborative R&D models.
