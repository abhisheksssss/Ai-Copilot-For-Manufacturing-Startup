# Overview
The PM National Dialysis Programme (PMNDP) is a flagship scheme under the National Health Mission (NHM), launched to provide free dialysis services to economically vulnerable patients suffering from end-stage renal disease (ESRD). Initiated in April 2016 under Union Budget 2016–17, it has been implemented via Public-Private Partnership (PPP) and hybrid models.

# Eligibility
Primarily caters to Below Poverty Line (BPL) patients. However, the infrastructure benefits all ESRD patients.

# Benefits
The programme aims to provide equitable, affordable dialysis care across India, reduce financial burden on BPL families, and strengthen renal-care infrastructure in district hospitals and beyond.

# Funding
Implemented via Public-Private Partnership (PPP) and hybrid models. The scheme has functional presence in 751 districts across 36 States/UTs with 1,704 dialysis centres operational as of June 2025. Services are extended to Community Health Centres in tribal and remote areas.
