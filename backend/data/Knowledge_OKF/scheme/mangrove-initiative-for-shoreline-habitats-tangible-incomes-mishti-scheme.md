# Overview
The MANGROVE INITIATIVE FOR SHORELINE HABITATS & TANGIBLE INCOMES (MISHTI) SCHEME aims to enhance mangrove coverage along coastlines and saltpan lands, thereby improving ecological health, protecting against natural disasters, and supporting local livelihoods. The programme operates through convergence between MGNREGS, CAMPA Fund, and other sources.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include states and local communities that are involved in mangrove plantation and rehabilitation activities.

# Benefits
The scheme provides financial assistance for:
- Mangrove plantation and rehabilitation.
- Promotion of sustainable mangrove management practices.
- Research on mangrove ecology and conservation.

# Funding
Funding is provided through a convergence of MGNREGS, CAMPA Fund, and other sources.
