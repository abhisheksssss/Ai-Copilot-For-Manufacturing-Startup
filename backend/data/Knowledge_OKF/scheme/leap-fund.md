# Overview
The LEAP Fund is a scheme for biotech startups, providing equity/equity-linked funding for commercialisation of products. It is a part of the BIRAC-BioNEST initiative.

# Eligibility
Biotech startups incubated at selected LEAP/BioNEST fund partners are eligible to apply for the LEAP Fund.

# Benefits
The LEAP Fund provides equity/equity-linked investment to biotech startups, supporting them in piloting and launching their products in the market.

# Funding
The fund provides up to ₹1 crore per startup.
