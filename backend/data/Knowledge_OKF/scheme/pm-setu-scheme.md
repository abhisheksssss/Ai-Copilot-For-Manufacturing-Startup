# Overview
PM-SETU (Prime Minister’s Scheme for Empowerment through Technology Upgradation) is a centrally sponsored flagship initiative aimed at transforming India's skill ecosystem through modernized, industry-driven Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs).

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text.

# Benefits
The scheme aims to upgrade 1,000 government ITIs nationwide and align them with current and future skill needs of industries. It also seeks to bridge the gap between classroom learning and global employability, enabling India's youth to meet international skill demands.

# Funding
The total project cost is ₹60,000 crore over 2025–28, jointly supported by the Centre and States.
