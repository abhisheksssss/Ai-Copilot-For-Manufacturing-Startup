# Overview
NIDHI–EIR is a fellowship scheme that gives young people with startup ideas monthly financial support, workspace, and mentoring so they can turn their idea into a business. The initiative is executed at selected NIDHI-Technology Business Incubator (TBI) known as Programme Executing Partner (PEP).

# Eligibility
The entrepreneur should be an Indian citizen, have completed minimum 4 years of formal full-time undergraduate/post-graduate education or 3 years degree or diploma program, and have 2-year full-time work experience post-degree or diploma.

# Benefits
* Grant ranging from ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 per month for a period of 12 months, extendable up to 18 months.
* Co-working spaces for developing the idea into a marketable product.
* Guidance from experienced, innovative, and highly successful entrepreneurs on the business concept, strategy, or venture and insight into specific industries or markets.

# Funding
The selected NIDHI-TBI will receive a grant of ₹39.6 lakh each year, of which ₹36 lakh is to be provided to the NIDHI EIRs.
