# Overview
The Niveshak Shivir Initiative is a program under the Ministry of Heavy Industries aimed at providing comprehensive training and mentorship to entrepreneurs in the heavy industries sector.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants include individuals or teams who are interested in starting or expanding businesses within the heavy industries domain.

# Benefits
Participants will benefit from:
- Intensive training sessions on various aspects of business management.
- Mentorship from experienced professionals in the industry.
- Networking opportunities with key stakeholders and potential investors.

# Funding
The scheme does not provide direct funding but offers participants access to resources, networks, and mentorship that can be instrumental in their entrepreneurial journey.
