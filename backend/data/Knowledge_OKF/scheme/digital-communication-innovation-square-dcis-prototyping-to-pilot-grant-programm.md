# Overview
The Digital Communication Innovation Square (DCIS) Prototyping to Pilot Grant Programme is a grant initiative by the Department of Telecommunications aimed at supporting startups, MSMEs, academia, and innovators in developing telecom and digital communication solutions.

# Eligibility
Startups, MSMEs, academia, and innovators working on indigenous telecom and ICT product innovation are eligible to apply for this programme.

# Benefits
Selected proposals will receive up to ₹50 lakh per proposal for prototyping and piloting their innovations. Additionally, mentoring and testing support will be provided.

# Funding
The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) offers grant-in-aid funding along with mentoring and testing support for the selected proposals.
