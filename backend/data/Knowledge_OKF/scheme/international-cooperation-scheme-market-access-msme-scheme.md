# Overview
The International Cooperation Scheme Market Access MSME Scheme is designed to support MSMEs, industry associations, and startup MSMEs in seeking export/market linkages by participating in international exhibitions, trade fairs, and buyer-seller meets.

# Eligibility
The scheme is open to MSMEs, industry associations, and startup MSMEs seeking export/market linkages.

# Benefits
The scheme provides reimbursement/subsidy for travel, stall, registration, and event costs, with the amount of assistance varying by event category and guidelines.

# Funding
The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of reimbursement/subsidy to support MSMEs in participating in international events.
