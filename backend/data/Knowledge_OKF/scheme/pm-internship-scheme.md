# Overview
The Prime Minister’s Internship Scheme was announced in the Budget 2024-25 as a pilot project to provide 1.25 lakh internship opportunities for the Financial Year 2024-25.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates must be Indian nationals aged between 21 and 24 years, not employed full-time or engaged in full-time education. They should have passed High School or Higher Secondary School, hold a certificate from ITI, diploma from polytechnic institute, or graduated with degrees such as BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, BBA, B.Pharma, etc. Family income must be less than Rs 8 Lakh. Graduates from premier institutions (IITs, IIMs) or holders of qualifications such as CA, BDS, MBBS, or any master’s/higher degree are not eligible.

# Benefits
Participants will receive a monthly stipend of ₹5,000 during the internship period through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Additionally, they will be provided with a one-time grant of ₹6,000 through DBT. The scheme aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements, enhancing employability and skills among participants.

# Funding
The scheme provides a monthly stipend of ₹5,000 during the internship period and a one-time grant of ₹6,000 through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
