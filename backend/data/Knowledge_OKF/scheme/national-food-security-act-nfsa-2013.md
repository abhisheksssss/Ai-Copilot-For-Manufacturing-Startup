# Overview
The National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013, is a scheme under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution aimed at providing food security to eligible citizens through the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS).

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the NFSA are based on the Antyodaya Anna Yojana and other relevant provisions.

# Benefits
Beneficiaries receive subsidized food grains under the TPDS, ensuring access to essential nutrition.

# Funding
Funding is provided through the Price Stabilization Fund (PSF) and other government allocations.
