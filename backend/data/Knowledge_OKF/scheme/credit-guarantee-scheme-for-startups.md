# Overview
The Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) provides credit support guarantee cover to lenders for collateral-free loans extended to eligible DPIIT-recognised startups borrowing from eligible banks, NBFCs, AIFs.

# Eligibility
The scheme is available to DPIIT-recognised startups that borrow from eligible banks, NBFCs, AIFs.

# Benefits
The scheme provides credit guarantee cover up to ₹20 crore per eligible borrower, reducing lender risk and enabling startups to get a loan without collateral.

# Funding
The scheme provides a credit guarantee cover, which is not a direct grant, but helps startups get a loan without collateral.
