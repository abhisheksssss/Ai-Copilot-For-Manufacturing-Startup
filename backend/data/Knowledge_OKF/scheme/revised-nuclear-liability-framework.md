# Overview
Retains the no-fault liability principle for operators and compulsory insurance coverage.

# Eligibility
No specific eligibility criteria mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
- Introduces a tiered liability cap based on reactor capacity, ranging from ₹100 crore to ₹3,000 crore (replacing the flat ₹1,500 crore cap).
- Central government bears excess liability beyond the operator’s cap.
- Retains liability exclusions such as certain natural disasters and claims adjudication mechanisms.
