# Overview
The Market Access Support (MAS) Intervention is a government-backed programme that provides financial and institutional assistance to Indian exporters for accessing and expanding in international markets through curated trade and buyer-engagement activities.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs, first-time exporters, and priority sectors. The scheme focuses on mandatory 35% MSME participation with a prioritization of smaller and new exporters.

# Benefits
- Support for Buyer-Seller Meets (BSMs), Mega Reverse BSMs, international trade fairs, exhibitions, and trade delegations.
- A 3–5 year rolling calendar of approved market access events to ensure continuity and predictability.
- Financial rationalisation with revised cost-sharing norms, event-wise financial ceilings, and partial airfare support for exporters with turnover up to ₹75 lakh.
- Digital governance through end-to-end online processes via trade.gov.in, including approvals, fund release, monitoring, and feedback.
- Outcome tracking with mandatory online feedback on buyer quality, leads generated, and market relevance.

# Funding
The scheme offers financial assistance in the form of support for various market access activities, revised cost-sharing norms, event-wise financial ceilings, and partial airfare support. The Department of Commerce, Ministry of MSME, and Ministry of Finance jointly implement this initiative under the NIRYAT DISHA sub-scheme as part of the Export Promotion Mission (EPM).
