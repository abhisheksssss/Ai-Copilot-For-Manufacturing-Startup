# Overview
The GYAN BHARATAM MISSION for Manuscript Conservation is a nodal ministry initiative under the Ministry of Culture, aimed at focusing on the survey, documentation, and conservation of India's manuscript heritage.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include academic institutions, museums, libraries, and private collectors holding more than one crore manuscripts.

# Benefits
- National Digital Repository for Indian knowledge systems.
- Increased budget allocation from ₹3.5 crore to ₹60 crore.
- Conservation using modern and indigenous methods.
- Training of new manuscript conservators.
- Promotion of access through digitization and publication.

# Funding
The mission will be executed under the National Mission for Manuscripts (NMM), with a significant increase in funding.
