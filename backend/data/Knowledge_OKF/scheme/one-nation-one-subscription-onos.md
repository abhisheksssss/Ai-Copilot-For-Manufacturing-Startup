# Overview
ONOS is an initiative by the INFLIBNET Centre to facilitate better access to academic resources for India's higher education institutions (HEIs). The scheme aims to democratize access to knowledge and promote research in India, stemming from the National Education Policy 2020.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include nearly 6,300 government-run institutions such as universities, colleges, research bodies, and Institutions of National Importance (INIs), including those in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Private HEIs are not currently covered under the scheme but may be included in the future.

# Benefits
- **Enhanced Accessibility**: Expands access to over 13,000 scholarly journals published by 30 international publishers for nearly 1.8 crore students, faculty, and researchers.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Reduces duplication of journal subscriptions across different library consortia and individual HEIs, leading to cost savings.
- **Data-driven Insights**: Allows the government to analyze journal download and usage data to improve resource allocation.

# Funding
The scheme is implemented by INFLIBNET Centre, an Inter-University Centre under the University Grants Commission in Gandhinagar. The funding mechanism details are not provided in the given text.
