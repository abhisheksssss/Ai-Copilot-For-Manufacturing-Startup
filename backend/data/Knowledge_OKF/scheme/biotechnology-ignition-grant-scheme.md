# Overview
The Biotechnology Ignition Grant Scheme (BIG) is a flagship grant by BIRAC for translating innovative biotech ideas into proof-of-concept, from ideation to PoC.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators and early biotech startups with commercially promising ideas are eligible for this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant-in-aid of up to ₹50 lakh for up to 18 months, along with mentoring, and does not require equity dilution.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC, with a grant of up to ₹50 lakh for up to 18 months.
