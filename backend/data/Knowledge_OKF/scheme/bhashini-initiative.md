# Overview
BHASHINI Initiative is a pathbreaking initiative aimed at democratizing access to digital content and services across India's linguistic spectrum using cutting-edge artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP) technologies.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it can be inferred that any user of digital devices who wishes to access content and services in their preferred Indian language is eligible.

# Benefits
Users can access content and services in 22 scheduled Indian languages through BHASHINI's dedicated Android and iOS applications, ensuring a seamless and interactive user experience.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. The initiative was launched under the National Language Translation Mission (NLTM) in 2022.
