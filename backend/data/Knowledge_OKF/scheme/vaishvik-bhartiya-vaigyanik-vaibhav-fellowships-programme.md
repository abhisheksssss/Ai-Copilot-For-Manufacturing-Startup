# Overview
The VAISHVIK BHARTIYA VAIGYANIK (VAIBHAV) Fellowships Programme is implemented by the Department of Science and Technology (DST). It aims to award outstanding scientists/technologists of Indian origin who are engaged in research activities abroad. The programme seeks to foster collaboration between these scientists and institutions within India.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates for the VAIBHAV Fellowship are outstanding scientists/technologists of Indian origin, including Non-Resident Indians (NRI), Overseas Citizens of India (OCI), and Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) who are currently engaged in research activities in their respective countries.

# Benefits
VAIBHAV Fellows will have access to the following benefits:
- A fellowship grant of INR 4,00,000 per month.
- International and domestic travel allowances.
- Accommodation support.
- Contingency funds for unforeseen expenses.

Additionally, fellows are expected to collaborate with their Indian counterparts and initiate research activities in cutting-edge areas of Science and Technology at the host institution.

# Funding
The fellowship grant (INR 4,00,000 per month), international and domestic travel, accommodation, and contingencies are funded by the Department of Science and Technology.
