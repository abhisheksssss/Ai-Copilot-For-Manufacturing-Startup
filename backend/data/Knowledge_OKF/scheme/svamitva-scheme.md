# Overview
SVAMITVA stands for Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas. The scheme was launched on National Panchayati Raj Day, 24th April 2021, by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

# Eligibility
The applicant should have a property in rural inhabited (Abadi) area.

# Benefits
- Establishment of clear ownership of property in rural inhabited areas.
- Issuance of legal ownership cards (Property cards/Title deeds).
- Mapping accuracy of 5 cm using Drone survey and CORS Networks.
