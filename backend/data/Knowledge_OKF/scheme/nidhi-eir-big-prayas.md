# Overview
The NIDHI-EIR, BIG, and PRAYAS schemes are government initiatives designed to support startups in various stages of their lifecycle, from ideation to market access and IP protection. These schemes aim to provide financial and technical assistance to entrepreneurs, helping them to develop and scale their innovative ideas.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for these schemes vary, but generally, they are open to startups and entrepreneurs who have innovative ideas or prototypes in various stages of development, from ideation to market-ready products.

# Benefits
The benefits of these schemes include financial support, mentorship, and access to resources and networks, which can help startups to develop and scale their products, enter new markets, and protect their intellectual property.

# Funding
The funding for these schemes is provided by the government, and the amount of funding varies depending on the scheme and the stage of the startup.
