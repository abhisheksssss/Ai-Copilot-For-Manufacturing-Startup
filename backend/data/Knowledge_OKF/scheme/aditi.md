# Overview
ADITI is a sub-scheme under iDEX, focusing on the development of innovative technologies with larger R&D investments, specifically for critical and strategic defence technologies.

# Eligibility
Startups, MSMEs, and innovators developing advanced deep-tech defence technologies are eligible for ADITI.

# Benefits
The benefits of ADITI include a grant-in-aid of up to ₹25 crore per project, as well as user engagement under the iDEX framework, providing a large R&D grant.

# Funding
ADITI provides a grant-in-aid of up to ₹25 crore per project.
