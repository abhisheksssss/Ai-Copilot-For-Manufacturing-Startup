# Overview
The BIRAC SEED Fund is a government scheme that provides seed funding to promising biotech startups through BioNEST incubators.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for BIRAC/BioNEST-incubated biotech startups that require early commercialization capital.

# Benefits
The scheme provides up to ₹30 lakh per startup in the form of equity or equity-linked seed support.

# Funding
The funding for the scheme is provided by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC, with a typical incubator corpus of approximately ₹2 crore.
