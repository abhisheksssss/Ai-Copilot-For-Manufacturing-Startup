# Overview
Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) is a free foodgrain distribution scheme under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013, launched in March 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic to provide relief to vulnerable populations.

# Eligibility
Eligible households are categorized into two types:
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households: receive 35 kg foodgrains per family per month.
- Priority Households (PHH): receive 5 kg rice/wheat per person per month.

# Benefits
The scheme provides free rice and wheat to eligible households through the Public Distribution System (PDS). It covers approximately 81.35 crore beneficiaries, with 75% in rural areas and 50% in urban areas. The distribution is carried out through around 5.4 lakh fair price shops (FPSs) across India.

# Funding
The scheme has a budgeted food subsidy of ₹2.03 lakh crore for the fiscal year 2026. Additionally, there are ongoing efforts to re-verify beneficiaries to ensure that only eligible individuals receive the benefits.
