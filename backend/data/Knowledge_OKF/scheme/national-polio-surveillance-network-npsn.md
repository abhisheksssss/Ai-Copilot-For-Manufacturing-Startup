# Overview
The National Polio Surveillance Network (NPSN) is India’s key disease surveillance mechanism for detecting and controlling polio and other vaccine-preventable diseases.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it involves all states and union territories through its field units led by Surveillance Medical Officers (SMOs).

# Benefits
- Supports polio eradication by identifying and investigating cases of Acute Flaccid Paralysis (AFP).
- Monitors immunisation campaigns to ensure zero transmission of wild polio virus.
- Trains state and district health officials on surveillance protocols.
- Assists in pulse polio campaigns and routine immunisation.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the given text. However, it is a government initiative involving the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and the World Health Organization (WHO).
