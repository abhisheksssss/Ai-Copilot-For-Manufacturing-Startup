# Overview
JANANI SURAKSHA YOJANA was launched in 2005 as a centrally sponsored scheme under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality by promoting institutional delivery through financial incentives.

# Eligibility
- Pregnant women, especially from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and BPL households.
- Women must be above 19 years of age.
- Only women with up to two live births are eligible under the scheme.
- SC/ST women can receive cash incentives in both low and high-performing States.
- Women from BPL households can only get benefits in high-performing States.

# Benefits
- An expecting mother receives ₹1,400 in rural areas and ₹1,000 in urban areas after delivering at a public health facility or an Accredited Private Hospital.
- The cash benefit is disbursed to the beneficiary preferably at the institution. If ASHA is unable to organise transport, disbursement of transport assistance should be done in the health centre as soon as pregnant women arrive and register for delivery.

# Funding
The scheme is centrally sponsored with a focus on low-performing States (mainly in north India and north-east).
