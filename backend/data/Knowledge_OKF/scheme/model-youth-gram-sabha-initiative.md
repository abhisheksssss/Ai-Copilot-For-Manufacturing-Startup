# Overview
The Model Youth Gram Sabha (MYGS) is a nationwide initiative aimed at providing students hands-on experience in grassroots democracy by simulating the functioning of real Gram Sabhas. It encourages civic awareness, leadership, and participatory governance among youth.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it can be inferred that the scheme targets school-going students across 1,000+ schools nationwide.

# Benefits
- Nurtures democratic leadership among students through experiential and activity-based learning.
- Aligns with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 in fostering responsible, participative, and community-oriented citizens.
- Promotes learning by doing, teamwork, transparency, and decision-making through mock Gram Sabha sessions.

# Funding
The initiative is supported by Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs), Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRSs), and State Government Schools. No specific funding details are provided in the text.
