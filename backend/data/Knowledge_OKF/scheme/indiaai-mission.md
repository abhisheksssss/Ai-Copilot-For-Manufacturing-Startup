# Overview
The IndiaAI Mission aims to develop AI infrastructure and foster innovation through a public-private partnership model led by the 'IndiaAI' Independent Business Division (IBD) under Digital India Corporation (DIC).

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text.

# Benefits
- Development of AI compute infrastructure with 10,000 or more GPUs.
- Deployment and development of indigenous Large Multimodal Models and domain-specific foundational models.
- Provision of a one-stop solution for accessing non-personal datasets through AIKosha.
- Promotion of AI applications in critical sectors.
- Mitigation of barriers to entry into AI programs by increasing courses at various levels and setting up Data and AI Labs.
- Streamlined access to funding for deep-tech AI start-ups.
