# Overview
Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX) is a challenge-led defence innovation platform that connects startups, MSMEs, and individual innovators to solve defence problems.

# Eligibility
Eligible applicants include:
- Defence & aerospace startups/MSMEs
- Armed forces
- Defence PSUs

# Benefits
Participants receive:
- Prototype grants up to ₹1.5 crore
- User mentoring
- Procurement pathway up to ₹10 crore

# Funding
Funding is provided in the form of prototype grants, with a maximum amount of up to ₹10 crore for procurement.
