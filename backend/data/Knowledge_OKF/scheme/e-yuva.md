# Overview
E-YUVA is a programme encouraging students and young researchers to pursue translational biotech innovation, providing support through E-YUVA centres.

# Eligibility
Undergraduate/postgraduate students, fellows, and young innovators are eligible for the E-YUVA programme.

# Benefits
The programme provides a common fellowship, innovation support of up to ₹5 lakh per project, and mentoring.

# Funding
The funding for the programme is provided by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC, with a grant of up to ₹5 lakh per project.
