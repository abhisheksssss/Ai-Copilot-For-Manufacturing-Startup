# Overview
GREAT stands for Grant for Research & Entrepreneurship across Aspiring Innovators in Technical Textiles Prototype to Commercialisation. It is a startup grant under the National Technical Textiles Mission to convert prototypes into marketable technologies/products.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators and startups in technical textiles, associated with approved incubators, are eligible for this grant.

# Benefits
The grant provides up to ₹50 lakh for up to 18 months, with grant-in-aid (no royalty/equity) and incubator support.

# Funding
The funding for this scheme is provided by the Ministry of Textiles under the National Technical Textiles Mission.
