# Overview
PRADHAN MANTRI UJJWALA YOJANA (PMUY) is a scheme launched by the Government of India under the Ministry of Jal Shakti to provide LPG connections to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households.

# Eligibility
Eligible beneficiaries are BPL households identified through the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC).

# Benefits
The scheme provides an LPG connection, along with a one-time subsidy for the first cylinder and refills up to 8 cylinders free of cost within the first year. Subsequent refills are provided at a reduced rate.

# Funding
Funding is provided by the Central Government, with partial support from state governments as well.
