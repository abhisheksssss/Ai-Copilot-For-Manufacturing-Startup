# Overview
The Atal Community Innovation Centres (ACICs) Ideation to Incubation programme aims to set up community innovation centres in underserved regions, supporting institutions and organisations that foster early-stage startups.

# Eligibility
Institutions or organisations that support community innovators and early-stage startups are eligible for this programme.

# Benefits
Participants will benefit from grant support to host the centres and develop an incubation ecosystem. Additionally, they can receive up to ₹2.5 crore over a prescribed period.

# Funding
Up to ₹2.5 crore is available over the prescribed period for setting up community innovation centres and fostering an incubation ecosystem.
