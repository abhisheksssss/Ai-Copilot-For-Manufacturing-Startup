# Overview
The MERI LiFE (MY LIFE) APP is designed to showcase the impact of individual actions on environmental conservation, particularly among young people. It aims to catalyze a national movement for Mission LiFE by encouraging participation in various activities.

# Eligibility
Eligible participants include all citizens, with a focus on young individuals and communities.

# Benefits
Participants are motivated through engagement in five categories: conserving energy, preserving water, minimizing single-use plastics, embracing sustainable food practices, and cultivating healthy lifestyles. The app provides a platform for tracking progress and fostering a sense of community involvement.
