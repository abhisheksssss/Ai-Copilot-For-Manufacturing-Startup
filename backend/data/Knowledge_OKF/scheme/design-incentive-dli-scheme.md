# Overview
The Design Incentive (DLI) Scheme is a government initiative aimed at supporting domestic companies, startups, and MSMEs engaged in semiconductor design by providing infrastructure support and financial incentives.

# Eligibility
Eligible entities include:
- Semiconductor design companies
- Startups
- MSMEs

These entities must be involved in the design of IC/chipset/SOC/IP-core.

# Benefits
The scheme offers:
- Design infrastructure support
- Reimbursement or deployment-linked incentives for eligible expenditure
- Deployment incentive at 4–6% of net sales (with a cap of ₹30 crore)

# Funding
Eligible entities can receive:
- Reimbursement / deployment-linked incentives for eligible expenditure
- A deployment incentive of up to 4–6% of net sales, with a maximum of ₹30 crore.
