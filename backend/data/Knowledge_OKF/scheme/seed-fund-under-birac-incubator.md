# Overview
The SEED Fund under BIRAC Incubator provides seed equity/equity-linked support to promising biotech startups through BioNEST incubators.

# Eligibility
Biotech startups that are incubated by BIRAC/BioNEST and require early commercialization capital are eligible for this fund.

# Benefits
Startups can receive up to ₹30 lakh per startup. The incubator corpus typically ranges around ₹2 crore.

# Funding
Funding is provided in the form of equity/equity-linked instruments, with a maximum amount of ₹30 lakh per startup.
