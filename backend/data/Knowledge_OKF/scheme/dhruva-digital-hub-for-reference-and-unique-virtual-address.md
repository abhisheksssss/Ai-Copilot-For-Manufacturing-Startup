# Overview
DHRUVA is a national Digital Address Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) that standardizes, digitizes, and virtualizes physical addresses through secure, consent-driven sharing using UPI-like address labels.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it can be inferred that all citizens and businesses within India may benefit from this scheme.

# Benefits
- Unified, interoperable, secure, and user-controlled digital address ecosystem.
- Address-as-a-Service (AaaS) for government, businesses, and citizens.
- UPI-like Address Labels: Users get a virtual address such as “name@entity” to reduce the need to fill address forms repeatedly.
- Consent-Based Access: Companies can access users' geocoded or textual addresses only with time-bound authorization.
