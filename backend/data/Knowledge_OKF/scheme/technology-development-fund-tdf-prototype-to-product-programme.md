# Overview
The Technology Development Fund (TDF) Prototype to Product Programme is a grant initiative by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) in collaboration with the Ministry of Defence, aimed at supporting indigenous defence technology development by Indian industries, particularly Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and startups.

# Eligibility
Indian industries, MSMEs, and startups developing technologies for DRDO or service needs are eligible to apply for this programme.

# Benefits
Selected projects receive grant funding from the TDF. The maximum grant amount per project is up to ₹10 crore, with a general cap of 90% of the total project cost. Additionally, these projects benefit from mentoring and guidance provided by DRDO experts.

# Funding
The fund provides grants ranging up to ₹10 crore per project, typically covering 90% of the project costs. The funding is intended to support the development phase of technologies that meet defence requirements or are relevant to military services.
