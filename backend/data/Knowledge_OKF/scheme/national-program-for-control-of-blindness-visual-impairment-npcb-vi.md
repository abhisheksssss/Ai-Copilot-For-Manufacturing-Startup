# Overview
The National Program for Control of Blindness & Visual Impairment (NPCB&VI) was initiated to address the issue of blindness and visual impairment in India.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly mentioned in the provided text. However, it is aimed at individuals suffering from blindness or visual impairment who can benefit from the program's initiatives.

# Benefits
The program aims to reduce new infections by 50% (2007 baseline of NACP III) and provide comprehensive care and support to all persons living with HIV/AIDS along with treatment services for those requiring it. It also includes community-based testing and tracing of individuals who are left to follow up.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the text, but the program is part of larger initiatives like NACP IV which started in 2012.
