# Overview

Mera Gaon Meri Dharohar' (MGMD) Initiative is a nationwide cultural mapping project launched in June 2023 under the Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav celebrations, aimed at documenting and preserving traditional knowledge systems, rituals, oral traditions, festivals, and local art forms across 6.5 lakh villages.

# Eligibility

The initiative is open to all Indian villages with a focus on intangible cultural heritage.

# Benefits

- Mapping, preservation, and digital archiving of traditional knowledge systems.
- Development of MGMD Web Portal as the National Cultural Workplace (NCWP).
- User-editable data, artist registrations, UICs, and link to welfare schemes.
- Part of the National Mission on Cultural Mapping which aims to digitally map India’s cultural ecosystem and empower artist communities.

# Funding

Funding details are not provided in the given text. However, the initiative is implemented by IGNCA (Indian National Gandhi Institute for Cultural Archives) with support from the Ministry of Culture, Government of India.
