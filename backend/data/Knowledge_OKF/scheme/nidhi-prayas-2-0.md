# Overview
NIDHI – PRAYAS 2.0 is a programme that helps innovators convert technology ideas into working prototypes through PRAYAS Centres.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators and early-stage startups with technology-based ideas and prototype plans are eligible for the programme.

# Benefits
The programme provides a grant of up to ₹40 lakh through PRAYAS 2.0 calls, along with prototype grant, maker-space, and mentoring support.

# Funding
The programme is funded by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) and provides a grant of up to ₹40 lakh.
