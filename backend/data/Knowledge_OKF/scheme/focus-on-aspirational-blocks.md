# Overview
The Focus on Aspirational Blocks Programme (ABP) targets the most underdeveloped blocks across 27 states and 4 Union Territories, aiming to transform these areas through various development initiatives.

# Eligibility
Blocks are selected based on their level of backwardness, infrastructure gaps, and socio-economic indicators. The programme covers a total of 500 blocks.

# Benefits
The ABP focuses on improving infrastructure, education, health, and livelihood opportunities in the targeted blocks. It aims to uplift the quality of life for residents by addressing critical development needs.

# Funding
Funding is provided through a combination of central government grants, state government contributions, and private sector participation. The exact allocation varies based on the specific needs of each block.
