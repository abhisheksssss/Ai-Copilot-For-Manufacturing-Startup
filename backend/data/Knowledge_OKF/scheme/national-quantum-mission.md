# Overview
The National Quantum Mission (NQM) is a national mission that offers rolling startup grants for deep-tech R&D in quantum technology through 4 Thematic Hubs at IISc, IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, and IIT Delhi.

# Eligibility
DPIIT-recognised startups in quantum computing, communication, sensing/metrology, and materials/devices are eligible for the scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹25 crore per startup, along with incubation, testbed access, and ecosystem mentoring via T-Hubs.

# Funding
The mission has an outlay of ₹6,003.65 crore (FY2024–FY2031).
