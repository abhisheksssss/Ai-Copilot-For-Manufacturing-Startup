# Overview
NAKSHA (National Geospatial Knowledge-based Land Survey of Urban Habitations) is a geospatial technology-driven city survey initiative under the existing Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme (DILRMP). The scheme aims to create and update land records in urban areas, ensuring transparency, efficiency, and accuracy in property ownership documentation.

# Eligibility
NAKSHA Scheme is implemented as a pilot project in 152 Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) across 26 States and 3 Union Territories. Cities selected for the pilot meet two criteria: an area less than 35 sq km and a population less than 2 lakhs.

# Benefits
- Modernize urban land records to ensure accurate, updated, and digitalized land ownership records.
- Enhance urban planning by facilitating smart city development and infrastructure planning.
- Reduce land disputes through clear, verifiable records.
- Foster transparency with the establishment of a Web-GIS-based IT system for land record management.
- Support sustainable development by improving urban governance and land resource management.

# Funding
The estimated cost of the pilot project is ₹194 crore, fully funded by the Government of India. The scheme utilizes drone-based land surveying for high-precision aerial surveys and a Web-GIS platform for end-to-end IT-based land record management. Citizens can also access digital land records for ease of living.
