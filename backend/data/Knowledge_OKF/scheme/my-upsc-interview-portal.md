# Overview
A new initiative launched during UPSC’s centenary year inviting serving and retired civil servants to share their interview experiences.

# Eligibility
Serving and retired civil servants are eligible to participate.

# Benefits
- To build a repository of real-life anecdotes for aspirants.
- Enhance transparency in recruitment.
- Preserve institutional memory.

# Funding
Not applicable as this is an initiative rather than a funding scheme.
