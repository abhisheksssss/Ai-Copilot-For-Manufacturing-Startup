# Overview
The IFSCA Fintech Incentive Scheme is designed to support fintech entities, startups, and accelerators operating or engaging with the GIFT IFSC ecosystem.

# Eligibility
Indian and foreign fintech entities, startups, and accelerators operating or engaging with GIFT IFSC are eligible to apply for this scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme offers grant/incentive support in various categories, including Sandbox, PoC, listing, accelerator, and special grants, with a maximum grant of up to ₹75 lakh.

# Funding
The funding for this scheme is provided by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA), with varying grant amounts depending on the incentive type.
