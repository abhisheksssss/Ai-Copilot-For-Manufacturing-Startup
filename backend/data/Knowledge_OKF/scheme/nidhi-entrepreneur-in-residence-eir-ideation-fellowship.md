# Overview
NIDHI – Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) Ideation Fellowship is a pre-startup fellowship that lets aspiring entrepreneurs work full-time on technology business ideas before incorporation. It is designed for graduate students and individual innovators with technology-based entrepreneurial ideas.

# Eligibility
Graduate students and individual innovators with technology-based entrepreneurial ideas are eligible for the NIDHI – EIR Ideation Fellowship.

# Benefits
The fellowship provides a monthly stipend of ₹10,000–₹30,000 for up to 12 months, along with mentoring and access to Technology Business Incubators (TBI).

# Funding
The fellowship is funded by the Department of Science & Technology (DST) and provides a grant of ₹10,000–₹30,000 per month for up to 12 months.
