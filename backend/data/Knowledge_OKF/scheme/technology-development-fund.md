# Overview
The Technology Development Fund (TDF) is a programme under the DRDO that supports indigenous defence technology development by industry, especially MSMEs/startups.

# Eligibility
Indian industries, MSMEs, and startups developing technologies for DRDO/Services needs are eligible for the scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides a grant of up to ₹10 crore per project, generally up to 90% of the project cost, along with DRDO mentoring.

# Funding
The scheme is funded by the DRDO, Ministry of Defence, and provides grant-in-aid/project funding.
