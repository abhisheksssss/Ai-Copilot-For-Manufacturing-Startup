# Overview
The NM-ICPS mission, also known as the National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems, aims to create 25 Technology Innovation Hubs for R&D, translational research, and startup incubation in CPS technologies. The mission focuses on deep-tech startups, researchers, and innovators in areas such as AI, IoT, robotics, cyber security, and autonomous systems.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for the NM-ICPS mission are not specified, but it is targeted towards deep-tech startups, researchers, and innovators in CPS technologies.

# Benefits
The NM-ICPS mission provides various benefits, including deep-tech CPS incubation, grants/seed support, testbeds, mentoring, and market connects via Technology Innovation Hubs (TIHs).

# Funding
The mission outlay is ₹3,660 crore, with startup support varying by TIH/call.
