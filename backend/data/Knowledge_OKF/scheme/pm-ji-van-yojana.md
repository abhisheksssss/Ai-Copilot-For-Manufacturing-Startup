# Overview
In 2019, the Government launched Pradhan Mantri JI-VAN (Jaiv Indhan-Vatavaran Anukool fasal awashesh Nivaran) Yojana.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria and specific requirements for participation in this scheme are not provided in the given text.

# Benefits
The scheme aims to:
- Provide financial support to integrated bio-ethanol projects for setting up Second Generation (2G) ethanol projects using ligno-cellulosic biomass and other renewable feedstocks.
- Promote ethanol blending in petrol to enhance energy security and reduce carbon emissions.
- Encourage the use of agricultural residues, preventing stubble burning and associated air pollution.

# Funding
Financial support is provided for setting up 2G ethanol projects. Specific funding details are not mentioned in the given text.
