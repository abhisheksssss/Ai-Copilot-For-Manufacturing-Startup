# Overview
AMRIT (Affordable Medicines and Reliable Implants for Treatment) Pharmacy is a government initiative launched in 2015 under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare to expand affordable access to critical medicines across India.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated, but it primarily benefits low-income and high-burden patients who require life-saving medicines, implants, and medical consumables at discounted rates.

# Benefits
The scheme offers deep discounts on essential medicines, oncology drugs, cardiac implants, and surgical consumables. It also includes a pan-India network of operational outlets, digital upgradation through AMRIT ITes Eco-Green Version 2.0, enhanced services such as mobile pharmacy vans for rural outreach, and a 24×7 national contact centre.

# Funding
Funding details are not provided in the text. The scheme is implemented nationwide by HLL Lifecare Limited, a Central Public Sector Enterprise under MoHFW.
