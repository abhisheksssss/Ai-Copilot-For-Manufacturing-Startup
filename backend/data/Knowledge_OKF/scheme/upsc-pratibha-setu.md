# Overview
UPSC Pratibha Setu is a public recruitment linkage platform launched by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) in 2018, now renamed from PDS to Pratibha Setu in 2024. It aims to connect meritorious yet non-selected aspirants with job opportunities across PSUs, autonomous bodies, and private sectors.

# Eligibility
Eligible examinations covered include the Civil Services Examination, Indian Forest Service Examination, Engineering Services Examination, Indian Economic/Statistical Services, Combined Medical Services Examination, CDS Examination, Central Armed Police Forces (ACs), and Combined Geo-Scientist Examination only.

# Benefits
Key features of Pratibha Setu include:
- Merit Recognition: Highlights UPSC-qualified candidates who missed final recommendation.
- Public Visibility: Candidates voluntarily consent to share details with employers.
- Digital Platform: Accessible via UPSC’s official portal with secure employer login.
- Employment Linkage: Helps bridge labour market gaps by providing job-matching tools.
- Equity in Opportunity: Ensures skilled candidates are not left out of India's talent pool.
