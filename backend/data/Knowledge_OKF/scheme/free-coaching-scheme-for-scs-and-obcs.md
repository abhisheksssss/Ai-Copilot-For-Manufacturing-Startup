# Overview
The Free Coaching Scheme for Scheduled Castes (SCs) and Other Backward Classes (OBCs) is designed to provide quality coaching services to economically disadvantaged candidates from these communities. The scheme aims to enhance their chances of securing appropriate jobs in the public or private sector, as well as admission into reputable technical and professional higher education institutions.

# Eligibility
Eligible candidates must be from Scheduled Castes (SCs) and Other Backward Classes (OBCs). They should also meet the economic criteria set by the government for being considered economically disadvantaged.

# Benefits
The scheme offers quality coaching services to help eligible candidates prepare effectively for competitive and entrance examinations. This includes support in various subjects relevant to their chosen fields of study or career paths, thereby increasing their chances of success in these exams.

# Funding
Funding for this scheme is provided by the government to ensure that economically disadvantaged SCs and OBCs can access quality coaching services without financial burden.
