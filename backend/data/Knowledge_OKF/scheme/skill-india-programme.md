# Overview
The Skill India Programme is a Central Sector Scheme restructured by the Union Cabinet with an overlay outlay of Rs.8,800 crore from 2022-23 to 2025-26. It consolidates three flagship schemes: Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 (PMKVY 4.0), Pradhan Mantri National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (PM-NAPS), and Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Scheme.

# Eligibility
- **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 (PMKVY 4.0):** Age group of 15-59 years.
- **Pradhan Mantri National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (PM-NAPS):** Target Group: 14-35 years.
- **Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Scheme:** Target Group: 15-45 years, especially women, rural youth, and economically weaker sections.

# Benefits
The programme aims to build a skilled future-ready workforce by integrating demand-driven, technology-enabled, and industry-aligned training. It provides structured skill development, on-the-job training, and community-based learning. The scheme ensures access to high-quality vocational education for both urban and rural populations, including marginalized communities.

# Funding
- **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 (PMKVY 4.0):** Provides NSQF-aligned skill training through Short-Term Training (STT), Special Projects (SP), and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL).
- **Pradhan Mantri National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (PM-NAPS):** Focuses on the earn-while-you-learn model, with 25% stipend support via Direct Benefit Transfer.
- **Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Scheme:** Offers low-cost, doorstep training for self-employment and wage-based livelihoods.
