# Overview
The STI Hubs for SC/ST Communities programme is a DST initiative that aims to establish technology-led livelihood and enterprise development in SC/ST communities. The programme is designed to support institutions, NGOs, and community enterprises that serve SC/ST communities.

# Eligibility
Institutions, NGOs, and community enterprises serving SC/ST communities are eligible for the programme.

# Benefits
The programme provides project-based grants, technology deployment, training, and enterprise support to establish STI hubs for technology-led livelihood and enterprise development in SC/ST communities.

# Funding
The funding for the programme is in the form of project-based grants, with the amount varying by sanctioned STI hub.
