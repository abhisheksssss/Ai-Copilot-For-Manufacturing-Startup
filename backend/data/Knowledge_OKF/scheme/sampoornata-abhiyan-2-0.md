# Overview
Sampoornata Abhiyan 2.0 is a mission-mode, time-bound campaign designed to accelerate progress in 112 Aspirational Districts and 513 Aspirational Blocks. It aims to achieve full coverage of essential government services in health, nutrition, and education.

# Eligibility
The scheme targets the residents of 112 Aspirational Districts and 513 Aspirational Blocks across India.

# Benefits
- Full coverage of essential government services.
- Monthly tracking and healthy competition among districts and blocks.
- Ensuring that benefits of schemes like ICDS and TB Mukt Bharat reach remote households.
