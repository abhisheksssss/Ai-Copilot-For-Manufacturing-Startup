# Overview
Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) are government securities denominated in grams of gold and issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India.

# Eligibility
Eligible investors include:
- Individuals
- Hindu Undivided Families (HUF)
- Trusts and similar entities notified by the government

Minimum investment is one gram with a maximum limit of 4 kg for individuals, 4 kg for HUF, and 20 kg for trusts and similar entities.

# Benefits
- Denomination in grams of gold.
- Fixed interest rate of 2.50% per annum on the initial investment.
- Maturity period of 8 years with a lock-in period of 5 years.
- Capital gains tax exemption on redemption to individuals.
- Encourages investment in gold bonds, reducing demand for physical gold and gold imports.

# Funding
The money raised through SGBs is counted as part of the Fiscal Deficit.
