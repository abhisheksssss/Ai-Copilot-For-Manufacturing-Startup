# Overview
Under the National Overseas Scheme for SCs, financial assistance is provided to selected students from Scheduled Castes (SCs), De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes, landless agricultural labourers, and traditional artisan categories for pursuing masters and Ph.D. level courses abroad.

# Eligibility
Students are eligible under this scheme if their total family income, including the candidate, is less than Rs. 8 lakhs per annum.

# Benefits
The scheme provides financial assistance covering:
- Total tuition fee
- Maintenance allowance
- Contingency allowance
- Visa fee
- To and for air passage

# Funding
Funding under this scheme covers all necessary expenses related to pursuing higher studies abroad, including but not limited to tuition fees, maintenance, contingency allowances, visa fees, and travel costs.
