# Overview
The Promoting Innovations in Individuals, Startups & MSMEs (PRISM) scheme is a DSIR initiative that supports individual innovators, startups, and MSMEs in developing prototypes and commercializing innovations, from ideation to prototype.

# Eligibility
Individual innovators, startups, and MSMEs with technology or product innovation ideas are eligible to apply for the PRISM scheme.

# Benefits
The scheme provides grant-in-aid, prototype support, and mentoring to successful applicants, helping them to develop and commercialize their innovations.

# Funding
The scheme offers a grant of up to ₹50 lakh, depending on the PRISM phase or category.
