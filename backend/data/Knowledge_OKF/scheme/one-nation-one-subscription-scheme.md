# Overview
The 'One Nation, One Subscription' Scheme is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) aimed at providing comprehensive healthcare services to all citizens across India.

# Eligibility
Eligibility criteria for this scheme are yet to be officially announced. However, it is expected that all Indian citizens will be eligible based on their unique identification number.

# Benefits
The scheme offers a wide range of benefits including but not limited to:
- Access to primary and secondary healthcare services.
- Telemedicine facilities.
- Emergency medical assistance.
- Health insurance coverage for certain categories.

# Funding
Funding details are yet to be finalized. However, the scheme is expected to be funded through a combination of central government allocation, state contributions, and private sector partnerships.
