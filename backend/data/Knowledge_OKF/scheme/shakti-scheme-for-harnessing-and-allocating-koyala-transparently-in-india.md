# Overview
The SHAKTI scheme aims to make coal available to all thermal power plants in a transparent and objective manner. It ensures that the benefits of linkage coal are transferred to end consumers, benefiting power companies, consumers, indigenous coal sectors, and banking sectors.

# Eligibility
Private-owned independent power plants (IPPs) with or without power purchase agreements (PPAs) can bid for coal linkages based on location proximity and economic gains. State-owned power distribution companies (Discoms) will assign these linkages to thermal power plants.

# Benefits
- Ensures transparent allocation of coal.
- Reduces cost of power for consumers.
- Decreases reliance on imported coal.
- Lowers non-performing assets in the banking sector.

# Funding
The scheme is funded through Coal India Limited (CIL) and does not specify any direct government funding. The allocation process is managed through coal auctions.
