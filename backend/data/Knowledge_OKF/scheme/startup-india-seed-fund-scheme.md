# Overview
The Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) provides seed funding to early-stage startups with technology-enabled business ideas. The scheme disburses funds through approved incubators for proof-of-concept, prototype, market entry, and commercialization.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for DPIIT-recognised early-stage startups with technology-enabled business ideas.

# Benefits
The scheme provides funding of up to ₹20 lakh as a grant and up to ₹50 lakh as convertible debt.

# Funding
The funding is provided through a combination of grant and debt/convertible debt, disbursed through approved incubators.
