# Overview
The STPI – Next Generation Incubation Scheme (NGIS) is a scheme supporting software product startups in selected Tier-II/III locations through incubation and seed support.

# Eligibility
The scheme is eligible for tech/software product startups in identified NGIS cities.

# Benefits
The benefits of the scheme include incubation, mentoring, cloud credits, seed support, and market access.

# Funding
The scheme outlay is ₹95.03 crore, with support varying per startup/city.
