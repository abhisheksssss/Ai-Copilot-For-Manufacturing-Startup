# Overview
The Standardised Tariff Rate (SOR) Template is a standardized tariff structure for major ports introduced by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (MoWCD). This initiative aims to reduce ambiguity, ensure transparency, and provide a digital comparison tool for tariffs.
