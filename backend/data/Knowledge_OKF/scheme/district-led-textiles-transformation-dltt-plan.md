# Overview

A sector-specific, district-level transformation strategy that uses data-driven categorisation to strengthen India’s textile ecosystem—ranging from advanced export clusters to foundation-stage districts—through tailored interventions.

# Eligibility

Eligibility criteria are not explicitly stated in the provided text. However, it is implied that all districts within the scope of the Ministry of Textiles can participate based on their categorisation as Champion or Aspirational Districts.

# Benefits

- Inclusive, sustainable, and export-oriented growth in textiles.
- Decentralised policy execution to districts.
- Strengthening Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).
- Formalising the workforce.
- Creation of globally competitive clusters.
- Data-driven district scoring based on Export Performance, MSME ecosystem, and Workforce presence.
- Two-pronged district strategy: Champion Districts focus on Scale & Sophistication while Aspirational Districts focus on Foundation & Formalisation.

Funding details are not provided in the given text. However, it is implied that the scheme involves convergence of government schemes with industry and academia to scale proven cluster models.
