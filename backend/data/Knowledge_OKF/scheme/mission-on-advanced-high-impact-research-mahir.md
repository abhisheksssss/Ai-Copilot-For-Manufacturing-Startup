# Overview
The Mission on Advanced & High Impact Research (MAHIR) is a joint mission between the Ministry of Power and the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) to identify, develop, and demonstrate emerging power-sector technologies indigenously.

# Eligibility
Startups, industry, academia, and research institutions working on power-sector technologies are eligible for the mission.

# Benefits
The mission provides project-based funding, pilot support, and commercialization facilitation for power sector deep-tech R&D projects.

# Funding
The mission is funded through pooled resources from the Ministry of Power, MNRE, and Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSE). The funding is provided in the form of grants.
