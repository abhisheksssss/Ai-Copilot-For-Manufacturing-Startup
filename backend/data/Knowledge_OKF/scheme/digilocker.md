# Overview
A flagship initiative under Digital India providing citizens with a secure cloud-based platform to access and share authentic digital documents.

# Eligibility
Eligible for all Indian citizens.

# Benefits
- Secure storage of legally valid digital documents.
- Easy access to documents from anywhere.
- Facilitates paperless governance and faster service delivery.

# Funding
Not applicable as it is a government initiative.
